# IRC §1060 and Form 8594 — residual method

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §1060; Treas. Reg. §1.1060-1; Form 8594 Instructions  
*Status in this library:* in force

In an applicable asset acquisition, consideration is allocated under the residual method among seven classes in order: I cash; II actively traded personal property; III mark-to-market assets and receivables; IV inventory; V all other assets (equipment, real property); VI section 197 intangibles other than goodwill (including covenants not to compete); VII goodwill and going concern value. Each class is allocated up to fair market value before moving to the next. **Buyer and seller must each file Form 8594 and report consistently** with any written allocation agreement.
