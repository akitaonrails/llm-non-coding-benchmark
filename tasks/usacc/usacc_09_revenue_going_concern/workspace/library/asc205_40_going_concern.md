# ASC 205-40 — going concern

*Citation:* FASB ASC 205-40-50-1 to 50-14  
*Status in this library:* in force

Management evaluates whether conditions raise **substantial doubt** about the entity's ability to continue as a going concern within **one year after the financial statements are issued**. If so, management considers whether its plans **alleviate** the doubt; a plan is considered only if it is **probable** that it will be effectively implemented **and** probable that it will mitigate the conditions. A non-binding term sheet with conditions precedent is not a probable plan. If doubt is alleviated by plans, disclose the conditions and the plans; if not, disclose that substantial doubt exists.
