# WARN Act thresholds

*Citation:* 29 U.S.C. §2101(a)(1); 20 C.F.R. §639.3  
*Status in this library:* in force

WARN applies to employers with **100 or more employees** (excluding part-time) and to plant closings or mass layoffs affecting 50+ employees at a single site. A 14-employee business is **not** a covered employer; no WARN notice is required even if the buyer does not hire everyone. (Texas has no mini-WARN statute.)
