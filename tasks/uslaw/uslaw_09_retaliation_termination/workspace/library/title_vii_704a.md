# Title VII §704(a) — retaliation; temporal proximity

*Citation:* 42 U.S.C. §2000e-3(a); *Burlington Northern v. White*, 548 U.S. 53 (2006); *Clark County Sch. Dist. v. Breeden*, 532 U.S. 268 (2001)  
*Status in this library:* in force

It is unlawful to discriminate against an employee because she has opposed a practice made unlawful by Title VII or participated in a proceeding. An internal complaint of sexual harassment is protected opposition. Causation may be shown by **very close temporal proximity** (days to a few weeks) between the protected activity and the adverse action, by **pretext** (shifting or thin justifications, disparate treatment of comparators who did not complain), and by **direct evidence** (statements by decision-makers linking the action to the protected activity).
