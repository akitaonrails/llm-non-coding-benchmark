# Nedlloyd Lines B.V. v. Superior Court

*Citation:* 3 Cal.4th 459 (1992)  
*Status in this library:* in force

California follows Restatement §187: a contractual choice of law is enforced if the chosen state has a substantial relationship to the parties, unless the chosen law is contrary to a **fundamental policy** of California and California has a **materially greater interest** in the determination of the issue.
