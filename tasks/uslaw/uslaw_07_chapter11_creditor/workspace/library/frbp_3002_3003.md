# Fed. R. Bankr. P. 3002, 3003 — proofs of claim; bar date

*Citation:* Fed. R. Bankr. P. 3003(c)(2)-(3); 3002(c)  
*Status in this library:* in force

In a chapter 11 case, a creditor whose claim is not scheduled or is scheduled as disputed, contingent or unliquidated **must file a proof of claim** by the bar date fixed by the court; a creditor who fails to do so shall not be treated as a creditor for purposes of voting and distribution. Late claims are allowed only on a showing of excusable neglect (*Pioneer*). A §503(b)(9) request is typically made by motion or by the procedure the bar-date order specifies, and should be asserted separately from the general unsecured claim.
