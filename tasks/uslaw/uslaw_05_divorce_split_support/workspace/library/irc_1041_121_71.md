# IRC §§1041, 121(d)(3), 71/215 (post-TCJA) — divorce tax rules

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §1041; §121(b)(1), (d)(3); TCJA §11051 (P.L. 115-97)  
*Status in this library:* in force

**§1041:** no gain or loss on transfers between spouses or former spouses incident to divorce; the transferee takes the transferor's basis (carryover). **§121:** exclusion of gain on sale of a principal residence: $250,000 (single) / $500,000 (joint return) if owned and used as principal residence 2 of the last 5 years. **§121(d)(3)(A):** an individual is treated as using property as a principal residence during any period of ownership while the individual's spouse or former spouse is granted use under a divorce instrument. **(B):** property transferred to an individual incident to divorce includes the transferor's ownership period. **Alimony (TCJA):** for divorce or separation instruments executed after December 31, 2018, alimony is **neither deductible by the payor nor includible by the recipient**. Child support is never deductible or includible.
