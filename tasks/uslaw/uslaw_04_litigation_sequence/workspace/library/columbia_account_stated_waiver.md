# Account stated; waiver; course of dealing

*Citation:* Columbia pattern instructions CJI 14.02, 16.10; UCC §1-303 by analogy  
*Status in this library:* in force

Account stated: an invoice retained without objection within a reasonable (or contractually fixed) period is prima facie evidence of the debt. **Waiver** is the intentional relinquishment of a known right; a written waiver limited by its terms ('for Rosa and Tay as a one-off') does not extend to later hires, but **a course of dealing** (acquiescence in later hires without objection — 'ha, we'll talk', 'call me') may support an argument of implied waiver or estoppel as to those hires; a fact question.
