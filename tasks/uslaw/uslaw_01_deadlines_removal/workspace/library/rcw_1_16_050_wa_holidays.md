# RCW 1.16.050 — Washington legal holidays (2026 dates)

*Citation:* RCW 1.16.050  
*Status in this library:* in force

State legal holidays: New Year's Day (Jan 1); Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday Jan — Jan 19, 2026); Presidents' Day (third Monday Feb — Feb 16); Memorial Day (last Monday May — May 25); Juneteenth (June 19); Independence Day (July 4 — observed Fri July 3, 2026); Labor Day (first Monday Sept — Sept 7); Veterans Day (Nov 11); Thanksgiving (Nov 26); Native American Heritage Day (Nov 27); Christmas (Dec 25). **Washington does not observe Columbus Day as a state legal holiday**; state courts are open on October 12, 2026.
