# Buyer A: proposed Form 8594 allocation

This is a negotiating proposal, **not an appraisal**. It assumes $3,100,000 is the entire consideration for tax allocation, including any assumed trade payables; that inventory's $150,000 figure is fair market value; and that a separately paid covenant/consulting component has no supportable value. Recalculate if any of those assumptions fails. The residual method allocates consideration through the asset classes in order, up to fair market value, and both parties must file consistently with their written agreement ([§1060 and Form 8594](../library/irc_1060_form_8594.md)).

| Class | Proposed allocation | Basis used for seller illustration | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| I, cash | $0 | $0 | No cash is listed among assets sold. Confirm at closing. |
| II, traded property | $0 | $0 | None identified. |
| III, receivables | $200,000 | $200,000 **assumed** | Equal to the listed face amount. Confirm tax basis and collectibility. |
| IV, inventory | $150,000 | $140,000 **assumed** | Retain Buyer's proposed value pending a physical count and valuation; $140,000 is the recorded carrying amount, not proof of fair market value. |
| V, equipment and vehicles | $650,000 | $240,000 **assumed current** | Equals the sum of the fixed-asset register's six fair market value estimates; Buyer's $900,000 exceeds that estimate by $250,000. Obtain an independent appraisal before binding allocation. |
| VI, covenant and other §197 intangibles except goodwill | $0 **conditional** | $0 | Ray says he is retiring and is willing to sign for five years. Propose a covenant ancillary to the goodwill sale with no separately bargained payment. No consulting services or fee have been specified. Any real consulting arrangement should identify services, duration, and fair compensation; an independently valued covenant must receive its supportable amount. |
| VII, goodwill and going concern | $2,100,000 | $0 **assumed** | Residual: $3,100,000 − $200,000 − $150,000 − $650,000 = $2,100,000. The 2023 appraisal supports at least $900,000 of preconversion goodwill value, but does not itself establish current $2,100,000 fair market value. Obtain a current valuation. |
| **Total** | **$3,100,000** | **$580,000 assumed** | $200,000 + $140,000 + $240,000 = $580,000. |

The covenant allocation must reflect economic reality. A five-year covenant could have a positive value even if Ray intends to retire, and a written allocation can bind the parties; do not present $0 as an established fact ([§197 and noncompete](../library/irc_197_and_noncompete.md); [§1060 and Form 8594](../library/irc_1060_form_8594.md)). If inventory's fair market value is $140,000 rather than $150,000, $10,000 shifts from Class IV to Class VII; ordinary income falls $10,000 and capital gain rises $10,000 under the stated basis assumptions. If assumed payables increase tax consideration beyond $3,100,000, allocate that extra amount under the residual method after confirming their treatment.
