# Judgment calls and alternatives

1. **S corporation loss and basis.** I used $11,500 of stock basis at the beginning of 2025 and no debt basis. The 2025 $28,000 loss is allowed only to $11,500, leaving $16,500 suspended. I rejected Marcus's suggested full current deduction: prior distributions and losses reduce basis, and a bank line guaranteed by Pike is not Marcus's direct loan. The K-1 states that Marcus materially participates, so I treated the allowed loss as nonpassive for the rental MAGI calculation. [IRS Form 7203 Instructions 2025](../library/form7203_instructions_excerpt.md); [IRS Form 8582 Instructions 2025](../library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md).

2. **Below-market Brooklyn rent.** The primary calculation assumes $1,500 monthly is fair rent under the existing lease with an unrelated tenant. I rejected automatic personal-use treatment solely because current market rent is about $2,600: the supplied rule says an unchanged below-market rent to an unrelated tenant does not by itself trigger that result if it is fair for the lease. The client cannot provide the 2025 lease terms or confirm personal-use days. If rent is not fair for that lease, the library says the rental loss is not allowed. This could remove the $19,550 deduction and change AGI, income tax, premium credit, and refund. [IRS Form 8582 Instructions 2025](../library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md).

3. **Dependents.** I claimed Sofia, age 15, and used one $2,200 child credit. I did not claim Leo: the custody order gives his father odd-numbered years, including 2025. The alternative of claiming both children would add an unsupported dependent and child credit. [IRS 2025 Form 1040 Instructions](../library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md).

4. **Marketplace family and shared policy.** I used all six months of the 1095-A and the only FPL amount in the library, $31,200 for four people. I rejected extrapolating a three-person FPL or an allocation percentage from outside the library. Leo is on the policy but is claimed by his father, so the correct policy allocation and family size cannot be established from the supplied excerpt. The Form 8962 amounts and federal refund are provisional. Elena's employer coverage began July 1, so no later marketplace months were assumed. [IRS Form 8962 Instructions 2025](../library/form8962_instructions_excerpt.md).

5. **New York resident days.** I used January 1–June 30, or 181 days, as the provisional resident period because the Brooklyn apartment lease ended June 30 and the family was out by then. I rejected treating July 3 as an established change date: closing on and moving into Tampa took place around that date, but the client cannot establish the exact date or location for July 1–3. New York resident days could therefore change. [NYS IT-203-I (2025)](../library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md).

6. **New York wages and remote work.** I treated both employers' post-move Florida work as work for the employees' convenience, making all $68,000 of Marcus's and $54,000 of Elena's wages New York source. This is provisional: the employers' business reasons and actual workdays are unavailable. I rejected automatically sourcing post-move wages to Florida from physical presence alone because the supplied instructions expressly apply the convenience rule to New York employers. If either employer required Florida work for its business, that spouse's nonresident out-of-state workday wages would be excluded from New York source income, but the number cannot be computed without workday records. [NYS IT-203-I (2025)](../library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md).

7. **Deduction and missing income.** I used the $31,500 married-filing-jointly standard deduction; the known $6,480 Tampa mortgage interest is smaller, and the file establishes no itemized total above $31,500. I assumed no other income, deductions, credits, or modified-AGI additions beyond the supplied documents. These are file-completeness assumptions, not independently verified facts. [IRS 2025 Form 1040 Instructions](../library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md); [IRS Form 8962 Instructions 2025](../library/form8962_instructions_excerpt.md).
