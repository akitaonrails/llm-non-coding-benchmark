## 4. Verdict, stated plainly: **improved — on liability and leverage; not yet in cash**

(2) Their aggression arrived pre-empted: the 12(b)(6) draft targets a tort count we abandoned months ago on our own documents (*Ashby*, Restatement §§766–767/773, §6 consequential bar, consent — advice §§ 5.3, 6), and their motion-to-compel trap cannot fire against a claim we file **in** the contractual forum (9 U.S.C. §§3–4 and the fee-shifting note in *faa_2_4.md*; *Zuniga v. Mercer*, 198 Col. 2d 77 (2017), on C.3). (3) Exhibit C flipped from landmine to weapon: C.3 makes us the fee-collector if we prevail; C.4 is the injunction door against any eighth hire. (4) Our credibility is now an asset with receipts: we credited the $3,100 before they found it, conceded Rosa/Tay, and corrected the pre-engagement letter's "never consented" line ourselves. That is why $428,399.50 reads as a serious invoice, not a bluster letter.

**What is worse, and I will not round it off.** (1) **The cash has not moved**: zero collected, and their stated policy is to keep $276,699.50 (plus interest) hostage until the conversion-fee "issue resolves" — a strategy that, unchallenged, just holds admitted debt longer. (2) The **pre-engagement letter** — sent before our engagement, containing a statement our own Feb. 11 email contradicts and a Superior Court threat that would have converted C.3 into a gun aimed at Juniper — is in their file, and we still don't know who signed it. Our Nov. 2 ¶1 neutralizes it prospectively; it cannot be unsent. (4) We **fund the forum**: AAA filing/administrative fees and arbitrator compensation are real advances **[not in library — unverified — schedule pulled at filing]**, and C.3 is mutual, so an arbitrator's wash result can leave Juniper paying both sides. (5) The CEO's "I want them scared" instinct keeps trying to re-arm a claim Rule 3.1 forbids (Columbia R. **Net:** the *claim* is stronger than on Oct. 26 (admission + disarmed weapons + forum leverage); the *probability-weighted money* is up only by the interest, $2,652.00/month; the *timeline to cash* is unchanged and is now entirely the arbitrator's (or Hanna's) decision, not ours.
