## B. Term sheet (draft for transmission)

Check: 265,200.00 + 11,499.50 + 71,700.00 + 80,000.00 = 428,399.50 ✓.

**2. Interest continues.** §2 interest (1%/month simple) continues to accrue on unpaid balances until actually paid, including during any settlement period — arithmetic on request; run-rate from Nov. 26–29 is +$2,652.00/month on the core alone (684.00 + 712.50 + 661.00 + 594.50 = 2,652.00 ✓).

**3. Core payment — unconditional.** $276,699.50 (¶1's 265,200.00 + 11,499.50, plus §2 interest to the payment date) is payable **without condition**, §2 being an unconditional 30-day payment term. Cash within 10 business days of signing. If Hanna requires installments: maximum four monthly installments, §2 interest continues on the unpaid balance, first installment ≥ 50% — a 0%-interest six-month schedule is not acceptable because it pays *less* than the admitted balance.

**4. Conversion fees — true-up, both directions.** For Priya S., Luis F., Keisha B., Anne O., Victor H. only: $80,000.00 is the placeholder (25% × $64,000.00 × 5); the parties exchange payroll documentation of each hire's first-year annualized compensation within 15 days and the number adjusts to **25% × actuals per hire** — up or down, as our Nov. 2 letter committed. Rosa M. and Tay W. are **excluded** by Bell's Feb. 11, 2026 written waiver ("as a one-off"), and this exclusion is stated on the face of the settlement to foreclose any later argument that it reaches beyond those two names (§3 requires waiver in writing; *columbia_account_stated_waiver.md*: a written waiver limited by its terms does not extend past them).

**5. J-4533 — substantiate-or-pay, made contractual.** Pinecrest delivers itemized audit findings by **Dec. 1, 2026**. Documented double-entries or errors are credited dollar-for-dollar (precedent: Juniper credited $3,100.00 from its own review before Pinecrest found it). The balance of $71,700.00 (as credited) is payable with the next core installment. Unresolved line items go to an expedited, documents-only single-arbitrator determination under Exhibit C.1 — both parties pre-consent here; mechanics beyond C.1 are **[not in library — unverified]**.

**6. Fees.** C.3 governs any issue that proceeds to an arbitrator: prevailing party recovers reasonable fees and costs. If all ¶¶1–5 resolve by performance with no award, each side bears its own fees, except that a default under ¶8 carries C.3 fees to Juniper automatically on the enforcement award. (What "reasonable" allocation an arbitrator makes on mixed outcomes is **[not in library — unverified]**.)

**7. Releases — on money, not on paper.** Mutual general releases (no liability admissions) become **effective only upon actual receipt of the final payment**, not at signing. Carve-outs, expressly: (a) enforcement of this term sheet; (b) §3's direct-hire restriction for the remainder of its term-plus-12-months run; (c) Exhibit C.1–C.5 survival. A signing-date release with installments outstanding (their Oct. 20 shape) is declined: it trades Juniper's only leverage for a promise.

**8. Default.** Any installment or true-up payment missed by more than 5 business days: full remaining acknowledged balance accelerates, §2 interest continues, and Juniper may file a consented, expedited AAA proceeding under C.1 for the accelerated balance, in which C.3 fees are Juniper's as prevailing party. (Alternatives such as confesses-of-judgment or escrow **[not in library — unverified]**; acceleration + consented expedited arbitration uses only machinery this contract already contains.)

**9. Forward §3 compliance.** Pinecrest confirms no further direct hires from Juniper's introduced roster while §3's restriction runs; any breach is excluded from the releases (¶7(b)) and Juniper preserves its C.4 remedy — a court petition in aid of arbitration — with both parties consenting to proceed on an expedited basis.

**10. Content guardrails (non-negotiable, ours).** No settlement paper will assert tortious interference, claim fees for Rosa or Tay, or repeat that Juniper "never consented to any direct hire." Those positions are abandoned on our own documents and Rule 3.1 forbids re-asserting them (Columbia R. Prof'l Conduct 1.4, 3.1, 3.3; *fee_shifting_and_candor.md*). Confidentiality and mutual non-disparagement are acceptable; anything resembling an admission by Juniper of the pre-engagement letter's contents is not.

**11. Conditions.** (a) Signatures: D. Okonkwo for Juniper [and board consent if required — Q14]; officer signature for Pinecrest. (b) If executed before **Nov. 17, 2026**, Juniper does not file; if unsigned on Nov. 17, the AAA demand (invoices + interest + J-4533 + conversion fees + **C.3 fees pleaded**) is filed that day, and this term sheet is withdrawn.
