**2. Invoice J-4533 (June 2026) — adjusted balance of $71,700.00.**
Pinecrest notified Juniper on July 9, 2026 that it was "auditing" June shifts and "holding payment." Juniper has now completed its own line-level review of June. If Pinecrest's audit has produced any further specific discrepancies, its findings — shift by shift, with the basis for each — must be delivered **by November 9, 2026**. Absent specific substantiation by that date, Juniper will treat the audit objection as resolved and pursue the adjusted balance, with interest, in the forum specified below.

**3. Conversion fees — $80,000.00 (estimated).**
Section 3 prohibits Pinecrest, during the term and for 12 months thereafter, from directly or indirectly hiring personnel introduced by Juniper "except upon payment of a conversion fee equal to 25% of the Personnel's first-year annualized compensation." Between March and June 2026, Pinecrest hired from Juniper's roster: Priya S. (RN), Luis F. (RN), Keisha B. (LPN), Anne O. (RN), and Victor H. (CNA). No conversion fee has been paid for any of them. At Juniper's present estimate of first-year annualized compensation (a $64,000 average, used as a placeholder), the fees due are **$80,000.00** (5 × 25% × $64,000). **Pinecrest is directed to provide, by November 9, 2026, the actual first-year compensation offered to each of the five individuals**, so the contractual formula can be applied to actual figures; the amount demanded will be adjusted accordingly, up or down.

In fairness, and consistent with the record: Juniper **does not** assert conversion fees for Rosa M. or Tay W. in light of Marcus Bell's e-mail of February 11, 2026, which waived those two fees "as a one-off." By its own terms that waiver extends to no other individual, and Juniper has never asserted it otherwise.

**4. Future compliance.**
The Section 3 restriction continues during the term of the Agreement and for 12 months after any termination. Juniper expects Pinecrest to honor it, and will seek injunctive relief in aid of arbitration (Exhibit C.4) for further violations.

**5. Demand and forum.**
Juniper demands payment of **$425,747.50** — comprising $265,200.00 in deemed-accepted invoices, $8,847.50 in accrued interest through October 25, 2026, $71,700.00 on adjusted J-4533 (subject to Paragraph 2), and $80,000.00 in estimated conversion fees (subject to true-up under Paragraph 3) — on or before **November 9, 2026**.

If payment or a binding payment schedule is not received by that date, Juniper will file a demand for **binding arbitration before the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, before a single arbitrator seated in Capital City, State of Columbia**, as Exhibit C.1 of the Agreement requires.
