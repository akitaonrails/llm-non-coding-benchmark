# Interpretation — 'notwithstanding' clauses and specific vs. general

*Citation:* Restatement (Second) of Contracts §203; Delaware cases on 'notwithstanding anything to the contrary'  
*Status in this library:* in force

Where a clause begins 'notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement,' courts give it **controlling effect over conflicting provisions**, including more specific ones, because the parties expressly resolved the conflict. A statement elsewhere that an obligation is 'uncapped' does not override a cap that is expressly made to apply 'notwithstanding anything to the contrary' unless the cap clause itself carves the obligation out. Delaware courts enforce contracts as written between sophisticated parties.
