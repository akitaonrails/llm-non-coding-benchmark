# RouteWise MSA — what the COO needs to know (one page)

**The deal.** RouteWise's routing/fleet-optimization platform, up to 350 vehicles, for 36 months at
**$18,500/month billed annually in advance ($222,000/yr)** plus a **$25,000** implementation fee —
about **$247,000 in year one, $691,000 over the term** ($222,000 × 3 + $25,000). After the first
36 months it **auto-renews for 2-year blocks** unless we say no 90 days early. It's the vendor's
form; almost every legal default favors RouteWise.

**Don't sign this week as drafted.** Five things must change first — none are exotic; all are
one- or two-sentence edits RouteWise's lawyers can turn around quickly:

1. **The EU data addendum is a blank page.** Exhibit C (the GDPR data-processing addendum) says
   "to be attached." Client has confirmed the platform will carry EU driver names, locations,
   working hours and customer addresses from Rotterdam/Hamburg, so when the platform runs without a
   signed addendum, **Cobalt is in breach of GDPR Art. 28 from day one** — that liability lands on
   us as the data controller, not on RouteWise. Fix: sign the DPA **with** the MSA, before EU data
   goes live.
2. **The "uncapped" IP guarantee is fake.** §10.1 says RouteWise's protection of us against
   software-infringement lawsuits is "uncapped" — but §9.1 caps *everything* at one year of fees
   (~**$222,000**) "notwithstanding anything to the contrary," and it doesn't carve the indemnity
   out. Under Delaware law the cap wins. If someone sues us over RouteWise's routing engine, our
   real safety net is $222k. Fix: carve the indemnity out of the cap.
3. **No price lock.** The order form lets RouteWise raise fees **once mid-term (up to the greater
   of 8% or CPI)** and raise renewal fees without limit. 8% = +$1,480/month (+$17,760/yr; up to
   ~$50,320 extra over the term if they trigger it early). Fix: fixed price for 36 months; renewal
   increases capped at the *lower* of 5%/CPI on 90 days' notice.
4. **The renewal trap.** To leave at month 36 we must notify by month 33 — and the clock starts on
   a **service-start date RouteWise sets**. Miss it and we're committed to 24 more months:
   ~**$444,000**, or ~$479,520 after a price increase. Fix: 12-month renewals, 30 days' notice —
   and until that's fixed, put the month-33 date in the calendar now.
5. **Our affiliates aren't covered.** The license covers Cobalt Holdings only. Client has confirmed
   both affiliates will use it — Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. (Rotterdam/Hamburg) and Cobalt Brokerage
   LLC (carrier routing) — so as drafted that is unlicensed use from day one, billable at extra
   fees. Fix: name both affiliates in the license at no charge (and fix the two definitions that
   say "Customer's" employees/drivers/customers, or the fix leaks).

**Also ask (not a deal-breaker):** if the platform keeps failing, credits (max **$4,625/month**,
reached at 97% uptime; 8 hrs/month of "maintenance" doesn't even count as downtime) are our *only*
remedy, and we forfeit them unless claimed in 30 days. Add: three bad months in any six → we can
walk with a refund. And suspension for "non-payment" after 15 days should exclude amounts we
dispute in good faith — with annual prepay, that's leverage we don't want them to have.

**Already fine — don't spend chips here:** Delaware law, Wilmington venue, jury waiver; mutual
liability cap at 12 months' fees *with* gross-negligence/willful-misconduct/fraud carve-outs (that
cutting both ways protects us too); 30-day data-export window; 99.5% availability target with
credits. Late-payment interest is 1.5%/month ≈ **19.6%/yr** compounded ($222,000 one month late =
$3,330) — commercial, worth a shrug or a ask, not a hill to die on.

**Bottom line.** The economics are acceptable once the price-protection edits land; the DPA and the
indemnity-cap fix are non-negotiable before EU data flows or signature. Realistic path: send the
redline this week, sign when RouteWise returns the five edits.
