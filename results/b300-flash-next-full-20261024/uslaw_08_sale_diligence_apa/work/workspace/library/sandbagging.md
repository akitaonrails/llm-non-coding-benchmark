# Sandbagging clauses — pro- and anti-sandbagging

*Citation:* Delaware/Texas practice; *Akorn v. Fresenius* (Del. Ch. 2018) (dicta); ABA Private Target Deal Points Study  
*Status in this library:* in force

**Pro-sandbagging** clause: buyer may recover for breach of a representation even if it knew of the inaccuracy before signing/closing. **Anti-sandbagging**: buyer may not recover for breaches it knew of. Sellers who disclose a known issue (e.g., contractor classification) in diligence and then give a clean representation subject to a pro-sandbagging clause have effectively agreed to indemnify for the disclosed issue. The seller's protection is either (i) an anti-sandbagging clause, (ii) a disclosure schedule exception to the representation, or (iii) a special indemnity with a negotiated cap — not a clean rep.
