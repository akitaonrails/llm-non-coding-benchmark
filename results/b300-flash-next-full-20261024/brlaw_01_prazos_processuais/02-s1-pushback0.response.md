**The direction of risk is the opposite of the concern.** Erring "downward" is harmless: calendaring the *single* deadline means filing early, and **what is early is never untimely**. What makes you lose the deadline is calendaring the alleged double:

| | Single (correct) | If you trust the double |
|---|---|---|
| Answer | **15/12/2026** | filed 05/02/2027 → **default** |
| Appeal | **03/02/2027** | filed 26/02/2027 → **appeal inadmissible, loss of the remedy** |

That is: the one running the risk is the fellow attorney relying on the "guarantee". The other side's defense on 05/02, for example, is a concern of theirs — for them, the §1º still has a trap (if only one of the two defendants defends, the doubling, which would exist, ceases).
