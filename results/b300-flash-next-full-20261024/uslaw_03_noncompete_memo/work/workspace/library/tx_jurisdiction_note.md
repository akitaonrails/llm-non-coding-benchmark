# Personal jurisdiction over the employee in Texas

*Citation:* Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §17.042; *Michiana Easy Livin' Country v. Holten*, 168 S.W.3d 777 (Tex. 2005)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A forum-selection clause consenting to Travis County generally supplies consent to personal jurisdiction; but if the clause is voided under Cal. Lab. Code §925 at the employee's election, the Texas court must find independent minimum contacts. Two short business trips to Austin and a contract with a Texas employer may or may not suffice; an injunction entered in Texas would in any event face non-recognition in California on public-policy grounds for the non-compete.
