# Professional fees — what may be paid, to whom, and when

Authorities: 11 U.S.C. §§ 330(a), 331 (library/bc_330_331.md) — professionals are paid **only upon application and court award**, or under **interim compensation procedures ordered by the court**; payments outside those procedures are **subject to disgorgement**. Use of DIP proceeds for professional fees is permitted only for "administrative expenses allowed by the Court" (DIP Order Dkt. 61 ¶ 2, documents/dip_financing_order_excerpt.pdf). Reported on MOR Part 5 (library/ust_mor_instructions.md).

## Holland & Pierce LLP (debtors' counsel) — $140,000.00 invoiced 3/31/2026

- **Nothing was payable in March.** No award existed on 3/31; reported in March MOR Part 5 as holdback $140,000.00, approved $0, paid $0.
- **Interim compensation order entered 4/14/2026** (80% interim / 100% holdback — documents/vendor_invoices_march.csv note). This is a § 331 interim procedure. Mechanically:
  - **When:** only from and after 4/14/2026, and only upon H&P's interim fee application and the order's payment mechanics — not before, not without the application.
  - **How much:** up to **80%** of the interim-awarded fees. On the 3/31 invoice as a proxy for the first interim award: $140,000.00 × 0.80 = **$112,000.00** maximum interim payment (actual award may be less or cover a different period — the filed application governs).
  - **100% holdback:** the full awarded amount stays held back as security for the final award, so the remaining **$28,000.00** (20%) — and any interim payments' worth again — can be disgorged/recovered pending final award. The final balance is payable **only after the final § 330 order**.
- **Payable now in April (subject to application + order):** max $112,000.00. **Not payable:** the balance until final award.
