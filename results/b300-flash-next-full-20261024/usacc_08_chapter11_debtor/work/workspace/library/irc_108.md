# IRC §108 — income from discharge of indebtedness; reduction of tax attributes

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §108(a)(1)(A), (b)(1)-(5)  
*Status in this library:* in force

(a)(1)(A) Gross income does not include any amount which would be includible by reason of the discharge of indebtedness of the taxpayer if the discharge occurs in a title 11 case. (b)(1) The amount excluded **shall be applied to reduce the tax attributes** of the taxpayer in this order: (A) net operating losses (the NOL for the year of discharge and carryovers), (B) general business credits, (C) minimum tax credit, (D) capital loss carryovers, (E) basis of property, (F) passive activity loss carryovers, (G) foreign tax credits. Reductions are made after the tax for the year of discharge is determined. (b)(5) The taxpayer may elect to apply any portion of the reduction first to the basis of depreciable property.
