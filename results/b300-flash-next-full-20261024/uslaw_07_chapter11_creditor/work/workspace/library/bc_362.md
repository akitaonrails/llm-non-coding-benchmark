# 11 U.S.C. §362(a), (k) — automatic stay

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §362(a)(1), (a)(6), (k)  
*Status in this library:* in force

(a) A petition operates as a stay, applicable to all entities, of— (1) the commencement or continuation of an action or proceeding against the debtor to recover a claim that arose before the commencement of the case; … **(6) any act to collect, assess, or recover a claim against the debtor that arose before the commencement of the case**. **(k)** An individual injured by any willful violation of a stay shall recover actual damages, including costs and attorneys' fees, and, in appropriate circumstances, punitive damages. Courts also sanction corporate debtors' creditors for willful violations under §105. Dunning letters, collection calls and threats to report to credit bureaus after the petition are acts to collect.
