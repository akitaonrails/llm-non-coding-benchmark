## 1. Why I am writing

The CEO has directed me, in writing, four times (2025-12-30 email; follow-up replies; "Book it"; and a final message coupling the request to nine employees' jobs), to (i) report Q4 2025 revenue of at least $1,500,000 — which requires booking $690,000 I have concluded is unsupported — and (ii) omit the going-concern disclosure. This memorandum is that escalation and that documentation.

## 3. Going concern — the disclosure is not optional (ASC 205-40-50-1 to 50-14)

The company's own 13-week forecast turns cash **negative w/e 2026-02-20** (−$105,000; −$509,000 by 3/27) — average net burn ≈ $86,200/week. The only plan is the Crestline $3.0M note: **non-binding, with conditions precedent**; the standard states a non-binding term sheet with conditions precedent is **not a probable plan**, so substantial doubt is not alleviated and must be disclosed. Note further that the standard provides **no outcome without disclosure**: if plans later alleviate the doubt, the conditions *and plans* are still disclosed. The Crestline note itself funds ≈ 35 weeks of burn (≈ 42 weeks counting the existing $612,000 of cash — to roughly Oct 2026), short of the one-year-after-issuance horizon, so some form of disclosure survives even a January closing.

## 4. The risk of the requested course

Booking $690,000 (44.8% of drafted revenue, more than covering the entire $650,000 gap to Crestline's condition precedent) would (a) be a material misstatement in financial statements used to induce a $3,000,000 investment; (b) require Pell & Marsh to sign a review contrary to their own stated bill-and-hold criteria; (c) contradict the contract documents Crestline's diligence will read (the LOI's no-legal-obligation clause; the refund side letter); and (d) convert a fixable financing gap into potential default, clawback, and personal exposure — including the CEO's — while jeopardizing the very payroll (nine employees) offered as justification. The corrected facts are defensible: both customer relationships appear real, and the revenue is plausibly recognizable in Q1 2026 (MSA/approvals; February delivery, refund-constrained).

## 5. What the Controller will and will not do

**Will:** issue Q4 statements with revenue $850,000, embedded-lease recognition (measurement inputs flagged as unobtainable assumptions in `STATUS.md`), and going-concern disclosure; prepare an accurate financing package for Crestline (renegotiated condition precedent, milestone tranches, disclosure of conditions and plans per ASC 205-40); support any board-directed alternative that does not require misstatement.

**Will not:** report revenue above $850,000; omit or soften the going-concern disclosure; or sign any representation to Crestline, Pell & Marsh, or others based on the drafted figures — irrespective of direction or assurances that responsibility is "owned" elsewhere (ET §2.130.020 prohibits subordinating judgment and knowingly misrepresenting facts).

## 6. Requested of the board

1. Acknowledge the corrected Q4 2025 figures as the company's basis for issuance and for any Crestline communication.
2. Direct or ratify disclosure of substantial doubt per ASC 205-40 (or obtain and document a contrary conclusion from counsel/Pell & Marsh in writing — a conclusion I could not reach on these facts).
3. Decide the financing path with full information: renegotiate Crestline's conditions precedent on accurate numbers, pursue alternatives, or accept the consequences of no bridge — a decision properly the board's, and one the drafted P&L would make for the board under false premises.
4. Confirm the governance channel for further communications of this nature.
