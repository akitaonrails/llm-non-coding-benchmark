# Corrected Separation Agreement Terms (company form)

**Scope.** These are the terms that must change in the company's standard Separation Agreement and General Release before it is used with **any** employee. Citations are to the closed library only (`library/INDEX.md`). Items the library does not address are labeled **[UNVERIFIED — not in library]**.

## 1. Consideration period — § 7 of current form: change "seven (7) days" to "twenty-one (21) days"

- **Current defect:** 7 days to consider.
- **Requirement:** A waiver of ADEA claims is not knowing and voluntary unless the individual is given **at least 21 days** to consider the agreement. **45 days** are required if the agreement is "requested in connection with an exit incentive or other employment termination program offered to a group or class of employees." — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(F) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).
- **Gap:** 21 − 7 = **14 days short**.
- **Drafting note:** **Resolved for Whitcomb** — client confirmed 10/06 the separation is individual ("Just her. No RIF."), so the 21-day period governs her agreement; the 45-day rule stays in the form as a conditional clause for any future group program. For employees under 40 the statute does not compel 21 days, but a single uniform 21-day term avoids an age-based distinction in the form itself.

## 2. Revocation period and effective date — § 7: change "revoke within three (3) days" to "seven (7) days" and add effectiveness language

- **Current defect:** 3 days to revoke; no statement that the agreement is ineffective until the period expires.
- **Requirement:** The agreement must provide that for **at least 7 days following execution** the individual may revoke it, and the agreement **shall not become effective until the revocation period has expired**. — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(G) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).
- **Gap:** 7 − 3 = **4 days short**.
- **Add:** "This Agreement shall not become effective or enforceable, and consideration shall not be paid, until the expiration of the seven (7)-day revocation period."

## 3. Right-to-consult clause — insert (missing from current form)

- **Requirement:** The agreement must **advise the individual in writing to consult with an attorney** before signing. — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(E) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).
- **Add:** "You should consult with an attorney of your choice before signing this Agreement. You are encouraged to do so, and this Agreement was prepared so that you have the opportunity to do so."

## 4. No waiver of future claims — § 4: narrow "release of all claims"

- **Current defect:** "Release of all claims" on its face sweeps in claims that arise **after** signing.
- **Requirement:** A valid ADEA waiver "does not waive any rights or claims that may arise after the date the agreement is executed." — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(C) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).
- **Fix:** Limit the release to claims **arising on or before the date of execution**, with a carve-out sentence: "This release does not waive any rights or claims that may arise after the date this Agreement is executed."

## 5. ADEA-specific reference — § 4: confirm and make explicit

- The current form names "the ADEA" among released statutes, which appears to satisfy the requirement that the waiver **specifically refer to rights or claims under the ADEA** (29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(B)), but the release should do so in an enumerated, unambiguous list (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). Keep the ADEA named expressly.

## 6. Consideration — § 4/§ 3: recite that severance is additional consideration

- **Requirement:** The waiver must be "in exchange for consideration in addition to anything of value to which the individual is already entitled." — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(D) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).
- **Fix:** Add a recital that the severance (e.g., 4 weeks' base pay) is consideration the employee is **not otherwise entitled to**, and carve from the release any right to accrued amounts already owed (e.g., earned wages — any final-pay obligations under Columbia law are **[UNVERIFIED — not in library]**).

## 7. Understandability — global

- **Requirement:** Written in a manner calculated to be understood by the individual. — 29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1)(A) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). Plain-language headings, defined terms, no buried release.

## 8. Consequence of non-compliance (why this matters)

- An ADEA release that fails § 626(f) is **unenforceable as to ADEA claims**, and the employee **may keep the severance and still sue** — *Oubre v. Entergy*, 522 U.S. 422 (1998) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). The current form therefore buys the company nothing against an employee 40+ — which here means **two of the five current Account Managers: Whitcomb (42) and Lind (47)**; the other three (Brennan 35, Osei 29, Park 31) are under the ADEA threshold.

## 9. Items the library does not resolve — **[UNVERIFIED]**

- Enforceability of a release of **FMLA** claims and of **Title VII** administrative-charge rights/EEOC filing: **no library item addresses this; do not rely on it.** Standard practice is to carve out the right to file a charge, but that cannot be verified from this library.
- Knowing/voluntary standards for non-ADEA releases under Columbia law: **[UNVERIFIED — not in library]**; state analog to be checked when authority is available.

## Summary table of required changes

| Term | Current form | Required | Authority (library) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consideration period | 7 days | ≥ 21 days (45 if group program) | § 626(f)(1)(F) |
| Revocation | 3 days | ≥ 7 days + effectiveness clause | § 626(f)(1)(G) |
| Attorney-consult advice | absent | required in writing | § 626(f)(1)(E) |
| Future claims | waived on face of § 4 | cannot be waived | § 626(f)(1)(C) |
| ADEA reference | listed in § 4 | keep, make explicit | § 626(f)(1)(B) |
| Consideration recital | absent | state it exceeds entitlements | § 626(f)(1)(D) |
| Understandability | boilerplate | plain language | § 626(f)(1)(A) |
