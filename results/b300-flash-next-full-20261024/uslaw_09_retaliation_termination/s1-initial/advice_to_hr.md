Twenty-four days of silence is already damaging; more delay compounds it.
- **The investigator must be outside Tom Hale's chain of command** (`library/harassment_investigation_duty.md`) — not HR reporting to Hale, not Hale's peer — **and, given your 10/06 answers, outside your own handling of the file too**: Dana filed with you, and you are now a fact witness to the "wait" instruction and the PIP's authorship.
