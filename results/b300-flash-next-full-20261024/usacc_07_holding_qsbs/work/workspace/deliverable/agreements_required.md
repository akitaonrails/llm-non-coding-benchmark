Under the revised plan (single-member holding structure for the three Colorado businesses, Helio stock
retained personally, real intercompany financing only), the following documents should be put in place.
Item 1 is on the critical path; items marked ⚑ depend on whether a true partnership holding (99/1) is
ultimately adopted instead of the recommended single-member holding LLC.

## A. Cure the current defects (immediately, regardless of structure)

Client has stated she will not paper loans ("no paperwork — I just move money where it's needed").
The §7872/veil-piercing consequences do not depend on her cooperation; the items below therefore
include, for each loan, the **floor option** (documented one-time capitalization or payoff — one form
per event, no ongoing loan program) alongside the full note. The floor is damage control, not advice
of preference.

1. **Intercompany note — Oakline Properties LLC → Oakline Services LLC ($400,000)** — OR, if the
   preferred clean-up is chosen: (i) a **payoff/release** documenting repayment in full, or (ii) a
   **loan capitalization agreement** (debt treated as contribution to capital / distribution, as
   applicable) with corresponding ledger entries. If a note instead: stated principal, term, interest
   **at or above the applicable AFR (engagement AFRs: 4.12% mid-term / 3.96% short-term,
   Rev. Rul. 2026-4)**, payment schedule, default and prepayment terms, signed by both entities.
2. **Intercompany note or capital-contribution agreement — Oakline Services LLC → Oakline Digital
   LLC ($150,000)** — same content; capitalization into Digital is the recommended treatment given
   Digital's losses. The existing "email only" is not documentation.
3. **Historic-loan §7872 catch-up workpapers** — imputed interest and offsetting
   contribution/distribution entries per loan from each funding date (2024-03-01, 2025-06-15,
   2025-09-01); input for amended returns or method change (CPA judgment; not library-covered).
4. **Intercompany treasury/funding policy** — written policy: no transfers between entities except
   under documented notes at ≥ AFR or documented capital contributions; separate bank accounts and
   books per entity; no commingling (the §7872/veil-piercing point in
   `library/irc_7872_afr.md`).
5. **Entity separateness protocol** — separate bank accounts, books, registered addresses/agents,
   contracts in each entity's own name, arm's-length **lease between Properties and Services**
   (currently implied), and annual member resolutions per entity.
