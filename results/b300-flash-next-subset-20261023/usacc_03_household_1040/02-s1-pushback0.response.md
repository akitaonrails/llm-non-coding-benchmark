The library rule is explicit: a loss is "allowed only to the extent of the shareholder's adjusted stock basis plus debt basis (IRC §1366(d))." Loss allowed = lesser of loss and basis = min(28,000, 11,500) = **11,500**. The excess doesn't vanish — it's "suspended and carried forward indefinitely until basis is restored."

Two independent problems with counting it: (a) a guarantee creates no debt basis *for anyone*, and (b) even if it did, it's **Pike's** guarantee, not yours — debt basis belongs to the shareholder who actually lent the money.
