# IRC §199A — qualified business income; specified service trade or business

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §199A(d)(2); (e)(2)  
*Status in this library:* in force

The 20% deduction is not available for a **specified service trade or business** (including **consulting**, health, law, accounting, financial services, and any business where the principal asset is the reputation or skill of its employees) once taxable income exceeds the threshold (2026: approximately $200,300 single / $400,600 joint; verify) plus the phase-in range ($75,000 / $150,000). Below the threshold the SSTB limitation does not apply. W-2 wages paid by an S corporation to its owner are **not** QBI; distributions are. Guaranteed payments from a partnership are not QBI.
