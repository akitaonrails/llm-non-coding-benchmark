# Anti-assignment clauses; change of control; asset sales

*Citation:* *SQL Solutions v. Oracle*, 1991 WL 626458 (N.D. Cal.); Restatement (Second) of Contracts §317, §322; Texas cases on 'operation of law'  
*Status in this library:* in force

A clause barring assignment 'by operation of law, merger, sale of assets, change of control or otherwise' is enforced according to its terms; an asset sale transferring the contract without consent is a breach and the transfer is void where the clause so provides. Absent such language, assignment of contract rights is generally permitted; obligations require consent only if performance is personal. A consent requirement that may be withheld 'in sole discretion' gives the counterparty leverage to renegotiate price.
