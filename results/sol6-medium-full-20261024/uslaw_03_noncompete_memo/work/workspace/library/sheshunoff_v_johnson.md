# Alex Sheshunoff Management Services, L.P. v. Johnson

*Citation:* 209 S.W.3d 644 (Tex. 2006)  
*Status in this library:* in force

The Texas Supreme Court held that a covenant not to compete in an at-will employment agreement is enforceable when the employer's promise (e.g., to provide confidential information) is **performed**, even if not performed at the moment of contracting — **modifying *Light v. Centel Cellular* (1994)** to the extent *Light* required the consideration to be given at the time the agreement was made. The court emphasized that the core inquiry is the reasonableness of the restraint under §15.50.
