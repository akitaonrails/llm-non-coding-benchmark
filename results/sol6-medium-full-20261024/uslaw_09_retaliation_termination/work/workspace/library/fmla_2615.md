# FMLA §105 — interference and retaliation

*Citation:* 29 U.S.C. §2615(a); 29 C.F.R. §825.220  
*Status in this library:* in force

(a)(1) It shall be unlawful for any employer to interfere with, restrain, or deny the exercise of or the attempt to exercise any right under the FMLA. (a)(2) It shall be unlawful to discharge or discriminate against any individual for opposing any practice made unlawful. **Terminating an employee to prevent her from taking approved or requested leave is interference** ('an employer is prohibited from using the taking of FMLA leave as a negative factor in employment actions' — §825.220(c)). An employee who requested leave need not have begun it. Remedies: lost wages, liquidated (double) damages, fees, reinstatement.
