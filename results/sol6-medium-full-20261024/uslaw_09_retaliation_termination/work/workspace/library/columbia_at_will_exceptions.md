# State of Columbia — at-will employment and exceptions

*Citation:* Col. Rev. Stat. §24-34-402 (fictional); common law  
*Status in this library:* in force

Employment is at will, but termination may not be for an unlawful reason (discrimination, retaliation, exercise of statutory rights). State law mirrors Title VII/FMLA with a longer limitations period and uncapped compensatory damages. *(Fictional jurisdiction for this benchmark.)*
