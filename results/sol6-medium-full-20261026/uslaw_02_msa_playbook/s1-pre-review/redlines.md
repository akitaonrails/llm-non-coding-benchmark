# RouteWise MSA — required redlines

These five edits address the playbook's hard rules. Section 10.1 already survives under §12.3. The DPA edit requires a completed Exhibit C to be attached and executed at signing; wording alone does not cure the missing exhibit.

## 1. IP indemnity and liability cap — A1

Replace §§9.1 and 9.3 with:

> **9.1** Except for the liabilities excluded from limitation under Section 9.3, neither party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
>
> **9.3** The exclusions and limitations in this Section 9, including the cap in Section 9.1 and the damages exclusion in Section 9.2, shall not apply to Provider's obligations under Section 10.1; either party's liability for its gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; or Customer's payment obligations. Provider's obligations under Section 10.1 are uncapped and survive expiration or termination as provided in Section 12.3.

The current §9.1 begins “notwithstanding anything to the contrary,” which can control the “uncapped” language in §10.1. [Interpretation authority](../library/contract_interpretation_notwithstanding.md).

## 2. Executed data processing addendum — A2

Replace §5.3 with:

> **5.3** The data processing addendum attached as Exhibit C is incorporated into this Agreement and shall be executed by both parties at the same time as this Agreement. Provider shall not process personal data of EU or UK residents on Customer's behalf unless and until the executed Exhibit C is in effect. The parties shall complete Exhibit C so that it specifies the subject matter and duration, nature and purpose of processing, categories of personal data and data subjects, and the parties' obligations and rights, including the processor obligations required by Article 28(3) of the GDPR where applicable.

Replace the placeholder Exhibit C with the negotiated, completed, signed DPA before signature. The [library's Article 28 excerpt](../library/gdpr_art28.md) identifies the required processor terms.

## 3. Initial and renewal pricing — A3

Replace Exhibit A, §4 (Pricing adjustments) with:

> **4. Pricing adjustments.** Fees stated in this Order Form are fixed for the entire Initial Subscription Term; Provider may not increase them during that term. For each renewal term, Provider may increase the fees once, effective only at the start of that renewal term, by no more than the lesser of five percent (5%) or the percentage increase in the CPI-U over the preceding twelve (12) months. If the CPI-U has not increased, no renewal increase is permitted. Provider must give Customer written notice of any proposed renewal increase at least ninety (90) days before the renewal term begins. In the absence of timely notice, fees for that renewal term remain unchanged.

This replaces the order form's unilateral midterm increase and uncapped renewal language. The order form controls conflicting MSA terms under §14.1.

## 4. Automatic renewal — A4

Replace §3.3 with:

> **3.3** Each Subscription Term shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 5. Affiliate license — A5

Replace §2.2 with:

> **2.2** The license and access rights granted under this Agreement and each Order Form extend to Customer and to Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. and Cobalt Brokerage LLC at no additional fee. Each such Affiliate may use the Services through its employees and contractors, who shall be deemed Users for purposes of this Agreement. Customer remains responsible for those Users' compliance with this Agreement. No separate Order Form or additional affiliate fee is required for these rights.
