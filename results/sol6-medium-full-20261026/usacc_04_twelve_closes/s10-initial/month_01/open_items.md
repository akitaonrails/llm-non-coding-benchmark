Accounts receivable therefore rolls from $92,650 opening + $54,450 billed − $54,450 collected = **$92,650 closing**. The opening receivable balance has no customer aging in the supplied file.

The new $24,000 invoice was initially deferred, then one month released. This follows [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md), which require ratable subscription revenue and deferral of advance billing.

- Vendor costs total $22,070 and were paid in full in January, so opening accounts payable $17,420 remains. Payroll cost is $59,480: $54,404 gross wages + $5,076 employer taxes and benefits. Depreciation is $1,777.78 per the [monthly close checklist](../../library/close_checklist.md). No prepaid expense amortization was recorded because no coverage periods or schedule were provided.
- The supplied opening trial balance includes a $30,450 credit in subscription revenue. It was carried forward exactly; January activity adds $40,650, producing a $71,100 closing credit. The $71,100 is a **cumulative account balance, not January revenue**. No assumption about the opening revenue's fiscal-year ownership was posted.

## Unresolved items

1. **Opening deferred revenue detail — $24,000.** The supplied opening deferred balance is $65,000, while the only identified opening annual contract is Trestle at $41,000. The residual $65,000 − $41,000 = $24,000 is held as an unallocated contract liability in the roll-forward, with no January release. Obtain the contract and service dates before recognizing any portion.
2. **Overlapping Trestle terms.** The supplied note explicitly schedules $8,200 per month on Trestle's June 2024–May 2025 contract, while the signed new order form starts a January–December 2025 term. This close follows both documented schedules. Management should confirm whether the new form grants an additional subscription or replaces the prior service rights; a replacement could change the treatment of the old $32,800 remaining balance and the January $8,200 release. No cancellation or credit was documented. The accounting conclusion depends on the actual promised service and terms under [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md).
3. **Opening balances lacking detail.** No aging was supplied for opening receivables $92,650, no vendor detail for opening payables $17,420, and no coverage schedule for prepaids $9,600. These balances were carried forward; recoverability, completeness, and prepaid amortization cannot be independently checked from the supplied files.
4. **Opening revenue account — $30,450 credit.** Confirm whether this is a 2024 account that should have been closed to retained earnings or an intended 2025 opening activity balance. The opening file supplies no period designation. No reclassification was made without that fact.
