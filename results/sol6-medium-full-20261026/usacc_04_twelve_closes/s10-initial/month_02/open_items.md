# February 2025 close — Board open items and judgments

## February results and accounting

- February invoices and receipts each total **$66,450**: 24 monthly subscriptions $30,450 and Pinecone Dental Group $36,000. All current-month invoices were collected. Accounts receivable remains **$92,650**: $92,650 opening + $66,450 billed − $66,450 collected.
- The client confirmed Pinecone received no bespoke service and self-onboarded. The one-time “implementation & license” label therefore does not establish a distinct, delivered implementation service. Its $36,000 invoice was recorded as deferred revenue, with **$0 recognized in February**, under the substance and set-up guidance in [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md). The client supplied no access end date, so there is no supported period over which to calculate a subscription release. Holding the full amount deferred is a cautious accounting judgment pending that fact; it may understate February revenue if service over a determinable term was provided.
- February subscription revenue posted is **$40,650**: monthly subscriptions $30,450 + Trestle old contract $8,200 + Trestle new contract $2,000. The Trestle releases follow the supplied opening note and January–December order form, respectively. Deferred revenue is **$104,600**: $78,800 opening + $36,000 Pinecone billing − $8,200 old Trestle release − $2,000 new Trestle release.
- February vendor costs total **$22,240** and were paid in full. Opening accounts payable $17,420 therefore remains. Payroll expense is $54,404 wages + $5,076 employer taxes and benefits = **$59,480**, agreeing with bank payments. Depreciation is **$1,777.78** under the [monthly close checklist](../../library/close_checklist.md). No prepaid amortization was recorded because coverage dates remain unavailable.
- The cumulative subscription revenue account closes at **$111,750**: $71,100 January 31 balance + $40,650 February postings. This includes the unexplained $30,450 opening credit; **February revenue is $40,650**, not $111,750.

## Open items carried or identified this month

1. **Pinecone access term — $36,000 deferred.** The customer self-onboarded, and the client has no further information on the access/license end date or price allocation. The entire billing remains deferred. Its release schedule cannot be established. The order form's “one-time” wording alone does not establish that platform access was wholly delivered in February under [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md).
2. **Unallocated opening deferred revenue — $24,000.** The December opening balance was $65,000; only $41,000 was identified as Trestle's old annual contract. The remaining $24,000 has no contract or service dates, and the client has no further detail. It is carried as a liability without release. The February closing deferred balance consists of old Trestle $24,600 + new Trestle $20,000 + Pinecone $36,000 + unallocated $24,000 = **$104,600**.
3. **Trestle's overlapping terms.** The old June 2024–May 2025 release schedule and new January–December 2025 order form both continue in this close. The client could not confirm whether the new form added service or replaced the old contract, nor the treatment of the old unearned balance if replaced. Accordingly, the February $8,200 old-contract release and its remaining $24,600 balance are subject to that unresolved contract interpretation. The accounting depends on the actual promised service under [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md).
4. **Opening balances lacking support.** The client has no further aging for the $92,650 opening receivables, vendor detail for the $17,420 opening payables, or coverage schedule for the $9,600 prepaids. These balances have been carried forward. Recoverability, completeness, and prepaid amortization remain unverified.
5. **Opening revenue credit — $30,450.** The client supplied no period designation for the opening credit in account 4000. It remains in the cumulative trial balance. Whether it belongs to a prior fiscal year and needs a retained-earnings closing entry remains unresolved.
