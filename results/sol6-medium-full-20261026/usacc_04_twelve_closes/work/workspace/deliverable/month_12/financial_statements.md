## Notes

**1. Revenue and deferred revenue.** Subscription access is recognized ratably over documented terms; advance annual billings are deferred under [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md). Deferred revenue reconciles: **$65,000 opening + $156,600 annual billings − $154,600 annual releases = $67,000 closing**. The closing balance is Pinecone $3,000, Marigold $3,000, Quarry $10,000, Saltline $9,000, Vireo $18,000, and $24,000 unidentified opening deferral. Trestle's old June 2024–May 2025 scheduled releases totaled $41,000 in 2025, overlapping with $24,000 released on a new January–December 2025 term. The client could not establish whether the new form added concurrent service or replaced the old rights; the $41,000 old-term revenue remains subject to that unresolved interpretation.

**2. Opening revenue classification.** The December trial balance carries a $543,250 credit in subscription revenue. It includes a **$30,450 credit already present in the supplied opening trial balance**, with no evidence of the period to which it belongs. The income statement reports only the **$512,800 2025 movement** ($543,250 − $30,450). For balance-sheet presentation, opening retained earnings of **($205,953)** and the unidentified opening revenue credit of **$30,450** are grouped as **opening cumulative results of ($175,503)**; this aggregation does not assert that the credit belongs to a particular year. If the $30,450 is established as 2025 revenue, reported 2025 revenue would instead be **$543,250** and the 2025 loss **($477,903.36)**, while opening results would be **($205,953)** and year-end total equity would remain **($33,856.36)**. Classification remains unresolved; no unsupported closing reclassification was posted.

**3. Correction of a previously issued interim error.** The July signed Pinecone amendment established that the original $36,000 order form was a 12-month subscription commencing February 1 and that no implementation service was delivered. February–June had initially deferred the whole amount. Each of those five months was restated to add **$3,000 revenue** and reduce deferred revenue by the same amount, for a **$15,000 cumulative June 30 effect**. Full corrected monthly packages have been prepared while preserving the as-issued files. The July ledger catch-up was separately identified and excluded from July revenue. The 2025 income statement includes **$33,000 Pinecone revenue for February–December (11 × $3,000)**. This is an error correction of original facts, rather than prospective July revenue, under [ASC 250 excerpts](../../library/asc250_excerpts.md) and [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md). The CEO said the original monthly reports went to the Board and bank; delivery of the corrected versions to those recipients is not evidenced.
