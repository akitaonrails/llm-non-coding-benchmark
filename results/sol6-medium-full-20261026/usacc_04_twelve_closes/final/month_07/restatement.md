# Reissued February–June 2025 close balances — Pinecone correction

The signed July 2 amendment says Pinecone's original February 1 order form contained a clerical description error. The original $36,000 consideration bought a 12-month platform subscription beginning February 1, 2025; no implementation service was or will be delivered. The amendment changes neither price nor substantive promised service. Accordingly, the annual subscription should have been recognized at **$36,000 / 12 = $3,000 per month from February**, under [ASC 606 excerpts](../../library/asc606_excerpts.md) and the [Board revenue policy](../../library/revenue_policy_memo.md). The earlier full deferral was an error based on incomplete documentation of original facts, not a July contract modification. [ASC 250 excerpts](../../library/asc250_excerpts.md) require retrospective restatement and disclosure of affected previously issued interim periods.

Each affected month's subscription revenue and net income increase **$3,000**; deferred revenue decreases **$3,000** for February, then a further $3,000 in each subsequent month. Assets, cash, expenses, and other liabilities are unchanged. The February–June balances above are cumulative account balances; the unexplained $30,450 opening revenue credit remains in both as-issued and restated figures. There is no pre-2025 effect or opening retained-earnings adjustment because Pinecone service began February 2025.

Full reissued monthly close packages—including journals with the $3,000 monthly Pinecone release, trial balances, contract roll-forwards, unchanged bank reconciliations, and corrected open-items notes—are in `restated_prior_periods/month_02/` through `month_06/`. Historical as-issued files are retained for audit trail. The July 2 journal entry debits deferred revenue and credits subscription revenue **$15,000** solely to align the continuing ledger with the restated June 30 balance; it is identified as a prior-period correction and **excluded from July revenue**. July's own Pinecone release is a separate $3,000 entry. The restated June deferred balance $113,800 − July releases $10,800 = July closing deferred **$103,000**. Delivery of the reissued packages to prior Board and bank recipients is not evidenced and remains open.
