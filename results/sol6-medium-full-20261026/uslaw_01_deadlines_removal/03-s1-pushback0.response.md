Yes, **on the facts Noor supplied, I would not file a diversity removal notice**. Your friend’s Delaware-versus-Washington comparison would apply to a *corporation*. An LLC is a citizen of every state in which a member is domiciled, regardless of its formation state or office location. Caleb’s 35% ownership is enough: if Seattle is his domicile, Harbor Street is a Washington citizen. Sound Cascade is also a Washington citizen, so complete diversity is absent. The served LLC’s Washington citizenship creates a separate forum-defendant barrier. [LLC citizenship cases](/work/workspace/library/llc_citizenship_cases.md); [28 U.S.C. §§1332, 1441](/work/workspace/library/28usc_1332_1441_1446_1447.md).

The qualification is **domicile**: Noor confirmed where Caleb lives, not whether Seattle is his permanent home. If he is domiciled elsewhere, counsel should reassess removal promptly. Meanwhile, the state-court response deadline is **October 1**; the October 13 removal deadline does not extend it.
