# IRS Notice 2025-5 — 2025 standard mileage rates

*Citation:* Notice 2025-5, 2025-3 I.R.B.  
*Status in this library:* in force

**SECTION 3. STANDARD MILEAGE RATES.** The standard mileage rate for transportation or travel expenses is **70 cents per mile** for all miles of business use (business standard mileage rate) for 2025. [...] The standard mileage rate is 21 cents per mile for use of an automobile for medical care or as part of a move [...], and 14 cents per mile for use of an automobile in rendering gratuitous services to a charitable organization.

**SECTION 4.** [...] Commuting expenses between a taxpayer's residence and a regular place of business are nondeductible personal expenses regardless of the distance.

*(Prior-year rate for reference: 67 cents for 2024; 65.5 cents for 2023.)*
