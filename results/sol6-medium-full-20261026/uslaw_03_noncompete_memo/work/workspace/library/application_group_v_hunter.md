# Application Group, Inc. v. Hunter Group, Inc.

*Citation:* 61 Cal.App.4th 881 (1998)  
*Status in this library:* in force

California had a materially greater interest in applying §16600 to a Maryland employer's non-compete with an employee recruited to work for a California employer; the Maryland choice-of-law clause did not control. California's policy against non-competes is 'fundamental'.
