# Northwind Analytics Inc. — Q4 2025 close memo

## Basis and scope

This memo uses the supplied Q4 draft, contracts, correspondence, cash forecast, and the closed reference library. It assumes the draft's $850,000 of subscription revenue is valid because no underlying subscription contracts or testing were supplied. The $850,000 is therefore a corrected total for the **three matters reviewed**, not an assurance conclusion on all revenue.

## Halvorsen letter of intent

**Facts.** The December 19 letter states a proposed 24-month license for $480,000. It is expressly non-binding except confidentiality, subject to a definitive agreement, procurement approval, and security review. Either party may stop negotiations. The draft booked $480,000 as license revenue.

**Criterion.** A revenue contract requires approved, committed parties, identifiable rights and payment terms, commercial substance, and probable collection. A non-binding letter subject to a definitive agreement lacks enforceable rights and obligations. [ASC 606 contract guidance](../library/asc606_contract_and_bill_hold.md).

**Conclusion and Q4 amount.** No revenue contract is established. Recognize **$0** in Q4; reverse **$480,000**. A signature on the non-binding letter does not cure the stated conditions.

## Saltline kits

**Facts.** Saltline ordered 120 kits for $210,000 and requested February delivery. Northwind invoiced on December 28 but planned to pull kits later from general, untagged inventory. The countersigned side letter permits a full refund of unused kits within 180 days if the Tampa rollout is cancelled or reduced. Saltline did not want a storage charge. The draft booked $210,000.

**Criteria.** Bill-and-hold control transfer requires a substantive reason, separate identification of the customer's goods, readiness for physical transfer, and no ability for Northwind to use or redirect the goods. All are required. A right of return makes consideration variable; revenue is limited to amounts not expected to be refunded and a refund liability is required. [ASC 606 bill-and-hold and return guidance](../library/asc606_contract_and_bill_hold.md).

**Conclusion and Q4 amount.** Saltline's requested delay may provide a substantive reason, but general, untagged stock fails separate identification and leaves Northwind able to direct kits elsewhere. No specific kits are documented as ready for transfer. The return right adds uncertainty; no support for an expected non-refunded amount was supplied. An invoice does not establish transferred control. Recognize **$0** in Q4; reverse **$210,000**. The 2024 Trestle transaction had segregated, labeled units and no return right, so its acceptance does not establish control transfer here.

## Ironvale dedicated infrastructure

**Facts.** The 36-month agreement begins October 1, 2025 and charges $18,000 monthly in arrears. Schedule A identifies 12 servers and two storage arrays by serial number for Northwind's exclusive use. Ironvale can substitute only on failure with Northwind's consent. Northwind directs workloads, configuration, operating systems, and utilization. Ironvale supplies power, cooling, and network. The draft expenses $54,000 for Q4.

**Criterion.** A contract contains a lease when it conveys control over an identified asset for a period for consideration. Exclusive economic use and authority over how and for what purpose equipment is used establish control; power, cooling, and network are non-lease services. The lessee recognizes a right-of-use asset and lease liability at the present value of lease payments. [ASC 842 identification guidance](../library/asc842_identification.md).

**Conclusion and Q4 amount.** This contract contains an equipment lease and separate hosting services. It produces **$0 revenue** and **$0 revenue adjustment**. It cannot remain wholly classified as hosting expense. The draft's Q4 cash fee is 3 × $18,000 = **$54,000**. Total contractual fees are 36 × $18,000 = **$648,000**; the 33 scheduled payments after December 31 total 33 × $18,000 = **$594,000** before allocation and discounting. These totals include services and are **not** a lease liability or right-of-use asset. The client supplied neither the equipment/service payment allocation nor the applicable discount rate. Consequently, both lease balances and the corrected Q4 mix of service expense, right-of-use asset expense, and interest remain unquantified; no zero balance is implied.

## Revenue and earnings bridge

Draft revenue: $850,000 subscription + $480,000 Halvorsen + $210,000 Saltline = **$1,540,000**. Corrected revenue for the reviewed items: $1,540,000 − $480,000 − $210,000 = **$850,000**, which is **$650,000 below** the $1,500,000 bridge condition.

The draft's $456,000 net income reconciles: $1,540,000 − $54,000 hosting − $712,000 salaries and benefits − $318,000 other operating expenses = $456,000. Reversing the two revenue entries alone gives $456,000 − $480,000 − $210,000 = **$(234,000)**. This is **before** the Ironvale lease adjustment; a final corrected net income cannot be calculated from the supplied facts.

## Going concern

Management must assess conditions for one year **after the financial statements are issued**. A plan alleviates substantial doubt only when both effective implementation and mitigation are probable. A non-binding, conditional term sheet is not a probable plan. If doubt remains, disclose that substantial doubt exists. [ASC 205-40 guidance](../library/asc205_40_going_concern.md).

The 13-week forecast begins with $612,000 cash and reaches $8,000 on February 13, **$(105,000)** on February 20, and **$(509,000)** on March 27, 2026, without bridge proceeds. Crestline's $3,000,000 note is non-binding; it requires reviewed or audited Q4 revenue of at least $1,500,000 and no going-concern qualification. The reviewed revenue total is $850,000. The client supplied no other documented plan. On the available facts, substantial doubt exists, and management's plans are **not probable** to alleviate it. Disclosure should describe the cash shortage, financing uncertainty, and substantial doubt.

The actual financial-statement issuance date and cash forecast beyond March 27 were not supplied. This conclusion assumes issuance early enough that the February 20 shortfall falls within the following year, consistent with the contemplated January bridge closing. Management must refresh the assessment at issuance. No assurance opinion or audit-report wording is determined by this memo.
