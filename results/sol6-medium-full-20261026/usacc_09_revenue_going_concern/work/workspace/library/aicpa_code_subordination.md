# AICPA Code of Professional Conduct — subordination of judgment

*Citation:* ET §2.130.020 (members in business)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A member who concludes that a position taken by a supervisor would result in a material misstatement must discuss concerns with the supervisor; if unresolved, escalate to higher levels (audit committee, board), consider documenting the matter, and consider whether to continue the relationship. A member must not knowingly misrepresent facts or subordinate judgment. 'The auditors accepted it last year' is not a basis where the facts differ.
