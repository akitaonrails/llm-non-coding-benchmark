# Session 2 advice to Juniper

**October 26, 2026.** Opposing counsel's September 29 letter changes the correspondence posture, but it does not supply proof that all five remaining conversion fees were waived or that the corrected June balance is invalid. Its description of an earlier demand is materially different from our later Session 1 **draft**. No copy of that earlier demand is in the file, and our draft was not sent. We should nevertheless expressly correct the quoted assertions rather than leave them standing.

## The bad news, plainly

Pinecrest quotes an earlier demand for **$862,000** that allegedly said Juniper “never consented to any direct hire” and threatened a Superior Court suit. The February 11 email **does** consent to Rosa and Tay's direct hires and waives their fees in writing. Bell confirms that he wrote it. A categorical no-consent statement is false on this record. We must retract it in the reply and must not repeat it in any tribunal filing. The email says the waiver was “a one-off,” supporting Juniper on the other five hires, but Bell's later “ha, we'll talk” and “call me” messages create an implied-waiver or estoppel risk as to later hires. Bell says he never agreed to waive those fees; the six-minute call's exact words and Pinecrest's reliance remain unknown. [Account stated and waiver](../../library/columbia_account_stated_waiver.md); [fee-shifting and candor](../../library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md).

The tort claim is also untenable on the present record. Pinecrest cannot interfere with its own contract with Juniper. If the intended relationship is Juniper's at-will relationships with its workers, the file lacks improper means and contains consent to two hires plus Pinecrest's stated corporate reason for reducing agency use. Its draft Rule 12(b)(6) motion has a substantial chance of dismissing the interference count if Juniper files it. No fact in Pinecrest's letter repairs the missing elements, and the agreement excludes consequential damages. Withdraw the **$410,000** lost-profit/tort demand. [Tortious interference](../../library/columbia_tortious_interference.md); [Rule 12(b)(6)](../../library/frcp_12b6.md). Bringing a claim without a non-frivolous basis, or misrepresenting the February email to a tribunal, raises professional-conduct concerns. [Fee-shifting and candor](../../library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md).

The Superior Court threat should also be withdrawn. Exhibit C requires arbitration of contract and related claims and delegates arbitrability to the arbitrator. No individual invoice is below $25,000, so the small-claims exception does not apply. A merits suit would expose Juniper to a stay or motion to compel and potentially Pinecrest's reasonable fees for that motion under Exhibit C. The FAA provisions in the closed library support that result if the transaction involved commerce; the file does not establish that predicate, but the contract's arbitration text independently drives our advice. [FAA §§2–4](../../library/faa_2_4.md); [fee-shifting and candor](../../library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md).
