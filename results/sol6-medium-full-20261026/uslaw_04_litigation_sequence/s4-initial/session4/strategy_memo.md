## Forum, leverage, and downside

Exhibit C requires exclusive AAA arbitration before one arbitrator in Capital City and assigns arbitrability questions to that arbitrator. It survives termination. No invoice fits its exception for a claim solely to collect an undisputed invoice below $25,000. Filing a Superior Court merits suit invites a stay or order to compel arbitration and can expose Juniper to Pinecrest's reasonable fees for that motion. The FAA's statutory mechanisms apply to transactions involving commerce; that predicate is not established in the present file, but the signed contract clearly selects arbitration. [FAA §§2–4](../../library/faa_2_4.md); [fee-shifting and candor](../../library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md).

The prevailing party can seek reasonable attorney fees and costs in arbitration, and the same clause covers an action to compel arbitration. **This cuts both ways.** If Juniper prevails on its supported claims, fee shifting can improve recovery; if Pinecrest prevails, especially after Juniper pursues unsupported theories, Juniper may owe its fees and costs. No dollar estimate is supportable without litigation budgets or a likely-result analysis. Counsel cannot assert the interference claim merely to scare Pinecrest; the current file lacks a non-frivolous factual basis for it, and the February email cannot be denied in a tribunal filing. [Fee-shifting and candor](../../library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md).

If Pinecrest refuses payment or a reasonable evidence exchange, prepare a focused arbitration for supported invoice balances, conversion fees, contractual interest, and applicable fees and costs. Reassess fee exposure before filing.
