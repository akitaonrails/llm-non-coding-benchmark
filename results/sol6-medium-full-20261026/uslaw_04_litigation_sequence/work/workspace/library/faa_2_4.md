# Federal Arbitration Act §§2–4

*Citation:* 9 U.S.C. §§2, 3, 4  
*Status in this library:* in force

§2: A written provision in a contract evidencing a transaction involving commerce to settle by arbitration a controversy arising out of such contract shall be valid, irrevocable, and enforceable, save upon such grounds as exist at law or in equity for the revocation of any contract. §3: On application, a court shall **stay** proceedings on issues referable to arbitration. §4: A party aggrieved by the failure of another to arbitrate may petition for an order **compelling arbitration**. A delegation clause (arbitrability to the arbitrator) is enforced if clear and unmistakable (*Rent-A-Center v. Jackson*, 561 U.S. 63 (2010)). A party that files suit on arbitrable claims may be compelled to arbitrate and, under a fee clause, may owe the fees of the motion to compel.
