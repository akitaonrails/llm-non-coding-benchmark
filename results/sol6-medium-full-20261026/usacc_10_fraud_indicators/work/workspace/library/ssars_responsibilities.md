# AICPA SSARS — preparation/bookkeeping engagements and fraud

*Citation:* AR-C §70; AR-C §90.A (paraphrased)  
*Status in this library:* in force

In a bookkeeping or preparation engagement the accountant does not provide assurance and is not required to perform procedures to detect fraud. However, the accountant **must not knowingly be associated with information that is materially false**, and if the accountant becomes aware of information indicating fraud or noncompliance, the accountant should bring it to the attention of the **appropriate level of management or those charged with governance**, which excludes persons who may be involved, and should document the communication. The accountant does not make legal determinations of fraud.
