## Recommendation

**Reject the $189,100 offer as presently framed and counter promptly, without signing a withdrawal or release.** Assert that the September dilution is ineffective because Section 6.3 required notice by **certified mail and email**; the Manager instead used a text with a PDF and an email that bounced. Ask the Company to withdraw the Dilution Event, recognize the 18% interest, provide the dilution worksheet and valuation support, and suspend any buyout election while the dispute is resolved. Offer to discuss a **voluntary** exit priced from a current independent equity valuation, with the allocation of the $1.5 million contribution and any dilution claim addressed explicitly. The broker's $1.764 million indication at 18% is a negotiating reference, not an established contractual buyout entitlement.

If Bram will not cure the interest or negotiate promptly, prepare a Delaware Court of Chancery action under Section 11.2 seeking a declaration that no valid Dilution Event occurred, restoration of Imani's interest, and relief preventing a buyout premised on that event. Plead the notice defect first. Plead the bona fide purpose and good-faith valuation issues as additional express contract grounds after seeking the supporting records; plead a narrow implied-covenant alternative only for gaps in how discretion is exercised. The affiliate fee claim should be an express Section 5.4 derivative claim for **proved excess over arm's-length terms**, with a separate direct claim for the missed personal disclosure right. Do not lead with fiduciary-duty counts: Section 7.3 eliminates those duties, which Delaware law permits, while preserving the implied covenant. [Delaware LLC Act §18-1101](../library/del_llc_act_18_1101.md); [implied-covenant authorities](../library/implied_covenant_cases.md).

## What survives and what must be proved

**Dilution.** The August 20 to September 20 period exceeds 30 days, so timing is not the defect. Delivery is: the record states no certified mail and a bounced email. The library says dilution provisions require strict compliance with the agreement's notice mechanics and stated purpose. Actual receipt of the text is a foreseeable defense; the record does not establish waiver of the prescribed methods. The notice says only “working capital and opportunities.” Without budgets, a specific transaction, or use-of-proceeds records, a finding that this was a bona fide Company purpose cannot be assumed. [Capital-call authority](../library/capital_call_notice_cases.md).

Section 6.3 separately commits fair value to the Manager **in good faith**. The $7.2 million figure is unexplained and is $2.6 million below the broker's $9.8 million equity indication. Those may value different things or dates; the Manager's figure is not automatically wrong. More troubling, no calculation explains 6.1%. If $7.2 million were pre-contribution equity value and the full $1.5 million bought new Bram equity, a simple proportional example gives `18% × $7.2m ÷ ($7.2m + $1.5m) = 14.8966%`. Using $9.8 million instead gives `18% × $9.8m ÷ ($9.8m + $1.5m) = 15.6106%`. Neither is a definitive Section 6.3 formula because the full agreement, capitalization mechanics, and Manager's worksheet are unavailable. The gap supports an accounting and good-faith challenge, not a judicially certain replacement percentage. The implied covenant can constrain arbitrary discretion but cannot replace an express bargain or create a market-price buyout. [Capital-call authority](../library/capital_call_notice_cases.md); [implied-covenant authorities](../library/implied_covenant_cases.md).

**Affiliate fees.** Section 5.4 permits affiliate transactions only on terms no less favorable than arm's-length terms and requires written disclosure to all Members within 30 days for payments over $50,000 per year. The August 15, 2022 engagement and fixed annual fees clear the threshold. The file shows no timely written disclosure, and Imani reports only generic one-page “management expenses” summaries until she obtained bank statements in 2026. The absence of a writing and the generic summaries make the disclosure claim substantial, subject to limitations. Bram's ownership alone does **not** prove an unfavorable price. The fixed payments are `$180,000 + $195,000 + $210,000 + $225,000 + $240,000 = $1,050,000` for 2022–26, plus **6% of unknown gross rents**. The record has no rent totals, service scope, market comparisons, or evidence that any fee was unnecessary; therefore neither the variable fees nor an excess-damages figure can be calculated. The stated 8% annual escalation also does not reproduce every listed payment exactly, so obtain the contract, amendments, invoices, and ledger before quantifying relief.

The Company paid those fees and would receive any recovery; Imani must bring that monetary claim derivatively, making demand on Bram or pleading futility. Because Bram is the sole Manager and owns the payee, futility has a strong factual basis. Her personal entitlement to timely written disclosure is direct, but distinct damages from late disclosure remain unproved. Wrongful dilution likewise directly harms her percentage interest. [Direct/derivative and demand authority](../library/tooley_direct_derivative.md).

**Buyout.** Section 9.2 uses **Book Value** upon withdrawal or the Manager's election following a Dilution Event. Imani has not said she withdrew, and the full offer and any separate election are unavailable. If this Dilution Event fails, the stated trigger for a compulsory purchase fails. If a valid Dilution Event and election are ultimately established, a gap between book and market value alone does not rewrite the agreed price. The offer's $3.1 million equity figure is unverified without the most recent year-end balance sheet. The Company's ability to enforce a purchase merely by sending an offer is likewise unresolved on the excerpted language.

## Limitations and immediate steps

Assuming filing on October 8, 2026, the three-year lookback begins October 8, 2023. The 2022 and September 2023 fixed payments total `$180,000 + $195,000 = $375,000` and are presumptively outside it; the September 2024–26 fixed payments total `$210,000 + $225,000 + $240,000 = $675,000` and are inside it. The original September 2022 disclosure breach is also presumptively out of time. A challenge to the initial August 2022 contract formation could be time barred independently of later payments. These are timing estimates; the unknown monthly 6% rent payments and any separate later contractual acts need dates. Chancery applies the limitation period by analogy through laches. [Limitations and tolling authority](../library/del_limitations_tolling.md).

Tolling is plausible because Imani first identified both Bram's ownership and the actual fees only from 2026 bank statements obtained through a books-and-records demand, while earlier summaries described only “management expenses.” Whether those summaries put her on inquiry notice is contested. The file shows no affirmative concealment statement, so fraudulent-concealment tolling is weaker; fiduciary-based equitable tolling is also weaker because fiduciary duties were eliminated. The missed express written-disclosure duty helps explain why the details were not discovered. Plead tolling with the actual demand, production, and discovery dates once known rather than treating every old payment as safely recoverable. [Limitations and tolling authority](../library/del_limitations_tolling.md).

Send a written counter and preservation request before the 30-day offer period ends (the exact expiry language in the full letter is unknown). Preserve the text, attached PDF, bounced-email record, prior summaries, bank statements, broker opinion, and all communications about the call. Request the full agreement and amendments, member ledger, certified-mail records, delivery logs, capital-call budget and use of proceeds, the $7.2 million valuation and 6.1% worksheet, the latest year-end balance sheet, affiliate service agreement and invoices, rent totals, and comparable third-party management terms. If Bram threatens an imminent compulsory purchase or ledger change before these issues can be resolved, seek prompt Chancery relief. The standards for interim injunctions and any fee-shifting entitlement are **not supplied in the library and are unverified here**; counsel should assess them before relying on either.

The critical unresolved facts are whether any valid alternate call notice or waiver exists; whether the call had a bona fide purpose; the Manager's actual fair-value and dilution method; market pricing and services for the affiliate; dates relevant to inquiry notice; the year-end balance sheet; and the exact offer/election terms. The client has no additional information on these points, so obtain them from the Company or through litigation discovery rather than assume favorable answers.
