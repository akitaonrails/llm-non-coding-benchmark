# Documents and approvals for the revised plan

Prepare documents that reflect actual transactions. The client confirms there has been no loan paperwork. The library identifies undocumented commingling as harmful to separate-entity liability protection ([§7872 and AFR](../library/irc_7872_afr.md)).

1. **LLC governance:** review or adopt separate operating agreements for Services, Properties, and Digital, covering management, signature authority, distributions, accounting, and approvals. Record member/manager resolutions for cash segregation, ledger cleanup, and future funding. No Holdings operating agreement is needed under the current plan because Holdings should not be formed.
2. **Transfer reconciliation:** prepare a signed schedule tracing Properties → Services $250,000 and $150,000 and Services → Digital $150,000 to bank entries, any repayments, current balances, and contemporaneous intent. Make supported current-dated entries; do not backdate documents.
3. **If actual debt is substantiated:** execute separate intercompany promissory notes naming lender and borrower, principal, actual execution date, appropriate interest rate, maturity, payment schedule, default rights, any security, and entity approvals. Maintain a principal and interest ledger. The library's 4.12% five-year rate supports an illustration, not the rate for historical advances ([§7872 and AFR](../library/irc_7872_afr.md)).
4. **If transfers were owner funding:** prepare accurate distribution and contribution authorizations and entries through Nadia, supported by bank evidence. Federal treatment among disregarded LLCs is unverified in the closed library; the tax preparer should confirm it before recording federal tax interest.
5. **Properties–Services lease and mortgage file:** review or execute a written building lease stating premises, rent, due dates, term, maintenance, taxes, insurance, and approvals. Retain the mortgage, refinancing, and $1.2 million distribution records to reconcile Properties' basis.
6. **Helio stock file:** preserve the purchase agreement, share ledger, $12,000 payment evidence, filed §83(b) evidence, and issuer qualification records. Commission a stock-specific §1202 and estate-transfer review before any disposition or trust transfer; do not include the shares in a Holdings contribution ([§1202 excerpt](../library/irc_1202_excerpt.md)).

If a genuinely beneficial holding structure is later approved, prepare its operating agreement, second-owner admission documents, contribution agreements, tax-basis and liability-allocation schedules, and Colorado/Wyoming filing analysis **before** implementation ([§721 and §752](../library/irc_721_and_752.md); [state nexus summary](../library/state_nexus_summary.md)).
