# Oakline restructuring opinion — 26 October 2026

**Recommendation:** Do not implement the advisor's proposal. Keep Nadia's Helio Grid founder stock directly held, keep the operating LLCs separately governed and accounted for, and reconcile Properties' tax basis and the cash transfers before considering a holding structure.

This opinion relies solely on the closed reference library. Legal conclusions outside it are marked **unverified**. The client's follow-up confirms the mortgage is nonrecourse to her, its refinancing proceeds entered her brokerage account, the proposed 1% owner is her revocable living trust, and the cash transfers had no loan paperwork.

## Helio shares and exclusion at risk

The purchase agreement reports that Nadia bought 3,000,000 shares directly from Helio on February 18, 2020 for $12,000, when the issuer represented that it was a domestic C corporation with under $1 million of gross assets and a qualified active business. More than five years have elapsed. These facts support, but do not conclusively prove, §1202 eligibility: ongoing qualification, issuer history, previous cap use, and actual exit terms remain unknown. The library states 100% exclusion for qualifying post-September 2010 original-issue shares held over five years, subject here to the greater of $10 million or 10 × basis. Ten times $12,000 is $120,000, so the $10 million cap is larger, assuming no prior use ([§1202 excerpt](../library/irc_1202_excerpt.md)).

At the supplied **$9.4 million 409A value**, indicative gain is $9,400,000 − $12,000 = **$9,388,000**. This is the illustrative **exclusion at risk** if the shares are contributed; 409A value is not an actual sale price or tax savings. A direct Nadia sale could qualify for exclusion if all requirements are met. Do not assume a transfer to her revocable trust is a statutory gift: that treatment is **unverified** here, despite the library's gift exception ([§1202 excerpt](../library/irc_1202_excerpt.md)).

## Properties basis and conditional gain

The supplied balance sheet gives buildings $3,400,000 − depreciation $1,120,000 = $2,280,000; plus land $600,000 and Services receivable $400,000 = **$3,280,000 stated asset basis**. Less the $1,900,000 mortgage = **positive $1,380,000 net tax assets**. Reported owner tax capital is **negative $380,000**. Difference: $1,380,000 − (−$380,000) = **$1,760,000 unexplained**. The $1.2 million refinancing cash went to Nadia's brokerage account, but the corresponding tax entries and remaining difference are not documented. No balancing entry is assumed.

For orientation only, if a genuine 99/1 partnership allocated 99% of the sole $1.9 million mortgage to Nadia, the shift would be **$19,000**. If the stated assets are complete, their $3,280,000 basis exceeds the deemed distribution and the §731(a) contribution gain would be **$0**. If reported negative tax capital and the mortgage were instead complete, implied asset basis would be −$380,000 + $1,900,000 = **$1,520,000**, also above $19,000, yielding **$0** under the same narrow assumptions. These are sensitivity calculations, not a final proposed-transaction estimate: omitted liabilities, asset basis, the receivable, the actual allocation, and even partnership status remain unresolved ([§721 and §752](../library/irc_721_and_752.md)). The revised plan has **no contribution** and therefore $0 contribution gain by design.

## Cash-transfer remediation and interest benchmark

The records show Properties → Services $250,000 on March 1, 2024 plus $150,000 on June 15, 2025 = **$400,000**, and Services → Digital **$150,000** on September 1, 2025. No notes, repayment terms, or confirmed current balances exist. Trace transfers and repayments to bank records; determine actual debt versus owner funding; make supported, current-dated ledger entries. If debt is real, sign notes with principal, interest, maturity, payment schedule, and approvals. If the transfers were owner funding, document the actual distributions and contributions instead. Keep each LLC's accounts and books separate ([§7872 and AFR](../library/irc_7872_afr.md)). Federal recognition of transactions among disregarded LLCs is **unverified** by this library.

As a **conditional annual §7872 benchmark only**, assume both balances remain outstanding all year as recognized interest-free five-year loans. At the library's 4.12% mid-term AFR, $400,000 × 4.12% = **$16,480** and $150,000 × 4.12% = **$6,180**, total **$22,660** ([§7872 and AFR](../library/irc_7872_afr.md)). Five years is an assumption, not an existing term. At 3.96% short-term AFR, the figures are $15,840 and $5,940; at 4.58% long-term AFR, $18,320 and $6,870. Actual federal imputation and historical-period amounts cannot be determined from these files.

## Revised step plan

1. Preserve Nadia's direct Helio ownership and obtain a stock-specific §1202 review before any exit or estate transfer. Do not contribute these shares to a partnership ([§1202 excerpt](../library/irc_1202_excerpt.md)).
2. Pause Holdings and all asset contributions. Determine whether there is a genuine second tax owner and reconcile Properties' basis, mortgage allocation, and refinancing distribution. Model any later contribution before execution ([§721 and §752](../library/irc_721_and_752.md)).
3. Separate bank accounts and ledgers, reconcile the three historical transfers, and document each as actual debt or owner capital. Adopt a prospective funding policy ([§7872 and AFR](../library/irc_7872_afr.md)).
4. Update LLC governance and the Properties–Services lease; determine actual federal and Colorado filing requirements for the retained structure ([state nexus summary](../library/state_nexus_summary.md)).
