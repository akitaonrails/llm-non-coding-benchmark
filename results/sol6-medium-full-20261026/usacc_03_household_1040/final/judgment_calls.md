# Judgment calls and alternatives

| Issue | Treatment used | Alternative rejected or open |
|---|---|---|
| S corporation basis | Beginning stock basis $11,500; no direct loan, so $0 debt basis. Allow $11,500 of the $28,000 loss and suspend $16,500. [Form 7203](../library/form7203_instructions_excerpt.md) | Deducting all $28,000 was rejected. A guarantee of corporate bank debt does not give Marcus debt basis. |
| Rental below-market rent | The tenant is unrelated and the rent was never raised. Assume $1,500 was fair for the existing lease, permitting the $19,550 passive special allowance. [Form 8582](../library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md) | The $2,600 current market estimate could support a finding that $1,500 was below fair rent. The library says an unchanged lease alone does not settle that issue. The lease and fair-rent support are unavailable. If rent was below fair rental value, the loss is disallowed; the library's Tax Table would then not cover the resulting taxable income. |
| Rental participation and depreciation | Elena's tenant and repair decisions support active participation. Use the supplied $9,818 depreciation. [Form 8582](../library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md) | The library has no depreciation rules to verify basis or useful life. No contrary participation facts were reported. |
| Rental MAGI | Include the allowed $11,500 nonpassive S corporation loss, producing $110,900 MAGI. [Form 8582](../library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md) | Using the full $28,000 or ignoring the allowed $11,500 was rejected under the excerpt's MAGI rule. |
| Dependents | Claim Sofia only. Leo's father has a signed Form 8332 for his 2025 year. Allow one $2,200 child credit. [1040 instructions](../library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md) | Claiming Leo because he lives with Elena or appears on the 1095-A was rejected. |
| Marketplace household size | Use $91,350 as provisional household income, but leave FPL percentage, applicable figure, credit, and repayment undetermined. [Form 8962](../library/form8962_instructions_excerpt.md) | The earlier use of the library's **family-of-four** FPL was withdrawn: Leo is claimed by his father, and the library has no poverty line for the apparent three-person tax household or rule defining its exact family size. Treating Leo as this return's dependent was rejected. |
| Marketplace policy allocation | Report the 1095-A's $9,900 APTC as a **whole-policy source amount**, not this return's allocated APTC. Leave the latter undetermined. [Form 8962](../library/form8962_instructions_excerpt.md) | The earlier attribution of all premiums, SLCSP, and APTC to this return was withdrawn. The client has no allocation information, and the library has no allocation rule. |
| New York resident period | Assume residency ended June 30, giving 181 days. [NY IT-203](../library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md) | A later July domicile change remains possible because July 1–3 travel and the exact Florida occupancy date are unknown. |
| New York wages | Treat all $122,000 as New York wages. Both employers are New York based; Florida remote work was at each employee's request. [NY IT-203](../library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md) | Excluding post-move wages solely because the work was remote was rejected under the convenience rule. Physical New York workdays after June 30 remain unknown but would not reduce this all-New-York wage result. |
| Federal deduction | Use the $31,500 MFJ standard deduction. Documented home mortgage interest $6,480 plus $6,752 state withholding as a potential itemized amount totals $13,232. [1040 instructions](../library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md) | Itemizing was rejected on the supplied facts. The closed library lacks complete itemized-deduction rules, and other deductible amounts may be unreported. |
| New York tax balance | Report $6,752 withholding and the wage allocation, without a tax/refund amount. [NY IT-203](../library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md) | A New York tax or refund calculation cannot be supported because the library has no state tax table or complete computation rules. |
