## Why the decision is risky

- Dana complained to HR about Tom's repeated appearance and home/travel comments on September 12. Tom issued her first PIP September 16: **September 16 − September 12 = 4 days**. Termination on October 6 would be **October 6 − September 12 = 24 days** after the complaint. An internal sexual-harassment complaint is protected opposition, and close timing and thin or uneven performance explanations can support a retaliation claim. No investigation has yet tested the complaint or the PIP. [Title VII retaliation](../library/title_vii_704a.md) · [Investigation duty](../library/harassment_investigation_duty.md)
- Dana asked for intermittent FMLA leave September 30 and submitted a certification October 3; HR has not responded. October 6 is **6 days** after the request. Tom's October 2 email says to act before she is out on leave because moving on her afterward would be a “nightmare.” That is strong evidence of an intent to use anticipated leave as a negative factor. The FMLA authority covers an employee who has requested leave even if leave has not begun. Confirm employer coverage, Dana's eligibility, and that the requested leave qualifies before reaching a final legal conclusion; the file does not supply those facts. [FMLA](../library/fmla_2615.md)
- Dana's 2026 year-to-date quota attainment is 88%. Same-role employees Brennan, Osei, and Park are at 79%, 84%, and 86%, respectively, without PIPs: **88 − 79 = 9**, **88 − 84 = 4**, and **88 − 86 = 2 percentage points**. This makes a quota-only explanation difficult on the current data. Lind, age 47, is at 103%, so she is a weaker performance comparator. The data do not show territories, quota difficulty, CRM timeliness, managers, tenure, or actual quarterly results. Dana's 88% year-to-date figure cannot be directly compared with the PIP's 100% quarterly target. Her 60-day PIP had run only **20 days** by October 6 (**October 6 − September 16 = 20**), leaving **40 days** in the stated plan. These gaps require an objective review; they do not prove discrimination by themselves. [Title VII retaliation](../library/title_vii_704a.md)

The strongest present claims are Title VII retaliation, FMLA interference if its prerequisites are met, and parallel Columbia-law claims. The underlying harassment merits investigation: the file lacks the exact comments, frequency, context, and witness evidence needed to assess its merits. Age discrimination is possible to examine because Dana is 42 and the three lower-attainment employees without PIPs are younger, but this record contains no age remark and is too thin for a strong age-based conclusion. 4. **Independently audit performance.** Obtain the actual PIP, objectives and dates; quarterly quota and CRM records; territory and pipeline assignments; prior reviews; and the same data for comparators. Test whether the 100% quarterly target and 24-hour CRM rule were consistently applied and whether a 60-day PIP was ordinarily allowed to run. Notify HR, Tom, other decision-makers, IT, and relevant custodians; suspend deletion or overwriting and document collection. The complaint followed by contemplated termination makes litigation reasonably foreseeable. [Litigation hold](../library/litigation_hold.md)

A covered group program calls for 45 days to consider. The form also needs a clear cutoff for future claims and confirmation that severance is additional consideration.
