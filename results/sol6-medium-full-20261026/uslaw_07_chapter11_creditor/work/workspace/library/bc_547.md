# 11 U.S.C. §547(b), (c)(2), (c)(4), (g) — preferences

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §547  
*Status in this library:* in force

(b) The trustee may avoid any transfer of an interest of the debtor in property— (1) to or for the benefit of a creditor; (2) for or on account of an antecedent debt; (3) made while the debtor was insolvent (presumed during the 90 days, §547(f)); (4) made **on or within 90 days before the date of the filing**; (5) that enables the creditor to receive more than in a chapter 7 liquidation. **(c)(2) Ordinary course:** the trustee may not avoid a transfer to the extent it was in payment of a debt incurred in the ordinary course and the transfer was made **(A) in the ordinary course of business or financial affairs of the debtor and the transferee, or (B) according to ordinary business terms**. Payments made under **shortened terms, pressure, or unusual methods (wire on demand) compared with the parties' history** generally fall outside (c)(2)(A). **(c)(4) New value:** to the extent that, after such transfer, the creditor gave new value to or for the benefit of the debtor not secured and on account of which the debtor did not make an otherwise unavoidable transfer. (g) The creditor bears the burden on (c) defenses.
