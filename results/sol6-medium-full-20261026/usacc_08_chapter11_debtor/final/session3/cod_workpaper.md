# COD and tax-attribute workpaper — provisional

The plan's allowed general unsecured claims are **$8,400,000**. Cash recovery is 35% × $8,400,000 = **$2,940,000** over 24 months. The nominal balance discharged on September 30, 2026 is $8,400,000 − $2,940,000 = **$5,460,000**. The discharge occurs in the confirmed title 11 case, so the $5,460,000 of COD income is excluded from gross income under §108(a)(1)(A); taxable COD from this specified claim pool is **$0** before considering any other tax issue. The DIP facility is repaid in full and is not part of this discharged-debt calculation. [26 U.S.C. §108(a)(1)(A)](../../library/irc_108.md)

The exclusion requires tax-attribute reduction after tax for the discharge year is determined. Under the default statutory order, NOLs are reduced first. If the petition-date $6,100,000 NOL carryforward is still the applicable NOL balance, the calculation is $6,100,000 − $5,460,000 = **$640,000** remaining. That uses the full exclusion, so no reduction reaches credits, capital losses, or property basis under this assumed NOL balance. The actual 2026 discharge-year NOL, intervening use or adjustment of the carryforward, and other attributes were not provided; the final reduction can differ. [26 U.S.C. §108(b)(1)–(2)](../../library/irc_108.md)

Section 108(b)(5) permits an election to reduce depreciable-property basis first. **No election is modeled** for this schedule; the client's actual or intended election is unknown. The supplied $3.2 million fixed-asset tax basis is from the petition date and is not verified as depreciable-property basis at the time relevant to the election. Nor is there a forecast comparing the value of future NOL deductions with future depreciation deductions. For illustration only, if all $3,200,000 were eligible depreciable basis and a full basis-first election were made, the remaining exclusion for NOL reduction would be $5,460,000 − $3,200,000 = **$2,260,000**, leaving $6,100,000 − $2,260,000 = **$3,840,000** of the assumed NOL. This is not a documented election or a recommended amount. [26 U.S.C. §108(b)(5)](../../library/irc_108.md)
