# April professional fees — payment instructions

Dkt. 77, entered April 14, permits retained professionals to serve monthly statements. If no objection is made within 14 days, the debtor may pay 80% of eligible fees and 100% of eligible expenses. The remaining 20% of fees is payable only after a court-approved interim or final fee application. No amount for work before the professional's retention date may be paid. This is an interim payment procedure, not a fee award. [11 U.S.C. §§330(a), 331](../../library/bc_330_331.md)
