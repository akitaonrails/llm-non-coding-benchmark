# September 30, 2026 emergence balance sheet — presentation and unresolved amounts

**Working conclusion:** on the documented 60% old-equity retention, assuming those are voting shares and the supplied values satisfy the test-date measure, Redwood does **not** adopt fresh-start reporting. It carries forward its asset bases rather than resetting assets to the $14 million reorganization value. Liabilities compromised under the plan are measured at the **present value of amounts to be paid**, and the gain on discharge is a **reorganization item in the income statement**. The gain is not credited directly to equity or netted against post-petition assets. This conclusion must be revisited if voting rights or pre-confirmation values differ. [FASB ASC 852-10-45-19 through 45-29; 852-10-45-9](../../library/asc852_fresh_start.md)
