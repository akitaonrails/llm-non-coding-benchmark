# Operating agreement — buy-sell and governance checklist

*Citation:* Practitioner checklist (ABA Business Law Section, paraphrased)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Trigger events: death, disability, divorce (spousal interest), bankruptcy, voluntary departure, for-cause removal. Price: formula or appraisal; discounts. Payment terms. Vesting of founder units with repurchase at cost for unvested units. Deadlock resolution for 50/50 owners (mediation → buy-sell shotgun). Drag-along/tag-along. Transfer restrictions and spousal consent in community-property jurisdictions.
