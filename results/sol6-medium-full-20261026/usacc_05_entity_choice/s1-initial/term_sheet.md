# Draft operating agreement term sheet

**Entity and economics.** California manager-managed LLC, taxed as a partnership. Priya and Tomás are equal managers and each owns 50% of future profit and voting rights, subject to Tomás's vesting and repurchase terms. On the working $120,000 tool valuation and $20,000 cash, opening economic capital accounts are $120,000 and $20,000. Neither member may withdraw opening contributed capital without both members' written approval. The initial waterfall returns contributed capital according to those accounts before remaining appreciation/profits are shared 50/50. Tax capital and built-in gain allocations need tax counsel's written schedule because the library does not specify them. [§721 and profits-interest guidance](../library/irc_721_and_revproc_93-27.md)

**Contributions and IP.** Priya executes a specified PriceLab assignment or exclusive license, with code, documentation, access credentials, third-party rights, and a schedule of prior licenses. The parties obtain a value for the rights actually transferred; the existing $120,000 appraisal is explicitly for a going-concern license. Tomás contributes $20,000 cash. Both assign LLC-created IP and client contracts where assignable. No client relationships are booked as contributed capital without a separately substantiated transferable property right and valuation.

**Vesting.** Tomás's 50% future-profits interest is granted on the actual documented grant date. It vests over 36 months: 0% until the first anniversary, 1/3 on that anniversary, then 1/36 of the total interest monthly for each of the next 24 months. The LLC may repurchase unvested units for the lower of the amount paid for those units or their fair market value when he leaves. His $20,000 contributed-capital claim is tracked separately and is not forfeited as a service-vesting penalty. A protective §83(b) election is considered within 30 days after grant, with treatment as a partner from grant if using the profits-interest safe harbor. [§721 and profits-interest guidance](../library/irc_721_and_revproc_93-27.md); [§83(b)](../library/irc_83b.md)

**Operations and reserved decisions.** Each manager may sign routine customer and vendor commitments within an annual budget approved by both. Both must approve debt, owner pay/draws, new equity, IP sale or material license, transactions with either founder or family, tax classification change, hiring above an agreed pay threshold, and sale or dissolution. Set dollar thresholds in the signed agreement; none were supplied by the client. Review profits and cash needs quarterly before distributions. Require reserves for taxes, payroll/contractors, and working capital.

**Departures and purchase rights.** Trigger a company first option, then the other founder's option, upon death, permanent disability, voluntary departure, for-cause removal, divorce or attempted spouse transfer, bankruptcy, or prohibited transfer. Vested interests are bought at fair market value determined by one jointly selected independent appraiser or, if the founders cannot agree, the average of two independent appraisals. Define cause narrowly (fraud, material willful misconduct, uncured material breach), with written notice and cure opportunity. Unvested service units follow the repurchase rule above. Pay 20% at closing and the balance over 36 months at an agreed market rate, with collateral and acceleration on default; final rate is a negotiated term. Insurance may fund death/disability purchase. [Buy-sell checklist](../library/llc_buy_sell_checklist.md)

**Deadlock.** Written notice describing the decision; 15 days of founder negotiation; then mediation within 30 days. If unresolved 30 days after mediation starts, either may initiate a reciprocal buy-sell process: one names a cash price per vested unit and the other must buy at that price or sell at that price within 30 days, with closing 60 days later. An independent appraisal alternative should apply if either lacks realistic financing; fundability and local enforceability require lawyer review. Preserve ordinary-course spending authority under the last approved budget during the process. [Buy-sell checklist](../library/llc_buy_sell_checklist.md)

**Transfers and spouse.** No transfer, pledge, or admission of a new member without the other's written consent, subject to a right of first refusal. A permitted estate or family transfer remains bound by the agreement and does not gain management rights automatically. Require Tomás's spouse to acknowledge transfer restrictions and buy-sell provisions; obtain Mexican community-property advice, because its legal effect is outside the library. For a third-party sale, include proportionate tag-along and a drag-along only on both founders' approval while they remain 50/50. [Buy-sell checklist](../library/llc_buy_sell_checklist.md)

**Disputes and records.** California governing law and forum, subject to local-law review for Tomás's marital rights and cross-border enforcement. Maintain separate books, capital ledgers, client contracting records, U.S./Mexico work-location data, and annual tax information. Amend economic or voting rights only by both founders' signed consent.
