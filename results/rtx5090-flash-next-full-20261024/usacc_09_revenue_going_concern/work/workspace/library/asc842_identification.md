# ASC 842 — identifying a lease embedded in a service contract

*Citation:* FASB ASC 842-10-15-3 to 15-20  
*Status in this library:* in force

A contract contains a lease if it conveys the right to control the use of an **identified asset** for a period in exchange for consideration. An asset is identified if explicitly specified (e.g., by serial number) and the supplier has no substantive substitution right. The customer controls use if it obtains substantially all economic benefits and **directs how and for what purpose** the asset is used. Dedicated servers identified by serial number, used exclusively by the customer, whose workloads the customer directs, are an embedded lease; the hosting services (power, cooling, network) are a non-lease component. The lessee recognizes a right-of-use asset and lease liability at the present value of lease payments.
