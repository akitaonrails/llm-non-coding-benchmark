# 2025 Schedule SE — computation excerpt

*Citation:* IRS, Schedule SE (Form 1040) 2025  
*Status in this library:* in force

Net earnings from self-employment = net profit from Schedule C × **92.35%** (0.9235). Self-employment tax = net earnings × **15.3%** (12.4% social security up to the wage base + 2.9% Medicare), where the social security portion applies only to the extent that the sum of W-2 social security wages and net SE earnings does not exceed the 2025 wage base of **$176,100**. One-half of SE tax is deductible on Schedule 1, line 15.
