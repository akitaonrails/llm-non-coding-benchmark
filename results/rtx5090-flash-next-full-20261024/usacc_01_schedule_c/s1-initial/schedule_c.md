## Gross receipts reconciliation — why Line 1 is NOT "the sum of the 1099s"

Dana asked to "just add up the 1099s": 18,500.00 (Beacon) + 12,200.00 (Orchard) + 9,400.00 (Tessaro)
+ 41,350.00 (PayHub 1099-K) = **81,450.00**. That overstates 2025 receipts by **30,700.00** because
of double reporting:

- The bank export shows only three kinds of business money in 2025: PayHub payouts, Tessaro ACH
  credits, and Larkspur payroll. There is **no** separate Beacon or Orchard deposit. Since Dana
  says one account only, the Beacon ($18,500.00) and Orchard ($12,200.00) payments must have come
  through PayHub, i.e., they are already inside the 1099-K gross of 41,350.00. The 2025 Schedule C
  instructions excerpt says a payment reported on more than one information return is **included
  only once**.
- PayHub ties out exactly: net payouts deposited 2025 = 40,150.85 (11 payouts, Feb–Dec);
  41,350.00 × 2.9% fee = 1,199.15; 41,350.00 − 1,199.15 = 40,150.85. The February payout 1,812.55
  equals 1099-K box 5b gross 1,866.68 × 0.971. So 1099-K gross = money actually received in 2025.
- Tessaro paid by direct ACH, not PayHub: 2025 deposits 2,400.00 (Mar 19) + 3,100.00 (Jun 19) +
  2,150.00 (Sep 19) + 1,750.00 (Nov 19) = 9,400.00 = the 1099-NEC.
- The ACH credit of 3,000.00 on **2024-12-27** (Tessaro invoice 1041) was received in December
  2024 → **prior year** under the cash method (instructions excerpt, "Cash method"), not 2025.

**Line 1 = 41,350.00 (1099-K gross) + 9,400.00 (Tessaro NEC) = 50,750.00.**
*Confirmed: Dana — "Beacon and Orchard both pay me through PayHub. Tessaro pays by bank
transfer."* The double-reporting treatment is now client-confirmed, matching the documentary
reconciliation.
