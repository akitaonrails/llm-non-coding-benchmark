**2. "Tenho relatório assinado das 5 e 6" — proof of the debt ≠ stopping the clock.**
Interruption requires an act *from outside the creditor*: the judge's citation order (art. 202 I) or an unequivocal act *of the debtor* recognizing the debt (art. 202 VI).
