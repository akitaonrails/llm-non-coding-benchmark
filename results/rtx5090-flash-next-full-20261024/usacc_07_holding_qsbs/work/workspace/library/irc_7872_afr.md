# IRC §7872 — below-market loans; applicable federal rates

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §7872; Rev. Rul. 2026-4 (AFR table, excerpt)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Interest-free or below-market loans between related entities are recharacterized: the lender is treated as receiving imputed interest at the AFR and the borrower as paying it (and, between commonly owned entities, as making a corresponding distribution/contribution). AFR for this engagement — short-term (≤3 yr): 3.96%; **mid-term (3–9 yr): 4.12%**; long-term: 4.58%. Loans without notes, interest or repayment terms are also evidence of commingling that undermines separate-entity liability protection (veil piercing).
