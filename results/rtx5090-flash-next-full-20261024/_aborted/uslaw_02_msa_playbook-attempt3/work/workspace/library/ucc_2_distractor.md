# UCC Article 2 — scope (excerpt)

*Citation:* U.C.C. §2-102; §2-316  
*Status in this library:* in force

Article 2 applies to transactions in **goods**. Courts applying the predominant-purpose test generally hold that a subscription to hosted software (SaaS) is a **service**, not a sale of goods, so Article 2's implied warranties and gap-fillers do not apply directly (though some courts apply them by analogy to software licenses). *(Included as context; the MSA is a services agreement.)*
