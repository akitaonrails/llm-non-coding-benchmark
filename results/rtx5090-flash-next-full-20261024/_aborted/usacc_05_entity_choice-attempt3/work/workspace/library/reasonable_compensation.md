# Reasonable compensation for S corporation shareholder-employees

*Citation:* Rev. Rul. 74-44; *Watson v. Commissioner*, 668 F.3d 1008 (8th Cir. 2012); IRS Fact Sheet FS-2008-25  
*Status in this library:* in force

An S corporation shareholder who performs substantial services must be paid reasonable compensation as wages before distributions. In a personal-services business where the shareholders generate essentially all of the revenue, courts have recharacterized distributions as wages where salary was set far below what comparable professionals earn (*Watson*: $24,000 salary vs. ~$200,000 of distributions recharacterized in large part). Payroll taxes, penalties and interest follow.
