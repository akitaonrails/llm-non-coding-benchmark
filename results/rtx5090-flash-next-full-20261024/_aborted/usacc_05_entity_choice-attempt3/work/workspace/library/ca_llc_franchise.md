# California LLC fee and franchise tax; foreign qualification

*Citation:* Cal. Rev. & Tax. Code §§17941, 17942, 23153; Cal. Corp. Code §17708.02  
*Status in this library:* in force

Every LLC doing business in California pays the $800 annual tax and a gross-receipts fee ($900 at $250k–$499,999; $2,500 at $500k–$999,999 ...). A Delaware LLC doing business in California must register as a foreign LLC and pays the same amounts; failure bars it from maintaining an action in California courts and incurs penalties.
