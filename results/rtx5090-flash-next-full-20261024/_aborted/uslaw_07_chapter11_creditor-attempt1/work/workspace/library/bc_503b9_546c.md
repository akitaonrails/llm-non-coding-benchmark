# 11 U.S.C. §503(b)(9) and §546(c) — 20-day goods; reclamation

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §503(b)(9); §546(c)(1)  
*Status in this library:* in force

§503(b)(9): administrative expense for **the value of any goods received by the debtor within 20 days before the date of commencement** of a case in which the goods have been sold to the debtor in the ordinary course of the debtor's business. **Services, freight and handling are not 'goods'.** §546(c): a seller of goods may reclaim goods received by the debtor while insolvent within 45 days before the petition, by written demand not later than 45 days after receipt (or 20 days after the petition if the 45 days expire after commencement); reclamation is subject to prior liens (here the DIP lender's blanket lien).
