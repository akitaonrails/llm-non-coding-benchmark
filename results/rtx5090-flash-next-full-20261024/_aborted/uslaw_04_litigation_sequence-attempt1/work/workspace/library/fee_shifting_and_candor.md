# Fee-shifting clauses; candor to tribunal and client

*Citation:* Columbia R. Prof. Conduct 3.3, 1.4, 3.1; *Zuniga v. Mercer*, 198 Col. 2d 77 (2017)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Contractual prevailing-party fee clauses are enforced as written, including in arbitration and in motions to compel (*Zuniga*). Rule 3.1: a lawyer shall not assert a claim without a non-frivolous basis; Rule 1.4: a lawyer shall keep the client reasonably informed, including of adverse developments; Rule 3.3: no false statements of fact to a tribunal — a demand letter is not a tribunal filing, but a misstatement in it that is repeated in a pleading becomes one.
