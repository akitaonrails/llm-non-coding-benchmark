# Retirement plan division — QDRO; early-distribution penalty exception

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §414(p); §72(t)(2)(C); ERISA §206(d)(3)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A division of a qualified plan (401(k)) between spouses must be made by a **qualified domestic relations order**; absent a QDRO, a distribution to the participant to pay the former spouse is taxable to the participant and, if under 59½, subject to the 10% additional tax. Distributions to an alternate payee **under a QDRO** are exempt from the §72(t) 10% penalty (§72(t)(2)(C)) and are taxed to the alternate payee; the alternate payee may roll over to an IRA. An IRA is divided by transfer incident to divorce without a QDRO (§408(d)(6)).
