# Capital calls and dilution — strict compliance with notice and purpose requirements

*Citation:* *Zimmerman v. Crothall*, 62 A.3d 676 (Del. Ch. 2013); contract-interpretation principles  
*Status in this library:* in force

Dilution provisions are enforced as written, but **the issuer must comply strictly with the agreement's notice mechanics and purpose requirements**; a call that fails the specified delivery method or lacks the stated bona fide purpose does not trigger dilution. A 'fair value' determination committed to the manager's 'good faith' is reviewable under the implied covenant for arbitrariness or bad faith (e.g., a value far below a contemporaneous independent indication).
