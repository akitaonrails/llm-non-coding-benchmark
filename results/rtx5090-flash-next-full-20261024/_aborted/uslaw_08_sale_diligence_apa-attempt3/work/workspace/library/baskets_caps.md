# Indemnity baskets and caps — deductible vs tipping; survival

*Citation:* ABA Deal Points Study (private targets); market summary  
*Status in this library:* in force

A **deductible** basket: seller pays only losses above the threshold. A **tipping** basket: once the threshold is crossed, seller pays from the first dollar. Market: cap ~10% of price for general reps; fundamental reps and taxes to purchase price; **a specific operational rep (like worker classification) carved out to a full-price cap is off-market and should be resisted or replaced with a sized special indemnity**. Survival 12–24 months general.
