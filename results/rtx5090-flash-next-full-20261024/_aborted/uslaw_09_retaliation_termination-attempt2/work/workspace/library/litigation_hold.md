# Litigation hold; spoliation

*Citation:* Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(e); *Zubulake v. UBS Warburg*, 220 F.R.D. 212 (S.D.N.Y. 2003)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Once litigation is reasonably anticipated — e.g., an internal discrimination complaint followed by contemplated termination — the employer must preserve relevant documents and ESI, including executives' emails and texts. Failure may result in adverse-inference instructions or sanctions.
