# State of Columbia — equitable distribution and disclosure

*Citation:* Col. Rev. Stat. §14-10-113 (fictional); Col. R. Fam. P. 16.2  
*Status in this library:* in force

Marital property is divided **equitably**, considering economic circumstances, contributions, and **the tax consequences of the division to each party** (§113(1)(d)). Each party must file a sworn financial affidavit with **full disclosure of income, including bonuses and expected compensation**; a party who fails to disclose may have the judgment reopened and may be sanctioned, including an unequal division in favor of the other party (Rule 16.2(e)).
