# Filing status; dependents of divorced parents; Form 8332

*Citation:* IRC §§2(b), 7703(a), 152(e); Form 8332  
*Status in this library:* in force

Marital status is determined on **December 31**; a decree final on or before that date → each files single or head of household. **Head of household** requires a qualifying child living with the taxpayer more than half the year and payment of more than half the cost of the home. **§152(e):** the custodial parent (more nights) claims the child unless the custodial parent releases the claim on **Form 8332**; the release moves the child tax credit but **not** head-of-household status, EITC or the dependent-care credit, which stay with the custodial parent.
