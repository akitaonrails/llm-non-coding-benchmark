# Critical vendor orders — practice note

*Citation:* *In re Kmart Corp.*, 359 F.3d 866 (7th Cir. 2004); Dkt. 44 in this case  
*Status in this library:* in force

A debtor may seek authority to pay certain pre-petition claims of vendors whose continued supply is essential and who cannot be replaced; the vendor must typically agree to customary trade terms for the case. Being designated a critical vendor is the legitimate route to early payment of pre-petition claims; it is at the debtor's discretion and the court's approval.
