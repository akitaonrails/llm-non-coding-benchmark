# Step transaction and substance over form

*Citation:* *Commissioner v. Court Holding Co.*, 324 U.S. 331 (1945); *Gregory v. Helvering*, 293 U.S. 465 (1935)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A series of formally separate steps is collapsed into a single transaction when they are interdependent or pre-planned toward a single end; tax consequences follow the substance.
