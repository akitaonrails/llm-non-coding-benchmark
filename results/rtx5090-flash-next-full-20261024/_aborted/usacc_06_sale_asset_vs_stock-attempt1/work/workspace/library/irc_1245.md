# IRC §1245 — gain from dispositions of certain depreciable property

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §1245(a)(1)  
*Status in this library:* in force

If section 1245 property is disposed of, the amount by which the lower of (A) the recomputed basis of the property, or (B) the amount realized, exceeds the adjusted basis of such property **shall be treated as ordinary income**. (Recomputed basis = adjusted basis plus depreciation allowed or allowable.) Such gain is recognized notwithstanding any other provision of this subtitle.
