# Earn-out drafting — EBITDA definitions and buyer control

*Citation:* Practice note; *Lazard Technology Partners v. Qinetiq*, 114 A.3d 193 (Del. 2015)  
*Status in this library:* in force

An earn-out measured by 'EBITDA as determined by Buyer under Buyer's policies' lets the buyer allocate overhead, management fees, integration costs and purchase-accounting adjustments against the target, zeroing the earn-out. Seller protections: defined accounting principles (seller's historical practices or GAAP consistently applied), exclusion of buyer overhead allocations and transaction/integration costs, covenant to operate the business in the ordinary course and not take actions with the purpose of avoiding the earn-out, information and audit rights, dispute resolution by an independent accountant, acceleration on sale of the business.
