# Texas — non-competes ancillary to the sale of a business

*Citation:* Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §15.50; *Marsh USA v. Cook*, 354 S.W.3d 764 (Tex. 2011); sale-of-business cases  
*Status in this library:* in force

Covenants ancillary to the sale of a business are reviewed more leniently than employment covenants but must still be reasonable in time, area and scope; courts reform unreasonable covenants (§15.51(c)). Seven years / 500 miles for a local custom-cabinetry shop is likely overbroad as to both; 3–5 years within the trade area is typical. Reformation risk means the buyer, not the seller, should care — but an overbroad covenant allocated $400k of price (see tax engagement) invites IRS scrutiny of the allocation as well.
