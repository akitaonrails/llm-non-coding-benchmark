# DeSantis v. Wackenhut Corp.

*Citation:* 793 S.W.2d 670 (Tex. 1990)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Applying Restatement (Second) of Conflict of Laws §187, the Texas Supreme Court **refused to apply a Florida choice-of-law clause** to a non-compete for an employee working in Texas, because Texas had a materially greater interest and application of Florida law would contravene fundamental Texas policy on non-competes. The case stands for the proposition that a choice-of-law clause does not control the enforceability of a non-compete where the state with the materially greater interest has a fundamental contrary policy.
