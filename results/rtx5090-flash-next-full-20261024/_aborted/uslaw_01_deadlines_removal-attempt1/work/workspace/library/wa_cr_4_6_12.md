# Washington Superior Court Civil Rules — CR 4, 6, 12 (excerpts)

*Citation:* Wash. Super. Ct. Civ. R. 4(a)(2), 6(a), 6(e), 12(a)  
*Status in this library:* in force

**CR 4(a)(2)** The summons must require the defendant to defend the action by serving a response **within 20 days after service**, excluding the day of service (60 days if served outside the state by publication or under RCW 4.28.185 — *not applicable to personal service on a registered agent*). **CR 12(a)(1)** A defendant shall serve an answer within **20 days** after the service of the summons and complaint upon the defendant. **CR 6(a)** In computing any period of time, the day of the act from which the period begins to run shall not be included; the last day is included unless it is a Saturday, Sunday or **legal holiday** (as defined by RCW 1.16.050), in which event the period runs until the end of the next day which is not one of those. **CR 6(e)** Whenever a party has the right or is required to do some act within a prescribed period after the **service of a notice or other paper** upon the party and the notice or paper is served by mail, **3 days shall be added**. *(CR 6(e) concerns papers served under CR 5, not the summons under CR 4.)*
