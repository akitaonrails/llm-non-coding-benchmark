# Citizenship of limited liability companies for diversity

*Citation:* *Johnson v. Columbia Properties Anchorage, LP*, 437 F.3d 894, 899 (9th Cir. 2006); *Carden v. Arkoma Associates*, 494 U.S. 185 (1990)  
*Status in this library:* in force

An unincorporated association — including a **limited liability company** — is a citizen of **every state of which its owners/members are citizens**; the §1332(c)(1) corporate test (state of incorporation and principal place of business) does **not** apply. *Johnson*, 437 F.3d at 899: 'like a partnership, an LLC is a citizen of every state of which its owners/members are citizens.' An individual is a citizen of the state of his or her domicile. A single non-diverse member destroys complete diversity.
