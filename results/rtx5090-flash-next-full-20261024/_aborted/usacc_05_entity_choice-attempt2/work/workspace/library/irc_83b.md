# IRC §83(b) election

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §83(b); Treas. Reg. §1.83-2  
*Status in this library:* in force

A person who performs services in connection with which property is transferred may elect to include in gross income the excess of the fair market value at transfer over the amount paid, **by filing the election not later than 30 days after the date of transfer**. No extension is available. Absent the election, income is recognized as the property vests, at the value at each vesting date.
