# Form 2553 — when to file

*Citation:* IRS Form 2553 Instructions  
*Status in this library:* in force

To be effective for a tax year, file no more than 2 months and 15 days after the beginning of that tax year, or at any time during the preceding year. Late-election relief under Rev. Proc. 2013-30 requires reasonable cause and that all shareholders were eligible for the entire period.
