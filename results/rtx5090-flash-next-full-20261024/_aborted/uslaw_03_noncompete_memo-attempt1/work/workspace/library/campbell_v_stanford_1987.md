# Campbell v. Board of Trustees of Leland Stanford Junior University

*Citation:* 817 F.2d 499 (9th Cir. 1987)  
*Status in this library:* superseded — see Edwards (2008)

Ninth Circuit applied a 'narrow restraint' exception under which a restraint barring an employee from only a small or limited part of the business, trade or profession does not violate §16600. **Status: this reasoning was rejected by the California Supreme Court in *Edwards v. Arthur Andersen* (2008); the narrow-restraint exception is not California law.**
