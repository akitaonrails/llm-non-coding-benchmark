# Ryan LLC v. Federal Trade Commission

*Citation:* 739 F. Supp. 3d 496 (N.D. Tex. 2024)  
*Status in this library:* in force

The court held the FTC lacked substantive rulemaking authority under §6(g) of the FTC Act to promulgate the Non-Compete Rule and that the Rule was arbitrary and capricious; it set the Rule aside with nationwide effect under the APA. The FTC's appeal to the Fifth Circuit was voluntarily dismissed in September 2025.
