# Advanced Bionics Corp. v. Medtronic, Inc.

*Citation:* 29 Cal.4th 697 (2002)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Addressed parallel California and Minnesota litigation over a non-compete; the Court held California courts should generally not enjoin a party from pursuing litigation in another state's courts, reversing a TRO — cautioning about interstate forum conflicts in non-compete disputes. Does not retreat from §16600's policy.
