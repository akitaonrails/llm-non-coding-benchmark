# Light v. Centel Cellular Co. of Texas

*Citation:* 883 S.W.2d 642 (Tex. 1994)  
*Status in this library:* modified — see Sheshunoff (2006)

Held that a covenant not to compete must be ancillary to an otherwise enforceable agreement and set out a two-part test requiring that the consideration be given, and the covenant be designed to enforce the employee's return promise, **at the time the agreement is made**. **Status: the timing requirement was overruled/modified by *Sheshunoff* (2006); *Light* should not be cited for the proposition that a unilateral at-will agreement cannot support a covenant.**
