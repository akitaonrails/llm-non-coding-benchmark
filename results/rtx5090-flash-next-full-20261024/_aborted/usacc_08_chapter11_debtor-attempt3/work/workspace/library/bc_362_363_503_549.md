# Bankruptcy Code — automatic stay, use of property, administrative expenses, post-petition transfers

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §§362(a), 363(b)-(c), 503(b), 549(a)  
*Status in this library:* in force

§549(a): the trustee (debtor in possession) may avoid a transfer of property of the estate that occurs after the commencement of the case and is not authorized by the Code or by the court. **Payment of a pre-petition unsecured claim outside the ordinary course and without a court order is an unauthorized post-petition transfer** (and may be recovered from the recipient). §503(b)(1): actual, necessary costs of preserving the estate after the petition (post-petition goods and services) are administrative expenses payable in the ordinary course. §363(c)(1): ordinary-course transactions need no court approval; §363(b): non-ordinary-course use requires notice and hearing.
