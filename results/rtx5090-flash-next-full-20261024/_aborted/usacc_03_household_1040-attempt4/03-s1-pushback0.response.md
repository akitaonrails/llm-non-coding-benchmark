## Why $16,500 is suspended

The rule — [`form7203_instructions_excerpt.md`] — is worded categorically:

> A shareholder's share of losses… is **allowed only to the extent of the shareholder's adjusted stock basis plus debt basis** (IRC §1366(d)). Losses in excess of basis are **suspended and carried forward** indefinitely until basis is restored. **A guarantee of corporate debt does not create debt basis; only a direct loan from the shareholder to the corporation does.**

A guarantee only matters if the guarantor actually *pays the lender and lends through to the corporation* — and even the guarantor here is Pike, not Marcus. Counting "Pike's guarantee or something" is exactly what the library forbids.
