# Delaware statute of limitations — 3 years; tolling

*Citation:* 10 Del. C. §8106; *Wal-Mart v. AIG Life*, 860 A.2d 312 (Del. 2004) (tolling doctrines); laches in Chancery  
*Status in this library:* in force

Contract and fiduciary claims: **three years** from accrual (Chancery applies the statute by analogy via laches). Tolling: (1) inherently unknowable injury; (2) fraudulent concealment (affirmative acts of concealment); (3) equitable tolling where a fiduciary relationship exists and the plaintiff reasonably relied — **equitable tolling under the fiduciary rationale is weaker where fiduciary duties are contractually eliminated**, but a contractual duty to disclose (§5.4's 30-day notice) that was breached supports concealment-based tolling as to the undisclosed fees.
