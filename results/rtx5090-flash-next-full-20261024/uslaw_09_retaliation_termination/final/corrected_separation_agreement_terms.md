# Corrected separation agreement terms

For **any future** agreement with Dana Whitcomb (and as a fix to the company form).
All cited requirements come from the reference library; items outside the library are labeled.

## 1. ADEA waiver — mandatory changes (29 U.S.C. § 626(f)(1), library: `adea_owbpa_626f.md`)

| Term | Company form today | Required | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consideration period | 7 days | **at least 21 days** — §626(f)(1)(F). Client confirmed an individual termination ("Just her. No RIF"), so the 45-day exit-incentive/group-program rule is **not triggered** and 21 days is the operative minimum | −14 days |
| Revocation period | 3 days after signing | **at least 7 days** after execution — §626(f)(1)(G); agreement **not effective until the revocation period expires** | −4 days |
| Attorney advice | absent | Must **advise the individual in writing to consult with an attorney** — §626(f)(1)(E) | missing |
| Scope of release | "release of all claims" | Must **not waive rights or claims arising after the date the agreement is signed** — §626(f)(1)(C) | must be expressly carved out |
| ADEA specificity | form names "the ADEA" | Keep: waiver must specifically refer to ADEA rights/claims — §626(f)(1)(B) | OK as drafted — retain |
| Understandability | OK | Keep: written in a manner calculated to be understood — §626(f)(1)(A) | OK as drafted — retain |
| Consideration | 4 weeks' base pay | Must be consideration **in addition to anything of value the employee is already entitled to** — §626(f)(1)(D). 4 weeks qualifies in kind **provided she has no pre-existing severance entitlement — no severance policy appears in the file; confirm (UNVERIFIED)**; see §3 below on adequacy | retain, reassess amount |

**Why this matters now:** an ADEA release that fails any of these is unenforceable as to
ADEA claims, and the employee **may keep the severance and still sue** (*Oubre v. Entergy*,
522 U.S. 422 (1998), as summarized in `adea_owbpa_626f.md`). The current form buys no ADEA
protection at all.

## 2. Timing and subject-matter limits

3. **Do not present any agreement while the FMLA request is pending unanswered or the
   harassment complaint is uninvestigated.** A release signed in that posture becomes
   evidence of the retaliation/interference case, whatever its terms. (Reasoning based on
   `fmla_2615.md` and `harassment_investigation_duty.md`.)
4. **FMLA claims:** the library contains no authority on whether FMLA claims can be waived
   by private agreement (as opposed to with DOL involvement). Treat the FMLA release clause
   as of **doubtful validity — UNVERIFIED in this library.** Do not assume the release bars
   FMLA claims.
5. **Effectiveness date:** because of §626(f)(1)(G), no payment-for-release structure should
   treat the agreement as effective before the 7-day revocation window closes.

## 3. Commercial terms (not legal requirements from the library)

6. Four weeks' severance against claims carrying lost wages, **liquidated (double) damages**,
   fees, reinstatement (`fmla_2615.md`) and **uncapped compensatory damages** under Columbia
   law (`columbia_at_will_exceptions.md`) is not a plausible bargain. If the company wants a
   real release, the consideration must reflect that exposure (salary data requested in
   `QUESTIONS.md`).
7. Keep the mutual non-disparagement clause if desired; it is not a defect, but it will be
   read against the backdrop of an uninvestigated complaint.

## 4. Minimum corrected clause set (for counsel to redraft)

- Consideration period: **≥ 21 days** from delivery — confirmed individual termination
  (client, 2026-10-06: "Just her. No RIF"), so no 45-day group-program trigger.
- Revocation: **≥ 7 days** after signing; agreement effective only on expiry.
- Sentence: "You are advised to consult with an attorney before signing this Agreement."
- Release scope: claims **through the date of signing only**; expressly excludes claims
  arising after signing; expressly refers to the ADEA.
- Severance conditioned on effectiveness after revocation; separate any amounts the employee
  is already owed (final pay, accrued PTO) from release consideration — §626(f)(1)(D).
