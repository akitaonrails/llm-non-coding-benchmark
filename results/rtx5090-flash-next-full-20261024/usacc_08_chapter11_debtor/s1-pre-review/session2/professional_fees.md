# Professional fees — what may be paid to each professional, and when

Authority in this memo is limited to the reference library (`library/INDEX.md`): 11 U.S.C. §§330(a), 331
(`bc_330_331.md`); §§362(a), 363(b)-(c), 503(b), 549(a) (`bc_362_363_503_549.md`). Anything outside the
library is labeled **unverified**.

## The rule that governs everyone

Professionals employed under §327 are paid **only upon application and court award (§330(a))** or **under
interim compensation procedures ordered by the court (§331)**. Payments outside those procedures are
**subject to disgorgement** — and a payment not authorized by the Code or the Court is an avoidable
post-petition transfer under **§549(a)**, recoverable from the professional. So the question is never "can
we pay them" but "has an application been awarded, and in what amount."
