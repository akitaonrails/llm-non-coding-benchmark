# Bankruptcy Code §507(a)(4) — wage priority

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §507(a)(4)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Allowed unsecured claims for wages, salaries or commissions earned within 180 days before the petition, up to $17,150 per individual (as adjusted), have fourth priority. Payment of pre-petition wages during the case requires a court order (first-day wage motion).
