# Professional fees — what may be paid to each professional, and when (updated for Dkt. 77)

Authority in this memo is limited to the reference library (`library/INDEX.md`): 11 U.S.C. §§330(a), 331
(`bc_330_331.md`); §§362(a), 363(b)-(c), 503(b), 549(a) (`bc_362_363_503_549.md`). Anything outside the
library is labeled **unverified**. Case orders: Dkt. 77 (interim compensation procedures, entered
4/14/2026), Dkt. 61 (DIP), Dkt. 83 (Mesa post-petition trade agreement).

## The rule that governs everyone

Professionals employed under §327 are paid **only upon application and court award (§330(a))** or **under
interim compensation procedures ordered by the court (§331)**. Payments outside those procedures are
**subject to disgorgement** — and a payment not authorized by the Code or the Court is an avoidable
post-petition transfer under **§549(a)**, recoverable from the professional.

## What Dkt. 77 actually provides (entered 4/14/2026 — now in the file, superseding our flagged assumption)

1. Retained professionals may **serve monthly fee statements**.
2. **Absent objection within 14 days**, the Debtor **may pay 80% of fees and 100% of expenses**.
3. The **20% holdback is paid only upon Court approval of an interim or final fee application**.
4. **No payment may be made for any period before the professional's retention date**
   (Holland & Pierce retained nunc pro tunc to **3/10**; Corbin retained effective **3/20**).

Key point: Dkt. 77 is a **procedures order, not a §330(a) fee award**. The no-objection mechanism permits
interim payment; it does not finally allow the fees. Everything remains subject to final review and
disgorgement (§§330(a), 331).
