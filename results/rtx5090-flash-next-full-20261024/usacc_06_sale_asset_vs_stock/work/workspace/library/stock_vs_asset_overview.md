# Stock vs asset sale — tax and liability overview

*Citation:* Practitioner summary  
*Status in this library:* in force

Stock (membership-interest) sale: seller recognizes capital gain on the difference between price and stock basis; buyer takes carryover basis in assets (no step-up unless a §338(h)(10)/§336(e) election, which converts the deal to a deemed asset sale with asset-sale consequences to the seller); buyer inherits all liabilities. Asset sale: gain computed asset by asset under the allocation; recapture and covenant payments are ordinary; buyer gets a basis step-up; S corporations within the §1374 recognition period owe entity-level built-in gains tax. Price differences between the two structures typically reflect the buyer's step-up value and assumed liabilities.
