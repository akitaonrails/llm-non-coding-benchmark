# IRC §197 and covenants not to compete

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §197(d)(1)(E); Rev. Rul. 69-643; *Muskat v. United States*, 554 F.3d 183 (1st Cir. 2009)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A covenant not to compete entered into in connection with the acquisition of a trade or business is a section 197 intangible amortizable by the buyer over 15 years. **To the seller, payments for a covenant not to compete are ordinary income** (compensation for refraining from competing), not capital gain. Allocation between a covenant and goodwill must reflect economic reality; the parties' written allocation is binding on them (§1060(a); *Danielson* rule) absent strong proof, and the IRS may challenge it.
