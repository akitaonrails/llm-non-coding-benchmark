**1. "Basta pedir a exclusão no portal em dezembro?" — The portal request exists, but it doesn't do what he thinks.**
The effect is fixed by law, not by the request date (LC 123/2006, art. 16 — `library/lc123_sublimite_opcao.md`):
- Simples enrollment is **irrevocable for the whole calendar year** (§2º);
- exclusion communicated after the last business day of January takes effect **only from January 1 of the next year** (§2º-A).

So a December 2026 portal request = leaving on **01/01/2027**. December's DAS is due regardless. The only retroactive exit in the system (forced exclusion for exceeding the ceiling, art. 26) is **not in the library** and doesn't apply anyway — they're at 4.2M, below 4.8M. And even if a December switch were possible, it would *cost more*: December under Simples = **R$ 75.7k** vs **R$ 90.1k** under Real.

**2. "Open another Lucro Real company and bill December through it?" — Worse on the math, and legally off-limits.**
- **Math:** December ≈ R$ 350k (85% consulting / 15% works). Taxed under Simples: R$ 75.7k (21.6%). Under Real, even copying the existing cost structure: R$ 90.1k (25.7%) — a brand-new company has near-zero PIS/COFINS credits (no purchases/depreciation of its own), pushing it toward ~R$ 97k: **+R$ 14–21k for one month**. And the ISS doesn't disappear: Vértice's RBT12 without December is still 3.85M, **above the 3.6M sublimite** (art. 13-A), and the new company pays ISS too.
- **Legality:** billing the same activity/contracts/employees of the Simples company through a new company of the same partners, purely to flip regimes mid-year, is textbook dissimulation — not something I can advise. Honest caveat: the library's LC 123 file cites arts. 3º §9º and 20 but **doesn't reproduce their text**, so I cannot ground the anti-abuse analysis in the library (flagged as **U6**); the professional recommendation stands regardless: don't do it. A genuinely new business in a new company would be a different conversation — "billing Vértice's December" is not that.

**Bottom line unchanged:** do nothing in December; the only valid act would be an exclusion notice by **29/01/2027** (effects from 01/01/2027) — and the numbers say don't do even that.
