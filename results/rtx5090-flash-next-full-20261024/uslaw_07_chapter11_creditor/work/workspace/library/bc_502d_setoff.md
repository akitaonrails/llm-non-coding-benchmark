# 11 U.S.C. §502(d); setoff and recoupment

*Citation:* 11 U.S.C. §502(d); §553  
*Status in this library:* in force

§502(d): the court shall disallow any claim of an entity from which property is recoverable under §547 (preference) unless the entity has paid the amount for which it is liable. A creditor with preference exposure may find its claim disallowed until it returns the preference. Setoff of pre-petition mutual debts requires relief from stay.
