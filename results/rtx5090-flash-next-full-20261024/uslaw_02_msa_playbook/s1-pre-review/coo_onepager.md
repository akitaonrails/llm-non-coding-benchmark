# RouteWise MSA — COO one-pager

**Deal:** RouteWise's routing/fleet-optimization platform (SaaS). 36-month initial term, up to 350 vehicles, $18,500/month billed annually in advance ($222,000/yr; $666,000 over the initial term) plus a one-time $25,000 implementation fee. Auto-renews afterwards. Our EU driver and customer data will run through it. **The paper is not signable this week as-is**, but the fixes are ordinary — none of them require RouteWise to change its product or pricing model, only its boilerplate.

## What must change before signature

1. **Their IP-infringement promise is a mirage.** The contract *says* RouteWise's infringement indemnity is "uncapped," but another clause caps *all* liability at 12 months of fees (~$222,000) "notwithstanding anything to the contrary" — and under standard interpretation rules that cap wins. If a patent troll sues over the routing platform, we'd absorb everything above ~$222K. **Fix:** one sentence carving the indemnity out of the cap. *(Most dangerous clause in the deal because it looks compliant.)*

2. **The privacy contract is missing.** The data processing addendum (DPA) covering EU driver data is "[To be attached]" — there is nothing to attach and nothing to sign. Under GDPR rules, *we* (not RouteWise) are in breach from the first day driver data flows without it. **Fix:** attach and sign a DPA with the MSA. We must supply our DPA form; Legal will drive this in parallel with the redlines.

3. **They can raise our price mid-contract — we can't stop it.** The order form lets RouteWise raise fees once during the 36-month term (on 60 days' notice) and at every renewal by the greater of 8% or inflation. A mid-term 8% hike costs $1,480/month — up to ~$48,800 in the initial term depending on timing — and every renewal year at least $17,760. **Fix:** price locked for the initial term; renewals capped at the *lesser* of 5% or inflation, 90 days' notice.

4. **A missed calendar date can lock in two more years at the raised price.** Auto-renewal comes in **24-month** jumps and requires notice **90 days** out. Wipe that miss against the price hike: two renewal years at the increased rate ≈ **$479,500 committed**, versus ≈ $233,100 under our standard 12-month/5%-cap terms — a ~$246,000 swing from one missed deadline. **Fix:** renewals in 12-month (or monthly) terms with 30 days' notice — and put the renewal date in the calendar either way.

5. **Our own affiliates aren't licensed.** The license covers Cobalt Logistics Holdings only; Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. and Cobalt Brokerage LLC would have to pay extra — or be using the system without a license while they handle the EU driver data from item 2. **Fix:** name both affiliates in the license at no extra fee.

## Worth asking for (not blockers)

- **SLA exit ramp:** service credits are our *only* remedy for downtime, capped at 25% of a month's fee (~$4,600). Ask for a right to walk away from an Order Form if availability misses target in any 3 months out of 6.
- Data export window is 30 days (within policy); 60 is our preference.
- The 12-month mutual liability cap, gross-negligence carve-outs, Delaware law, and 99.5% SLA target are all within our playbook — no fight needed there.

## Bottom line

Send RouteWise the five redlines (Legal has them drafted). Expect these to be routine asks for their sales desk. **Do not sign, and do not let any EU driver data go live, until (a) the five redlines are in and (b) the DPA is signed and attached.** If RouteWise refuses the uncapped-indemnity fix, that needs GC sign-off to waive — a decision for you and Priya together, not for the deal clock.
