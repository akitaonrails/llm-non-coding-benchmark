Cobalt Logistics — Vendor Contract Playbook (Legal, rev. 2026-03)
Applies to all SaaS/technology vendor agreements above $100k/year. Hard rules require GC sign-off to waive.

A. Hard rules ("never accept without GC approval")
A1. Uncapped IP infringement indemnity from the vendor, with no carve-back through the limitation-ofliability clause. The indemnity must survive and must not be subject to the liability cap. A2. Data processing
addendum executed at signing whenever personal data of EU/UK residents is processed (Cobalt processes EU
customer and driver data for its Rotterdam and Hamburg operations). "To be attached" is not acceptable. A3. No
unilateral price increases during an initial term. Renewal increases capped at 5% or CPI, whichever is lower,
with 90 days' notice. A4. Auto-renewal terms no longer than 12 months, with non-renewal notice of 30 days or
less. A5. Affiliates (Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. and Cobalt Brokerage LLC) must be covered by the license at no
additional fee, or the agreement must be signed by Holdings on behalf of affiliates.

B. Preferred positions (negotiate; may concede with Legal approval)
B1. Mutual limitation of liability at 12–24 months' fees is acceptable. Carve-outs for gross negligence, willful
misconduct and fraud are our preferred position and should be accepted when offered (they cut both ways
and protect Cobalt). B2. Data export window: 60 days preferred; 30 days acceptable. B3. Governing law: Delaware
or New York acceptable; jury waiver acceptable. B4. Suspension for non-payment: at least 30 days' notice
preferred; 15 acceptable if cure is allowed. B5. SLA: 99.5% with credits is acceptable; credits-as-sole-remedy
acceptable if termination right exists for chronic failure (3 months in any 6).

C. Drafting notes
C1. Always read the order form against the MSA: order forms often override the MSA (check the precedence
clause). C2. Check that "notwithstanding" clauses do not swallow protections elsewhere in the document.
