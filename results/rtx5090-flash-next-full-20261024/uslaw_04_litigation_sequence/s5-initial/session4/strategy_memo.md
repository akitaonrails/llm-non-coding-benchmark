## 2. Why the improvement is real, not cosmetic

1. **An adversary's written concession is better evidence than an argument.** Their Sept. 29 letter, ¶3, labels J-4411/J-4452/J-4490/J-4571 the "undisputed" invoices. Combined with §2's deemed-accepted clause and account stated (CJI 14.02; UCC §1-303 by analogy — `columbia_account_stated_waiver.md`), the AAA claim on $265,200.00 + interest is now a documents-only partial-award motion, not a contested dispute.
2. **The hostage condition creates their downside, not ours.** Payment of admitted debt "upon resolution of the conversion-fee issue" has no contractual basis. In arbitration that is an unexcused holdback, and C.3 ("prevailing party … reasonable attorneys' fees and costs," enforced as written per *Zuniga v. Mercer*, 198 Col. 2d 77 (2017)) points at **them** on that issue. Interest keeps compounding at $2,652.00/month in the meantime.
3. **Their fee trap only worked on a complaint we never filed.** The draft Rule 12(b)(6) (party defense; consent; justification via the corporate directive, their Ex. 2) plus ¶4's "we assume you have reviewed the full contract" shows exactly what awaited a Superior Court filing: §§3–4 FAA stay/compel, then a C.3 fee award for their motion (library `faa_2_4.md` says a party that sues on arbitrable claims "may owe the fees of the motion to compel"). Session 1 steered away from that; sessions 2–3 confirmed the steering was right.
4. **Forum risk is now closed on our side too.** The CEO's Oct. 6 admission ("we never looked at the exhibits … off a template") forecloses any romantic argument about escaping Exhibit C — FAA §2's revocation savings clause doesn't cover failing to read a signed contract (general principle, labeled unverified; §2 itself is the library citation). We will not spend a dollar on forum.
