## 2. Scope of the Section 3 waiver

Section 3 permits waiver only "in writing." The only writing ever issued is the February 11 email, and it names exactly two individuals and describes the waiver as a "one-off." As you correctly note, whether the waiver reached further is "a contract dispute." It resolved nothing: Marcus Bell's subsequent text replies were not writings, contained no waiver, and Juniper's account manager confirms he agreed to no waiver beyond Rosa and Tay. The five conversion fees therefore remain due.

Your letter states that Pinecrest "will pay the undisputed balance of the remaining invoices upon resolution of the conversion-fee issue." There is no basis in the Agreement for that condition. Under Section 2, invoices J-4411, J-4452, J-4490, and J-4571 were not disputed within 15 days of receipt and are "deemed accepted"; they were due no later than August 31, 2026. Payment of a debt admitted to be undisputed is not contingent on the resolution of a separate dispute, and your letter's concession that these invoices are "undisputed" is noted for the record. Interest continues to run at 1% per month — **$2,652.00 per month**, accruing to **$11,499.50** for the period beginning October 31, 2026. Continued withholding after this letter will be presented to the arbitrator as an unexcused holdback, with a request for fees and costs under Exhibit C.3, which applies to the prevailing party in any arbitration.
