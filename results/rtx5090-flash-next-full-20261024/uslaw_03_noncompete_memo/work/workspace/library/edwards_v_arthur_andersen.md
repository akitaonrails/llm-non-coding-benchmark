# Edwards v. Arthur Andersen LLP

*Citation:* 44 Cal.4th 937 (2008)  
*Status in this library:* in force

The California Supreme Court held that §16600 voids non-competition agreements in the employment context unless they fall within a statutory exception, **rejecting the Ninth Circuit's 'narrow restraint' exception** (which had upheld restraints that barred only a small part of a profession). 'Noncompetition agreements are invalid under section 16600 in California even if narrowly drawn, unless they fall within the applicable statutory exceptions.' The court disapproved *Campbell v. Board of Trustees* and *General Commercial Packaging* to the extent inconsistent.
