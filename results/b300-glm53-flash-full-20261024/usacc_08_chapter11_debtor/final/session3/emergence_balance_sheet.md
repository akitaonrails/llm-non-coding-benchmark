# Emergence balance sheet — presentation of the discharged debt and the gain

**Redwood Fabrication, Inc. — Case No. 26-11842 (Bankr. D. Colo.).** Plan confirmed 9/2/2026
(Dkt. 214); effective date 9/30/2026. Fresh-start reporting is **not** adopted (ASC 852 prong (b)
fails — old equity retains 60%, ≥ 50%; see `fresh_start.md`).

## What no-fresh-start means for this statement

- Assets remain at their **carrying amounts**: fresh-start restatement is not adopted, so there is
  no restatement at reorganization value and no goodwill (the library's fresh-start mechanics are
  not reached; its failed-test prescription covers only the compromised liabilities and the
  gain). The Corbin reorganization value ($14,000,000) stays disclosure only.
- The old-equity 60% / DIP-lender 40% allocation is disclosure only; no equity remeasurement.
- Pre-petition and post-petition liabilities are **not netted** (ASC 852-10-45-4 to 45-6,
  library: `asc852_fresh_start.md`), even though the plan settles the allowed unsecured pool
  under one GUC treatment.
- The only accounting displacement is the settlement of the liabilities subject to compromise —
  and the gain goes to the **income statement**, not equity.

- **Plan payables at present value.** The library rule: compromised liabilities "are stated at

  "pending Q13; PV will be lower."
  When Q13 arrives: plan payables ↓, gain ↑, still within reorganization items. Current vs.
  long-term classification of the plan payables also needs the Q13 schedule.
- **Gain presentation.** "Gain on discharge of indebtedness" inside the income statement as a
  **reorganization item** (library: asc852_fresh_start.md — "the gain on discharge is reported

  paid-in capital or equity. Interim
  figure $5,460,000 = $8,400,000 − $2,940,000 (undiscounted; final = $8,400,000 − PV).

## Tax footnote

The book gain of $5,460,000 (interim) has **no current taxable-income counterpart**: COD income
of $5,460,000 is excluded under 26 U.S.C. §108(a)(1)(A) and applied to tax attributes under
§108(b)(1) — NOL $6,100,000 → $640,000 (library: irc_108.md; `cod_schedule.json`). Deferred taxes on the gain and the
effect of the NOL reduction (a smaller NOL deferred tax asset) cannot be quantified without tax
rates — **Q14**; the library is silent on rates.
