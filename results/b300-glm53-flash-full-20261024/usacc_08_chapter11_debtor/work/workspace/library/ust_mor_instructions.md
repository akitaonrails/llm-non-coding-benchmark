# US Trustee Form 11-MOR — instructions (excerpt)

*Citation:* UST Form 11-MOR (2021)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Part 1 cash receipts and disbursements (all estate accounts); Part 2 asset and liability status, separately stating post-petition payables (aging) and pre-petition liabilities subject to compromise; Part 3 assets sold/transferred; Part 4 income statement; Part 5 professional fees (approved, paid, held back); Part 6 post-petition taxes; Part 7 questionnaire including: 'Were any payments made on pre-petition debt?' (Yes/No, with docket reference) and 'Were any payments made outside the ordinary course of business without court approval?'. Signed under penalty of perjury.
