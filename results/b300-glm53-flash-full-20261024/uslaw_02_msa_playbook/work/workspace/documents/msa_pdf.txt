MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT
Between RouteWise Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider") and Cobalt Logistics Holdings LLC
("Customer"). Effective Date: the date of the last signature below. Version 7.3 (Provider form).

1. Definitions
1.1 "Services" means Provider's RouteWise routing and fleet-optimization platform, as described in an Order Form.
1.2 "Customer Data" means data submitted by Customer or its Users to the Services, including personal data of
Customer's drivers and customers. 1.3 "Affiliate" means an entity controlling, controlled by or under common
control with a party. 1.4 "Documentation" means Provider's then-current online user guides. 1.5 "Order Form"
means an ordering document executed by the parties. 1.6 "Users" means Customer's employees and contractors
authorized to use the Services.

2. Services and Access
2.1 Provider shall make the Services available to Customer and its Users during the Subscription Term in
accordance with the Documentation and the Service Level Agreement attached as Exhibit B. 2.2 The license
granted hereunder is to Customer only and does not extend to Customer's Affiliates unless named on an Order
Form at additional fees. 2.3 Provider may modify the Services from time to time provided such modifications do
not materially degrade functionality.

3. Term and Renewal
3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been
terminated. 3.2 Each Subscription Term is stated in the Order Form. 3.3 Each Subscription Term shall
automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twenty-four (24) months unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

4. Fees and Payment
4.1 Customer shall pay the fees stated in each Order Form. 4.2 Fees are due net thirty (30) days from invoice. 4.3
Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month. 4.4 Fees for renewal terms are as stated in the applicable
Order Form. 4.5 All fees are exclusive of taxes.

5. Customer Data and Data Protection
5.1 Customer owns Customer Data. 5.2 Provider shall maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical
and technical safeguards. 5.3 To the extent Provider processes personal data subject to the GDPR or UK
GDPR on Customer's behalf, the parties shall enter into a data processing addendum in the form to be
attached as Exhibit C. 5.4 Provider may use aggregated, de-identified usage data for any purpose.

6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and use it only to
perform under this Agreement. 6.2 Obligations survive for five (5) years after termination; trade secrets survive as
long as they remain trade secrets.

DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, HOWEVER CAUSED. 9.3 The exclusions and limitations in this Section 9

10. Indemnification
10.1 Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe a
patent, copyright or trademark or misappropriate a trade secret, and shall pay all damages, costs and
attorneys' fees finally awarded or agreed in settlement; Provider's obligations under this Section 10.1 are
uncapped. 10.2 Customer shall defend Provider against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or
Customer's breach of Section 11. 10.3 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice and reasonable cooperation;
the indemnifying party controls the defense.

11. Acceptable Use
11.1 Customer shall not reverse engineer, resell, or use the Services to build a competing product. 11.2 Customer is
responsible for its Users' compliance.

12. Termination
12.1 Either party may terminate for material breach uncured within thirty (30) days of notice. 12.2 Upon
termination Customer may export Customer Data for thirty (30) days, after which Provider may delete it. 12.3
Sections 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13 and 14 survive.

13. Governing Law; Disputes
13.1 Delaware law governs, excluding conflict-of-laws rules. 13.2 Exclusive venue in the state and federal courts in
Wilmington, Delaware. 13.3 Each party waives jury trial.
