# Texas dental practice — ownership and fee-splitting (excerpt)

*Citation:* Tex. Occ. Code §251.003; 22 TAC §108.70  
*Status in this library:* in force

Payments by a dental practice to an unlicensed third party computed as a share of fees, or for 'consulting' with no deliverables, may constitute unlawful fee splitting or an unlicensed-ownership arrangement. (Context for why 'consulting' payments to a relative are sensitive beyond theft.)
