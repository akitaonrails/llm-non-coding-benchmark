# Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6) / Columbia Civ. R. 12(b)(6)

*Citation:* Rule 12(b)(6); *Ashcroft v. Iqbal*, 556 U.S. 662 (2009)  
*Status in this library:* in force

A complaint must state a claim that is plausible on its face; documents referenced in the complaint may be considered. A tortious-interference claim fails where the complaint and incorporated documents show consent or justification.
