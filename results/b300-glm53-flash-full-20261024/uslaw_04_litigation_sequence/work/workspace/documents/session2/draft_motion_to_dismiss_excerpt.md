# DRAFT — DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO DISMISS COUNT III (TORTIOUS INTERFERENCE) UNDER RULE 12(b)(6)
…A party to a contract cannot be liable for tortiously interfering with its own contract (*Ashby v. Caldera Partners*, 212 Col. App. 3d 440 (2019)). Nor can a claim lie for interference with Juniper's at-will relationships with its own nurses where Pinecrest acted with Juniper's express consent (Feb. 11 email, Ex. 1) and for a legitimate business purpose (corporate directive to reduce agency spend, Ex. 2) — justification is a complete defense (*Restatement (Second) of Torts §§766, 767, 773*)…
