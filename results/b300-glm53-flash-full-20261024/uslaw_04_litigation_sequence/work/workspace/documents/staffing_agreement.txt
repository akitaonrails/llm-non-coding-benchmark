STAFFING SERVICES AGREEMENT
Juniper Health Staffing, LLC ("Juniper") and Pinecrest Senior Living, Inc. ("Client") · Effective January 15,
2024

1. Services. Juniper will provide licensed nurses and CNAs ("Personnel") on a temporary basis at Client's facilities
per written staffing requests. 2. Fees; Payment. Client shall pay the hourly bill rates in Schedule 1 within 30 days
of invoice. Late amounts bear interest at 1% per month. Client shall notify Juniper of any invoice dispute within 15
days of receipt; invoices not disputed within that period are deemed accepted. 3. Conversion; Non-Solicitation.
Client shall not, during the term and for 12 months thereafter, directly or indirectly hire or engage any Personnel
introduced by Juniper, except upon payment of a conversion fee equal to 25% of the Personnel's first-year
annualized compensation. Juniper may waive the conversion fee in writing in its discretion. 4. Term;
Termination. One year, auto-renewing; either party may terminate on 60 days' notice. 5. Insurance; Compliance.
[standard] 6. Limitation of Liability. Neither party is liable for consequential damages; Client's liability for unpaid
fees and conversion fees is not limited. 7. Entire Agreement. This Agreement, Schedule 1 and Exhibits A–C
constitute the entire agreement. 8. Governing Law. State of Columbia.

Signed: Juniper — D. Okonkwo, CEO · Pinecrest — H. Lindqvist, Administrator
