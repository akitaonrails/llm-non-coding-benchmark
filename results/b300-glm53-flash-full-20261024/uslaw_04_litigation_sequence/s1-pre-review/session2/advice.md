Can we work something out?" — "totally fine … Just keep the RN orders coming :)") establishes a *pattern* that Pinecrest will try to stretch into implied waiver for Priya/Luis/Keisha. If the arbitrator reads the texts differently, worst-case on the conversion claim is five hires → **two clean hires (Anne, Victor)**, ≈ $32,000 at the (still unverified) $64,000 base rather than $80,000.
4. **To the CEO, plainly:** the 2/11 email shrinks the claim from $112,000 to at most ~$80,000 and possibly less. This will not change at settlement unless Pinecrest pays more to buy certainty. Marcus's phrasing "I didn't think it was a big deal" is exactly how an arbitrator will feel about conversion-fee disputes generally — expect to recover less than list on the conversions.
