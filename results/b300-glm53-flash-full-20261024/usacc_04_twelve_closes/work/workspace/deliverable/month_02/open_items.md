## 1. NEW — Pinecone Dental Group "Implementation & license — one-time" $36,000.00 (INV-2250): held in deferred revenue, no February recognition
- **Facts on file:** order form C-025 (2025-02-01) contains a **single combined line**, "Implementation & license — one-time", **$36,000.00**; scope of work is "platform deployment and user onboarding for up to 40 seats"; **payment due on signature**; "service start date" 2025-02-01; governed by Master Subscription Agreement v3.2. Invoiced 02/01, collected by wire 02/15 (agreed to bank). A contract exists — approval, identifiable rights, identifiable payment terms, commercial substance, and probable collection are all satisfied (`library/asc606_excerpts.md`, 606-10-25-1) — and payment was in fact received.
- **Why nothing is recognized in February — the facts fall short of the conditions, not the arithmetic:**
  1. Policy memo §2 recognizes implementation revenue **only** where a **separately priced, distinct** service is **actually delivered**. The fee is one combined price — implementation is **not separately priced** — and the file contains **no delivery, completion, or acceptance evidence**.
  2. Activities to set up a contract that do not transfer a good or service to the customer are not performance obligations (`library/asc606_excerpts.md`, 606-10-25-17); "platform deployment and user onboarding" is set-up to the extent it is not separately priced and distinct.
  3. The order form states **no license term or access period** ("one-time"), so no ratable release schedule is computable from the document. **The library contains no allocation guidance either** — there is no standalone-selling-price or transaction-price-allocation material in `library/asc606_excerpts.md` or `library/revenue_policy_memo.md` with which to split the combined price across performance obligations. That absence is stated plainly; no allocation has been used or invented.
  4. Amounts billed **before** performance are **contract liabilities** (`library/asc606_excerpts.md`, 606-10-45-2) — the same authority that governs the Trestle annuals.
- **Treatment in the books:** billed Dr 1100 / Cr 2200 on 02/01 (JE-2025-015), collected 02/15 (JE-2025-016); carried in `deferred_revenue_rollforward.json` as "Pinecone Dental Group — held, no release" at $36,000.00. It does **not** sit in A/R at month-end, and nothing was charged to account 4000.
- **Questioned to the client (`../QUESTIONS.md` #7):** (a) evidence that the implementation was performed and accepted — and when; (b) the license term or access period the fee covers; (c) whether C-025 is the complete pricing, i.e., any allocation of the combined price the parties intended. If answers arrive: delivery evidence of a distinct, separately performed service completed in February → recognize in February; a stated term → release 1/n; a revealed allocation → applied and disclosed here as a judgment call (§4). Until an answer arrives, recognizing any amount would require inventing a fact or an allocation basis the library does not provide.
- The $24,000.00 unidentified opening deferred (§2.1) is a **separate** item; nothing in the file connects the two, and I have assumed no connection.
