**Direct answer to Paulo: No — "authorized" is not the same as "regular," and this round is already documented in `orientacao.md` (§4, new dedicated block) and logged in `STATUS.md`. The core of the reply:**

What the IT team brought down was the *validation control* (rollback of the issuer version); **the obligation to highlight the IBSCBS group was not removed along with it**. The standard for "regular/irregular" in the apuração-teste (tax assessment) is the rule, not the result of the submission.

Authorization does not result in a fine, but it **does not fulfill the accessory obligation** — and the exemption (ADCT art. 125, §4º) depends on *compliance* (highlight, recordkeeping, apuração-teste), not on the cStat.
