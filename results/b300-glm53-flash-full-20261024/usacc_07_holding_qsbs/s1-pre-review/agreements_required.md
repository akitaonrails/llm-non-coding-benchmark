# Agreements and governance documents required under the revised plan

The revised plan (see `opinion.md`) keeps Services, Digital, and (subject to her choice) Properties inside the holding partnership — with the Helio Grid stock **excluded** — and replaces undocumented loans and commingled cash with documented, AFR-interest arrangements. The documents below are needed to make that work and to protect the legitimacy of the structure. Where the reason traces to the library, the file is cited. **Updated 2026-10-25 to reflect the client's responses** (loan terms confirmed never to have existed; the 1% member identified as her revocable living trust; a Helio Grid sale expected in 12–18 months — see the addendum in `opinion.md`).
