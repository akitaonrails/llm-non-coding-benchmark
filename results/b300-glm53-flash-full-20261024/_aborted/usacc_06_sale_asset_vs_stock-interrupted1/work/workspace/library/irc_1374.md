# IRC §1374 — tax imposed on certain built-in gains

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §1374(a), (b), (d)(7)  
*Status in this library:* in force

(a) If for any taxable year beginning in the **recognition period** an S corporation has a net recognized built-in gain, there is imposed a tax (computed under subsection (b)) on the income of such corporation for such taxable year. (b)(1) The tax is the highest corporate rate (**21%**) on the net recognized built-in gain. (d)(7) The recognition period is the **5-year period beginning with the first day of the first taxable year for which the corporation was an S corporation**. Recognized built-in gain on an asset is limited to the asset's built-in gain at the start of the recognition period. The tax paid is treated as a loss sustained by the S corporation (§1366(f)(2)).
