# Corrected separation agreement terms

**Matter:** Dana Whitcomb termination file — defects in the company's SEPARATION AGREEMENT AND GENERAL RELEASE (form as attached to the 2026-10-06 HR request)
**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Governing authority:** library/adea_owbpa_626f.md (29 U.S.C. §626(f)(1)(A)–(G); *Oubre v. Entergy*); library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md

These are the terms that **must change** for any future agreement. The current form releases Title VII, ADEA, FMLA, and state-law claims, and the employee here is 42; because the release covers ADEA claims and the individual is protected by the ADEA (age 40+), every requirement of §626(f)(1) applies. If the form fails them, the release is **unenforceable as to ADEA claims**, and under *Oubre* (library/adea_owbpa_626f.md) the employee keeps the severance and may still sue — the company would get nothing for the 4 weeks except the illusion of a release.

---

## 1. Term 3 — Severance (4 weeks' base pay) — CONDITIONAL CORRECTION
- **Requirement:** consideration must be "in addition to anything of value to which the individual is already entitled" — §626(f)(1)(D).
- **Change:** add an express statement of what is owed regardless (accrued PTO payout, earned commissions through the date of separation, final wages) drafted **outside** the sentence granting severance, and confirm the 4 weeks is paid purely as release consideration.
- **Open item (flagged unknown):** the client confirmed on 2026-10-25 that it has no information on whether a severance policy or plan entitles her to anything, and no base-pay figure (QUESTIONS.md Q1 — closed as unknown). If the 4 weeks overlaps amounts already owed, element (D) fails; the FMLA 2×-damages pricing also needs base pay.

## 2. Term 4 — Release of claims — REWRITE
Current: *"Release of all claims, including under Title VII, the ADEA, the FMLA, and state law."*
- **(B) Specificity:** the release must specifically refer to rights or claims arising under the ADEA — §626(f)(1)(B). "Including under ... the ADEA" should be replaced with a sentence expressly naming ADEA rights and claims, e.g., "Employee knowingly and voluntarily waives and releases all rights and claims arising under the ADEA ... through the Effective Date."
- **(C) No future-claims waiver:** §626(f)(1)(C) prohibits waiving rights or claims that may arise after execution. Replace "all claims" with "all claims arising or accruing on or before the date Employee signs this Agreement" and carve out future claims expressly.
- **(A) Plain writing:** the whole release should be "written in a manner calculated to be understood" — short sentences, defined Effective Date, no capitalized-medium-prose walls.
- **(E) Written attorney-consultation advice:** add a standalone sentence: "You are advised in writing to consult with an attorney before signing this Agreement." The form contains nothing of the kind today — §626(f)(1)(E) fails as drafted.
- **Unverified flag:** the form also purports to release FMLA claims. This library (library/fmla_2615.md) contains no authority on the validity of FMLA waivers, so no correction can be certified here — treat that clause as unverified and do not rely on it until verified.

## 3. Term 7 — Consideration and revocation — REWRITE (the disqualifying defect)
Current: *"Employee has seven (7) days to consider this Agreement and may revoke within three (3) days after signing."*
Arithmetic: 7 days vs. 21 required = **14 days short**; 3-day revocation vs. 7 required = **4 days short**, and the form has no statement that effectiveness waits out the revocation period.
- **(F) Consideration period:** "You have at least twenty-one (21) days to consider this Agreement" — §626(f)(1)(F). *(45 days applies only if connected to an exit incentive or group termination program — client confirmed 2026-10-25 that this is a single-person termination with no RIF, so the 21-day minimum governs here. For future group use, this library lists the 45-day period but does not include the additional group-program disclosure requirements; those would need separate verification.)*
- **(G) Revocation and effectiveness:** "You may revoke this Agreement by written notice within seven (7) days after you sign it. This Agreement shall not become effective or enforceable until the seven-day revocation period has expired." — §626(f)(1)(G).
- The statute's changes here are mechanical and non-negotiable; timing changes alone do not fix the other defects in §2 above.

## 4. Term 9 — Non-disparagement (mutual) — NO CHANGE REQUIRED ON THIS RECORD
The library contains no authority requiring or restricting mutual non-disparagement terms; no cited defect. (That is a statement about the library's coverage, not about other law.)

## 5. Term 11 — Governing law: State of Columbia — NO CHANGE REQUIRED, BUT NOTE THE EXPOSURE
library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md: Columbia mirrors Title VII/FMLA with a **longer limitations period and uncapped compensatory damages**. Any release understood to cover the state-law claims is therefore buying out the longest-lived, uncapped exposure — pricing and drafting (and the (C) accrual-date limit in §2 above) should be set with that in mind.
