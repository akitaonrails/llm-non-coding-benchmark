# Corrected separation agreement terms (for any future agreement)

**Context:** The company form as provided fails to meet the minimum standards for a knowing and voluntary ADEA waiver in 29 U.S.C. §626(f)(1) (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). Dana Whitcomb is 42, so full OWBPA compliance is mandatory for this population. As drafted, the release would be **unenforceable as to ADEA claims** — the employee could keep the severance and still sue (*Oubre v. Entergy*, quoted in `library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`).

> **Note on the source document:** the draft supplied (`documents/separation_agreement_draft.md`) is an **excerpt** — paragraphs 3, 4, 7, 9, and 11 only. Paragraphs 1–2, 5–6, 8, and 10 were not provided, so items marked ⚠️ must be verified against the full form before the corrected version is finalized.

## Mandatory changes

### 1. Consideration period: 7 days → at least 21 days
Current ¶7: *"Employee has seven (7) days to consider this Agreement."*
Defect: §626(f)(1)(F) requires **at least 21 days** for an individual termination — and **45 days** if the agreement is requested in connection with an exit-incentive or other employment-termination program offered to a group (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). The current 7-day window is less than a third of the statutory minimum (7/21 = 33.3%).

### 2. Revocation period: 3 days → at least 7 days, plus an effective-date clause
Current ¶7: *"may revoke within three (3) days after signing."*
Defect: §626(f)(1)(G) requires revocation rights for **at least 7 days following execution** (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). The draft's 3-day period fails the minimum by 4 days. Add an express clause: **"This Agreement shall not become effective until the revocation period has expired."** The excerpt contains no such clause.

### 3. Add written advice to consult an attorney
Missing from the excerpt. §626(f)(1)(E) requires the agreement to **advise the individual in writing to consult with an attorney** before signing (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). ⚠️ If an advisory clause exists elsewhere in the full form, verify its wording; if not, insert one.

### 4. Limit the release to past claims
Current ¶4: *"Release of all claims…"* with no temporal limit.
Defect: §626(f)(1)(C) — the waiver must **not** cover future claims (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). Redraft to: *"release of claims arising under Title VII, the ADEA, the FMLA, and [Columbia] law **that have accrued as of the date of execution**,"* and expressly preserve statutory rights that cannot be waived. ⚠️ Note a library gap: which non-ADEA rights are non-waivable in Columbia is **not addressed in the library** — treat as unverified and confirm before finalizing.

### 5. Refer to ADEA rights specifically and in plain terms
Current ¶4 names "the ADEA" only inside a generic list. §626(f)(1)(B) requires the waiver to **specifically refer to rights or claims arising under the ADEA**, and §626(f)(1)(A) requires it be written in a **manner calculated to be understood** (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). Redraft with a dedicated ADEA paragraph (plain-language description of the rights being waived, e.g., age-discrimination claims under the ADEA, including §626 claims). ⚠️ Paragraphs 1–2 and 5–6, 8, 10 unseen — verify they contain nothing that negates this.

### 6. Confirm the severance is genuine "additional" consideration
Current ¶3: *"Severance: 4 weeks' base pay"* (= 28 days; 4/52 ≈ **7.69%** of annual base).
§626(f)(1)(D) requires consideration **in addition to anything of value to which the individual is already entitled** (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md`). ⚠️ Whether a severance plan or policy already entitles an Account Manager to 4 weeks' pay is **unknown** (questioned in `QUESTIONS.md` #1). If it does, the clause fails as drafted and additional consideration (or a different benefit) is required.

### 7. Retain, and verify, the rest of the form
Items that appear acceptable from the excerpt: **mutual non-disparagement (¶9)** and **governing law of Columbia (¶11)**. Items that cannot be assessed: paragraphs 1–2, 5–6, 8, 10 (⚠️ unverified). Obtain the complete form and run one full §626(f)(1)(A)–(G) pass before any use — the arithmetic of the current form against the statute is: 7 days vs 21 required; 3 days vs 7 required; 0 attorney-advice clauses vs 1 required.

## Additional governance points (not OWBPA mechanics, but process)

- **Timing discipline:** never use the agreement while a harassment complaint against the decision-maker is unresolved, a pending-leave interference narrative exists, or the performance record was authored by the accused. A compliant release does not launder a retaliatory-filed termination.
- **Scope reality check:** the library addresses ADEA-waiver enforceability only. Do **not** assume the current form would bind Title VII, FMLA, or Columbia-law claims — that is **unverified** in the library and must not be relied upon (`library/adea_owbpa_626f.md` as sole authority on waiver standards).
- State-law overlay: Columbia mirrors Title VII/FMLA with a longer limitations period and uncapped compensatory damages (`library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md`) — exposure survives even a signed release unless the release is valid as to state claims, which cannot be verified from the library.
