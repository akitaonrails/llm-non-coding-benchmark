# Employer's duty to investigate internal complaints

*Citation:* *Faragher v. City of Boca Raton*, 524 U.S. 775 (1998); *Burlington Indus. v. Ellerth*, 524 U.S. 742 (1998); EEOC Enforcement Guidance (2024)  
*Status in this library:* in force

An employer that receives a harassment complaint must promptly and impartially investigate and take corrective action; failure undermines the *Faragher/Ellerth* affirmative defense and is itself evidence in a retaliation case. The investigation should be conducted by someone outside the accused's chain of command.
