## The 5 things that must change before signature (hard playbook rules A1–A5)

1. **Data Processing Addendum must be executed at signing, not "to be attached."** We will run EU driver data (Rotterdam/Hamburg). Under GDPR Art. 28(3) (library/`gdpr_art28.md`), without the executed DPA Cobalt is in breach **from the first day of processing**. Paper must not be signed with a blank Exhibit C.
2. **Restore the "uncapped" IP indemnity the fine print takes away.** §10.1 says the IP-infringement indemnity is uncapped, but §9.1's cap says "notwithstanding anything to the contrary" — per the interpretation authority in library/`contract_interpretation_notwithstanding.md`, that makes the $222,000/12-month cap apply to the indemnity as drafted. Fix = add an express carve-out inside Section 9. Exposure avoided: IP claims above $222,000 would otherwise be RouteWise's cost, not ours.
3. **Kill the mid-term price increase and the 8% floor.** Order Form §4 lets RouteWise raise fees once during the initial term on 60 days' notice by the **greater of 8% or CPI-U** (≈ +$1,480/mo minimal = +$17,760/yr; up to ~$53,280 over the 36-month term if applied early), and the same formula governs renewals. Playbook allows the **lower of 5% or CPI**, 90 days' notice, renewal terms only: max +$11,100/yr — $6,660/yr less than the draft floor.
4. **Auto-renewals: 12 months, 30-day notice** (draft: 24 months / 90 days). Miss the 90-day window once and we're locked for 24 months = **$444,000** exposure (12-month cycle = $222,000), on top of any increase.
5. **Cover the affiliates at no extra fee.** §2.2 excludes Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. and Cobalt Brokerage LLC unless they're named on an Order Form at additional fees — playbook requires coverage at no additional fee or signature by Holdings on their behalf. Needed for the Europe operations.

**Also recommended (preferred rule, not a deal-breaker):** add a termination right for chronic SLA failure (below target 3 of any 6 months). With credits capped at **$4,625/mo** (25% of fees), otherwise there is no clean way out of a chronically unreliable platform.
