# IRC §453 — installment method

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §453(a), (b), (i)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Income from an installment sale is taken into account under the installment method (gain recognized proportionately as payments are received), **except** that recapture income under §1245 is recognized in the year of sale regardless of payments (§453(i)), and the method is not available for inventory or for sales of depreciable property to related persons.
