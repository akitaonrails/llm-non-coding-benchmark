# Judgment calls and rejected alternatives

1. **S-corp basis and loss.** I reconstructed Marcus's beginning 2025 stock basis as $11,500 from his contribution, annual income/loss, and distributions. The K-1's $28,000 loss is deductible only up to that basis, leaving $16,500 suspended. I rejected Marcus's view that all $28,000 comes straight off income. The corporation's bank line and Pike's personal guarantee do not create Marcus debt basis, and Marcus reports no direct loan. Authority: `library/form7203_instructions_excerpt.md`.

2. **S-corp passive status.** I treated the $11,500 basis-allowed loss as nonpassive because the K-1 states Marcus materially participates. This loss therefore reduces rental MAGI. I rejected excluding it from the Form 8582 MAGI computation. Authority: `library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md`.

3. **Rental at an unchanged rent.** I treated the unrelated tenant's broker-sourced 2019 lease and unchanged $1,500 monthly rent as a fair rental for that lease, allowing the $21,018 net loss into the passive-loss calculation. I rejected treating the $2,600 current market estimate alone as proof of personal use: the library says an unchanged rent below current market does not by itself trigger that rule if rent is fair for the lease. **Open:** the 2025 lease, renewals, and fair-rent basis were not supplied, so the factual condition has not been verified. If the rent is not fair for the lease, the personal-use rule in the library disallows the loss. Authority: `library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md`.

4. **Rental active participation and allowance.** Elena's approval of tenants and repair arrangements supports active participation. The $25,000 special allowance is reduced to $19,550 at $110,900 MAGI, leaving $1,468 carried forward. I rejected deducting the whole $21,018 loss and rejected treating it as wholly passive without considering the special allowance. Authority: `library/form8582_instructions_excerpt.md`.

5. **Dependent claims and child tax credit.** I claim Sofia, age 15, and a $2,200 child tax credit. I exclude Leo because the court order gives his father odd tax years, including 2025; the client says a Form 8332 was signed for his father's year. I rejected a second child tax credit. The client's response used a literal `{TY}` placeholder, so the actual year written on the signed form remains unverified. Authority: `library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md`.

6. **Marketplace policy and family size.** For the working Form 8962, I used the four-person FPL amount supplied by the library and all January–June premiums and advance payments on the Form 1095-A. I rejected simply treating Leo's appearance on the policy as authority to claim him as a dependent. **Open:** no policy allocation between this return and Leo's father's return was provided. The library does not state how a covered child claimed by another household affects family size or policy allocation. Thus the four-person FPL and full-policy reconciliation are explicit assumptions, and the correct result cannot be verified from this library. Authority for the FPL, credit formula, and cap: `library/form8962_instructions_excerpt.md`.

7. **New York residence and wages.** I ended New York residence June 30, including the June 28–30 nights in Queens, for 181 resident days. Both New York employers permitted Florida remote work after employee requests, and neither required work there for business necessity. I therefore source their post-move wages to New York under the convenience rule and allocate their full W-2 wages to New York. I rejected using the Tampa home purchase date, July 3, as the New York residence end date and rejected excluding all Florida workdays solely because the work was physically performed in Florida. Authority: `library/ny_it203_part_year_excerpt.md`.

8. **Federal deduction and tax computation.** I used the $31,500 married-filing-jointly standard deduction. The file gives $6,480 of personal mortgage interest but does not substantiate itemized deductions over the standard amount. I used the $59,850–$59,900 Tax Table row ($6,702), rejecting rate-schedule arithmetic for taxable income below $100,000. Authority: `library/1040_instructions_tax_computation.md`; `library/tax_table_2025_excerpt.md`.
