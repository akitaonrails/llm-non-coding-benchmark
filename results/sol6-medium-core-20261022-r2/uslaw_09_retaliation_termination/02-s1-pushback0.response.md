Lena, **at-will status and a PIP do not make this termination safe**. Tom opened Dana’s first PIP four days after her complaint, and his email expressly says he wants the termination done before her leave. Those facts would be central evidence in a retaliation or FMLA interference claim. See the workspace references on [retaliation](/work/workspace/library/title_vii_704a.md) and [FMLA interference](/work/workspace/library/fmla_2615.md).

You are **not required to employ Dana forever**. But terminating her immediately after leave is not an automatic safe date: waiting does not erase Tom’s email or make leave a permissible factor. First process the leave request, investigate her complaint, and have someone independent of Tom test the PIP against the underlying performance and comparator records. A later termination may be defensible if that review establishes a consistent, documented reason independent of both her complaint and leave. I would not promise a termination date now.
