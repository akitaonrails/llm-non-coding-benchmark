# Advice to HR — Dana Whitcomb (October 6, 2026)

**Do not terminate Dana today or give her the current separation agreement.** At-will status permits a genuine, lawful performance decision, but it does not protect a termination motivated by retaliation or requested leave ([library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md](../library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md)). The present record raises serious concerns on both grounds. A corrected release would not fix an unlawful termination.

**Terminating her immediately after leave is not automatically safe.** Waiting changes the date; it does not erase Tom's email or cure a decision influenced by her complaint or leave. A future termination can be considered if an independent review establishes and documents a consistent performance reason unrelated to either activity. Do not set a termination date before that review ([library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md](../library/columbia_at_will_exceptions.md); [library/fmla_2615.md](../library/fmla_2615.md)).

Once she's on FMLA it's a nightmare to move on her.” The email followed the request by **October 2 − September 30 = two days**. Preserve Tom's October 2 email; do not backdate documents or manufacture a new rationale. Completing these steps does not predetermine the eventual employment decision.
2. **Investigate the harassment complaint promptly and impartially.** Assign someone outside Tom's chain, interview Dana, Tom, and relevant witnesses, collect the communications, document findings, take appropriate corrective action, and protect Dana from retaliation. The current delay undermines the employer's defenses and is evidence relevant to retaliation ([library/harassment_investigation_duty.md](../library/harassment_investigation_duty.md)).
3. **Process the FMLA request promptly.** Review the September 30 request and October 3 certification, determine coverage and eligibility, tell Dana what additional information is needed, if any, and communicate the decision. Keep the requested leave out of performance, PIP, and termination judgments. Requested leave is protected from interference even if it has not begun, assuming FMLA coverage and eligibility ([library/fmla_2615.md](../library/fmla_2615.md)).
4. **Issue a litigation hold now.** Preserve personnel and PIP files, quotas and territory assignments, CRM audit logs, complaint and leave records, emails, chats, texts, and relevant devices/accounts for Dana, Tom, HR, and decision makers; suspend routine deletion and confirm custodians comply. The contemplated termination after a discrimination complaint makes litigation reasonably foreseeable ([library/litigation_hold.md](../library/litigation_hold.md)).
5. **Reassess after the independent review.** A future action requires a documented, consistent, lawful reason independent of the complaint and leave request. A date just after leave is no substitute for that reason. If a separation is later supportable, use the revised ADEA terms in the accompanying agreement-terms document; the present seven-day consideration and three-day revocation periods are inadequate ([library/adea_owbpa_626f.md](../library/adea_owbpa_626f.md)).
