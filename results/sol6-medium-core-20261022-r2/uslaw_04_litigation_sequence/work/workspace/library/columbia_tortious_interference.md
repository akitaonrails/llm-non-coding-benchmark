# State of Columbia — tortious interference with contract / prospective relations (elements and defenses)

*Citation:* *Ashby v. Caldera Partners*, 212 Col. App. 3d 440 (2019); Restatement (Second) of Torts §§766, 767, 773  
*Status in this library:* in force

Elements: (1) a valid contract or expectancy between plaintiff and a third party; (2) defendant's knowledge; (3) intentional and improper interference causing breach or termination; (4) damages. **A party to the contract cannot tortiously interfere with it** (the remedy is breach of contract). Interference with plaintiff's relationships with its own at-will employees is actionable only if the means are improper; **consent of the plaintiff** and **legitimate business justification** (§773) are complete defenses. *(Columbia is a fictional jurisdiction created for this benchmark; treat these as the governing authorities.)*
