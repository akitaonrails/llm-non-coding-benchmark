# Firm memo (2024): 'Enforcing Texas non-competes against remote California employees'

*Citation:* Internal memo, Meridian outside counsel file  
*Status in this library:* internal memo — authorities cited within are NOT in this library

…Recent authority supports enforcement where the employee voluntarily accepted a Texas forum: see ***Calderon v. Pacific Dynamics Corp.*, 48 Cal.App.5th 1120 (2023)** (enforcing Texas choice of law against a remote California employee who 'availed himself' of Texas benefits) and ***Westbrook Systems, Inc. v. Ng*, 91 F.4th 1033 (9th Cir. 2024)** (holding §16600.5 does not apply extraterritorially to contracts with out-of-state employers). On that basis we recommended suit in Travis County in the Alvarez matter…
