## Halvorsen letter of intent — remove $480,000

**Facts.** The December 19 letter, signed by Halvorsen's SVP, proposes a 24-month license for $480,000. It expressly says it is non-binding except for confidentiality, requires a definitive Master Subscription Agreement, procurement approval, and a satisfactory security review, and permits either party to stop discussions.

**Criterion.** A revenue contract requires approval and commitment, identifiable rights and payment terms, commercial substance, and probable collection. An expressly non-binding letter subject to a definitive agreement creates no enforceable rights or obligations for revenue purposes ([ASC 606 contract existence](../library/asc606_contract_and_bill_hold.md)).

**Conclusion and amount.** There is no revenue contract on the supplied facts. Reverse the entire **$480,000** Q4 license revenue; recognized Halvorsen revenue is **$0**. The CEO's view that procurement is merely slow does not satisfy the missing commitment and approvals.

## Saltline edge kits — remove $210,000

**Facts.** Saltline ordered 120 kits for $210,000 and asked Northwind to hold them until its Tampa site is ready around February 10. Northwind invoiced December 28. Sales instructed operations to draw kits later from general stock without tagging them. The countersigned side letter permits unused kits to be returned for a full refund within 180 days if the rollout is cancelled or reduced. Saltline pays no storage fee.

**Criterion.** Bill-and-hold control transfer requires a substantive reason, separately identified customer goods, goods ready for physical transfer, and no ability for the seller to use or redirect them; **all** criteria must be met. A return right also makes consideration variable, with revenue limited to the amount not expected to be refunded and a refund liability ([ASC 606 bill-and-hold and rights of return](../library/asc606_contract_and_bill_hold.md)).

**Conclusion and amount.** The customer-requested delay supplies a substantive reason, but the kits remain in untagged general stock and can be drawn for another customer. Separate identification and inability to redirect fail. Current readiness for physical transfer is not established either. The prior year's accepted bill-and-hold involved segregated, labeled goods with no return right; those facts do not apply here. The side letter adds a material refund exposure, but no refund estimate is needed to decide Q4 recognition because control has not transferred. Reverse the entire **$210,000** Q4 hardware revenue; recognized Saltline revenue is **$0**. The invoice alone does not change this conclusion.

## Ironvale hosting — embedded lease; revenue adjustment $0, balance sheet amounts open

**Facts.** From October 1, 2025, the 36-month agreement specifies dedicated servers and arrays by serial number for Northwind's exclusive use. Ironvale can substitute them only upon failure and with Northwind's consent. Northwind directs workloads, configuration, operating systems, and utilization. Ironvale provides power, cooling, and network. The fee is $18,000 monthly in arrears; the drafted P&L expensed three months, **$54,000** (3 × $18,000).

**Criterion.** An identified asset with no substantive supplier substitution right, substantially all economic benefits, and customer direction of how and for what purpose it is used is an embedded lease. Power, cooling, and network are nonlease services. The lessee recognizes a right-of-use asset and lease liability based on the present value of lease payments ([ASC 842 identification](../library/asc842_identification.md)).

**Conclusion and amount.** The dedicated equipment is an embedded lease; the other hosting services are a separate nonlease component. The $54,000 charge cannot simply remain classified wholly as hosting service expense without measuring the lease and determining its period expense. The contract's total nominal fees are **$648,000** (36 × $18,000), but that includes services and is not the lease liability. If the first three payments were made at month-end, nominal fees for the remaining 33 months would be **$594,000** (33 × $18,000), also not a present value or lease-only amount. The client supplied no lease/service allocation, discount rate, payment confirmation, or information on prepayments, incentives, and initial direct costs. Thus **December 31 right-of-use asset and lease liability are unknown, not zero**. The supplied library does not provide enough subsequent-measurement or classification guidance to quantify a Q4 lease expense adjustment; any specific treatment beyond its stated identification and initial-recognition principles would be unverified here.
