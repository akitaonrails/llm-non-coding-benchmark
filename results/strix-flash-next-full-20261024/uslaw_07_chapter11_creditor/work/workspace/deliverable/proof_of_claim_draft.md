## Filing mechanics (do not miss)

- **Bar date: 2026-06-11 at 5:00 p.m. Mountain Time**, by electronic filing with the claims agent or by mail (documents/notice_of_commencement_and_bar_date.pdf). A non-governmental creditor that does not file by the bar date is barred from treatment as a creditor for voting and distribution, and a late claim requires a showing of excusable neglect (library/frbp_3002_3003.md).
- The §503(b)(9) piece must be **asserted separately** from the general unsecured claim — by motion or by the procedure the bar-date order specifies (library/frbp_3002_3003.md). The notice in the file does not state that procedure or a separate 503(b)(9) deadline, and the client holds no bar-date order beyond the notice (Round 1) → counsel must obtain the operative order from the claims agent/docket. Plan: file a **stand-alone §503(b)(9) motion** in parallel with the claim rather than relying on the checkbox.
- The notice in the file is internally inconsistent — it is dated **2026-04-02**, a date in the future relative to today (2026-03-19). Confirm the operative bar-date order before relying on the dates; the notice is the only date evidence in the file.
- Filing the claim is not itself a collection act and is permitted; nothing on the claim form demands payment from Redwood (contrast the stay analysis in library/bc_362.md).
- Reclamation demand deadline **2026-03-30** is independent of the bar date and is the nearer hard date (library/bc_503b9_546c.md).
