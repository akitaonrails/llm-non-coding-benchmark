## Part A — Resolved by client answers (applied)

- **Q1. Did Beacon and Orchard pay through PayHub, or outside it?** Answer: Beacon and
  Orchard paid **through PayHub**; Tessaro paid by **direct bank transfer**. Applied:

  1099-K gross and are not added again; the Tessaro NEC ($9,400.00) matches the four

  double-count and was rejected.
- **Q2. What was the June 13 AUS-DEN flight for?** Answer: her **cousin's wedding**.
  Applied: $389.00 is personal (IRC 262), not line 24a.
- **Q5. Business-use percentage for phone and internet?** Answer: **60%**. Applied:
  line 25 = ($1,020.00 + $840.00) × 60% = $1,116.00 (previously an assumed 80%).
- **Q9a. Is the BCBS premium her own policy?** Answer: **yes — her own plan, started
  after leaving Larkspur**. Applied: 8 × $380.00 = $3,040.00 to Form 1040 Schedule 1
  line 17 (library Schedule C instructions, line 15 note), not Schedule C. Documents
  corroborate: payroll credits end 2025-04-28, premiums start 2025-05-09, W-2 box 12
  code DD $1,960.00 shows employer coverage Jan–Apr.

  unknown.** Treatment: expensed on line 27a under the de minimis safe harbor

  line 9 shrinks.

  ($1,537.60 + $359.60) implies take-home $20,592.80, but payroll credits to this
  account total $19,502.80. Flagged for the 1040-side reconciliation (possible
  benefit/pre-tax deductions); does not affect Schedule C or Schedule SE.
