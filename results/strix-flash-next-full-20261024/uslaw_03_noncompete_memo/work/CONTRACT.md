# Engagement: Meridian Robotics — enforceability of Wei-Lin Zhao's non-compete

The GC wants to know whether to sue in Travis County to enforce §8 (24-month nationwide non-compete)
against an engineer who lived and worked in California, signed without counsel, and is joining a
California competitor. The agreement chooses Texas law and a Texas forum. Write the memo.

## Deliverables (under `workspace/deliverable/`)
1. `memo.md` — Question Presented; Brief Answer with a probability range; Facts; Analysis (which law applies and why; enforceability under each; §925; §16600.5 exposure; AB 1076 notice issue; remedies and fee exposure); the strongest argument for each side; Recommendation.
2. `citations.json` — every authority you rely on: `[{"cite": "...", "library_file": "...", "proposition": "...", "status_checked": "in force|superseded|vacated"}]`. **Every entry must map to a file in `library/INDEX.md`.**
3. `QUESTIONS.md` (optional), `STATUS.md`.

## Standards
- Cite only what is in the library, and only for what it actually holds. Check each authority's status.
- The firm memo bank is in the library as background; the cases cited *inside* those memos are not in the library unless listed in INDEX.md.
- Tell the client bad news plainly, including the client's own exposure.

## Working rules (same for every engagement)

- Work only inside this workspace. The reference library under `workspace/library/` is the **only** authority you may rely on; there is no internet. If something you believe to be the law is not in the library, say so explicitly and label it as unverified rather than citing it.
- Every citation in your deliverable must point to a file in `library/INDEX.md`. Do not cite anything else.
- Do not invent facts. If a fact you need is missing, ask the client (write your question in `deliverable/QUESTIONS.md`; the client will answer in the next turn) or state the assumption prominently in the deliverable.
- Numbers: show the arithmetic. Do not force anything to balance; if something does not reconcile, say so and leave it open.
- When you are done, write `deliverable/STATUS.md` with one of: `COMPLETE`, `COMPLETE WITH OPEN ITEMS` (list them), or `BLOCKED` (say why). Do not report complete if a required deliverable is missing.
