# FTC Non-Compete Clause Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 910

*Citation:* 89 Fed. Reg. 38342 (May 7, 2024)  
*Status in this library:* vacated — see Ryan LLC v. FTC

The Rule would have declared most worker non-compete clauses an unfair method of competition and required notice to workers. **Status: set aside nationwide in *Ryan LLC v. FTC*, 739 F. Supp. 3d 496 (N.D. Tex. Aug. 20, 2024); the FTC voted in September 2025 to dismiss its appeal and accede to vacatur. The Rule never took effect and is not in force.**
