# Washington CR 55 — default (excerpt)

*Citation:* Wash. Super. Ct. Civ. R. 55(a)(3)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Any party who has appeared in the action for any purpose shall be served with a written notice of motion for default and the supporting affidavit **at least 5 days** before the hearing on the motion. A notice of appearance, even if informal, is an appearance. Relief from default: CR 55(c), CR 60(b).
