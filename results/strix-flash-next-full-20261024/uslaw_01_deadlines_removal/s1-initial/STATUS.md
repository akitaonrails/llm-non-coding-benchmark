- **Removal question answered — NO.** The client confirmed Harbor Street Bakery LLC has exactly two members: Noor Haddad 65% (Portland, OR) and **Caleb Sorensen 35% (Seattle/Ballard, WA)**. An LLC takes the citizenship of all its members (*Johnson v. Columbia Properties Anchorage, LP*, 437 F.3d 894, 899 (9th Cir. 2006); *Carden*, 494 U.S. 185), so the defendant is a **Washington** and Oregon citizen while the plaintiff is a Washington citizen → no complete diversity (28 U.S.C. § 1332(a)(1)), **and** the forum-defendant rule bars removal (§ 1441(b)(2)).
