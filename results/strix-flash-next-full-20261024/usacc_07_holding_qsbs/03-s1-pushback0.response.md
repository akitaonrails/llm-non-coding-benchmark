Updated in `opinion.md` §3 (new rule (v) + sharpened DON'T #1) and §6 step 6. Direct answer:

**You're right to press on this — but the advisor is wrong, and the library proves it twice over.**

**1. His route doesn't defer tax — it destroys your exclusion.** §1202 requires you to have owned the stock since original issue. The law lists the transfers that preserve that status — gift, death, distribution *out of* a partnership — and the library states expressly: **"A contribution of stock to a partnership is not among the listed transfers... the stock ceases to qualify in its hands."** So the moment Helio stock sits inside Holdings and then sells, you have a **fully taxable** $9–10M gain. You currently have a **100% tax-free** one. His plan doesn't avoid the tax; it creates it.

**2. Even ignoring that, "the gain lands in the LLC" is mechanically false.** An LLC/partnership pays no entity tax and *retains* nothing — the library's own §1202(g) talks about gain from stock "held by a partnership" that **"flows to a partner."** The gain lands on *your* return in the year of the sale, whether or not the cash is reinvested. There is no rollover or "reinvest inside the LLC without tax" provision anywhere in the authorities available for this engagement. A wholly-owned LLC cannot postpone your own income to yourself.

**Can the holding own it after the sale?** Depends what "it" means:

- **The Helio stock** — after the sale the stock is gone (the acquirer bought it), so there's nothing for Holdings to own. And if Holdings held the stock *at the moment of sale*, that's just the advisor's plan in disguise — exclusion destroyed. **No.**
- **The sale proceeds** — **yes, and that's the workable version of his idea**: sell the shares yourself, in your own name → the exclusion locks in on your personal return → **then** contribute the cash into Holdings. §721(a) makes that contribution a $0-gain event. Holdings then serves as your pooling/reinvestment vehicle. But it only works in that order — and future gains earned inside Holdings get no special treatment; the exclusion doesn't travel with the cash.

The one-line test to remember: **money may move into Holdings before or after; Helio stock may never move in before the sale.** Gift/estate transfers of the shares are the only permitted movements, and they keep your holding period tacked.
