# IRC §721 and §752 — contributions to a partnership; liabilities

*Citation:* 26 U.S.C. §721(a); §752(b); §731(a)  
*Status in this library:* in force

§721(a): no gain or loss on contribution of property for a partnership interest. §752(b): a decrease in a partner's share of partnership liabilities is treated as a distribution of money. §731(a): a distribution of money in excess of the partner's basis in the partnership interest is gain. **Consequence:** contributing an LLC interest whose liabilities exceed basis (negative tax capital) can trigger gain to the extent liabilities shifted to other partners exceed the contributor's basis; with a 99/1 partnership, 1% of the mortgage shifts to the other partner.
