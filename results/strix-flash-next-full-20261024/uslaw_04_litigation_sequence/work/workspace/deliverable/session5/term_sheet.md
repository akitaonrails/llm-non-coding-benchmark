### 2. Our reservation value (BATNA)

Cost to arbitrate to final award — **UNVERIFIED estimate**: the library contains no AAA fee
schedules or arbitrator rate data. Planning assumption: 600 attorney hours × $225/hr = $135,000.00
+ AAA administrative and arbitrator costs ≈ $20,000.00 → **≈ $155,000.00**, planned at **$150,000.00**
(sanity range $110,000–$200,000).

C.3 shifts "reasonable fees and costs" to the prevailing party, mutual and enforced as written
(`library/fee_shifting_and_candor.md`; *Zuniga*). Probability we are found prevailing substantially
in a case whose invoice core is already conceded: 0.65.

> Net arbitrated value ≈ $380,837.88 − $150,000.00 + (0.65 × $150,000.00) = **$328,337.88**,
> before discounting for receipt ~9–12 months out (`library/faa_2_4.md` timeline is not specified in
> the library; the duration estimate is ours, unverified).

### 3. Their side of the table

Pinecrest's expected cash on the award path: $380,837.88 (award) + 0.65 × $150,000.00 (C.3 shift to
us) = **$478,337.88**, plus their own defense costs (≈ $120,000.00, our estimate). A rational
Pinecrest settles anywhere meaningfully below ~$478,000. Their written $265,200.00 concession means
the downside for them is not *whether* they pay, but *how much more*.

## Part II — Proposed term sheet (negotiable; subject to client approval)

**Settlement of invoice and conversion-fee dispute — Juniper Health Staffing, LLC / Pinecrest
Senior Living, Inc.** Terms proposed by counsel for Juniper; non-binding until executed.

**Recitals.** The parties operate under a written Staffing Services Agreement (the "Agreement,"
including Schedule 1 and Exhibits A–C). Five invoices (J-4411, J-4452, J-4490, J-4533, J-4571)
totaling $340,000.00 are billed and unpaid; Juniper has credited approximately $3,100.00 against
J-4533, leaving a net claimed invoice balance of $336,900.00. Pinecrest's counsel's letter of
Sept. 29, 2026 states Pinecrest "will pay the undisputed balance of the remaining invoices upon
resolution of the conversion-fee issue" and encloses as Exhibit 1 the Feb. 11, 2026 email from
Marcus Bell waiving the conversion fee "for Rosa and Tay as a one-off." The parties wish to resolve
all disputes under the Agreement without arbitration. *(Recitals cite only documents in the
parties' hands; no characterization of any other correspondence.)*

1. **Settlement payment.** Pinecrest pays Juniper total consideration ("Settlement Amount") per
   either structure, at Pinecrest's election:
   - **Structure A (lump sum):** $385,000.00 (draft; within the Part I band — final number per
     client-approved range) by wire within **21 days** of execution.
   - **Structure B (tranche structure):** (i) $279,351.50 — the undisputed invoice balance of
     $265,200.00 plus §2 interest through Nov. 30, 2026 — payable within **10 days** of execution,
     without regard to resolution of the conversion-fee component; (ii) $74,568.00 — the J-4533 net
     balance of $71,700.00 (billed $74,800.00, less $3,100.00 credited by Juniper) plus §2 interest
     through Nov. 30, 2026 — within **30 days** of execution; and (iii) a conversion-fee tranche
     per § 2 below within **60 days** of execution. Invoice tranches (i)+(ii) total $353,919.50
     (= $336,900.00 claimed + $17,019.50 interest through Nov. 30, 2026).
2. **Conversion-fee component (Structure B).** The fee tranche equals **25% of each Direct Hire's
   actual first-year compensation** for the five qualifying hires — Priya S. (RN), Luis F. (RN),
   Keisha B. (LPN), Anne O. (RN), Victor H. (CNA) — computed from compensation records in
   Pinecrest's custody, which Pinecrest shall produce within 15 days of execution; tranche
   **floor $80,000.00, ceiling $112,000.00**. The floor equals 5 × $16,000.00, i.e., 25% of
   $64,000.00 — the average first-year compensation indicated by Juniper's roster for comparable
   hires; the ceiling gives Pinecrest certainty of total exposure against higher actual
   compensation (three of the five are RNs). The February 2026 waiver as to Rosa M. and Tay W. is
   acknowledged and released without payment.
3. **Interest.** The Settlement Amount includes all §2 interest through a date certain recited in
   the agreement; timely payment extinguishes further interest. Default bears §2 interest (1%/month).
4. **Releases.** Mutual general release of all claims arising from or relating to the Agreement,
   the five invoices, and the direct hiring of any Pinecrest personnel introduced by Juniper
   (including all seven at-issue individuals). Mutual release of any claim for tortious interference
   in contract. No admission of liability by either party.
5. **No arbitration; withdrawal.** Neither party shall commence an arbitration under Exhibit C.1 on
   the released claims; if a filing has been made before execution, the filing party withdraws it
   with each side bearing its own costs incurred to date.
6. **Default and revival.** If any tranche is unpaid when due and not cured within 10 business days,
   the unpaid balance accelerates, the releases become voidable at Juniper's election, and Juniper
   may pursue the full released claims in arbitration with **fees and costs under Exhibit C.3**;
   payments received credit against any award.
7. **Confidentiality and non-disparagement.** Mutual; terms and amount confidential except to
   accountants, auditors, insurers, and as required by law or to enforce the agreement.
8. **Fees.** Each party bears its own attorneys' fees and costs incurred in this settlement.
9. **Execution deadline.** Signature pages returned by **Nov. 13, 2026**; after that date this
   proposal lapses and the demand figures re-run at the then-current interest total. Authority:
   Juniper by Dolapo Okonkwo, CEO; Pinecrest by an authorized officer.
