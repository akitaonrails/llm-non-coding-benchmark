## 3. Conversion fees — five hires

Section 3 provides that Pinecrest shall not directly hire Personnel introduced by Juniper during the term
(and for 12 months thereafter) "except upon payment of a conversion fee equal to 25% of the Personnel's
first-year annualized compensation." Between March and June 2026, Pinecrest hired seven Juniper personnel.

Juniper is not asserting conversion fees for **Rosa M. and Tay W.** In an email dated February 11, 2026,
Juniper's account manager waived the fee for those two hires. That waiver was honored, and by its own terms
it was "a one-off" — it did not extend to anyone else, and Section 3 permits waivers only in writing.

No written waiver exists for the remaining five hires, each of whom Pinecrest hired without payment of any
conversion fee:

| Hire | Role | Month hired | Conversion fee due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Priya S. | RN | April 2026 | $16,000.00 |
| Luis F. | RN | May 2026 | $16,000.00 |
| Keisha B. | LPN | May 2026 | $16,000.00 |
| Anne O. | RN | June 2026 | $16,000.00 |
| Victor H. | CNA | June 2026 | $16,000.00 |
| **Total** | | | **$80,000.00** |

Each figure is 25% × $64,000, the average first-year annualized compensation for this group based on the
information currently available to Juniper. These amounts are stated as minimums: the fee is calculated
hire by hire, and Juniper reserves the right to adjust each figure upon production of actual first-year
compensation data, including from Pinecrest's own payroll records.

To be clear about the record: Pinecrest announced each of these hires to Juniper in advance. Juniper's
informal responses to those announcements were not — and under Section 3 could not be — waivers, which the
Agreement requires in writing. Pinecrest's stated reason for the hires was a corporate mandate to reduce
agency spend, which does not relieve it of the fee that Section 3 attaches to exactly this decision.
