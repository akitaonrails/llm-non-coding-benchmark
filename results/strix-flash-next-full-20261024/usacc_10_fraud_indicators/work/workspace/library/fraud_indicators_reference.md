# Common red flags in disbursement and payroll fraud

*Citation:* ACFE Occupational Fraud reference (summary)  
*Status in this library:* in force

Shell vendors: vendor address matching an employee or relative; no W-9/PO; created by the approver; round-number invoices; vague descriptions; **amounts structured just under an approval threshold**; sequential or evenly spaced invoices. Ghost employees: no time records, no benefits, paper checks or P.O. box, hired by one person without HR documentation, address matching another party in the file.
