# Disbursement approval policy (adopted 2022)
- Invoices **≥ $5,000** require approval by **two** partners and a purchase order.
- Invoices under $5,000 may be approved by any partner.
- New vendors require a W-9 and a second-partner acknowledgment before first payment.
- Payroll additions require a signed offer letter, I-9, and enrollment in the time-clock system.
