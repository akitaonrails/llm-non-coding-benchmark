# Term sheet — Operating Agreement, Natarajan Herrera Advisory LLC (CA)

Drafting terms for the CA LLC recommended in `recommendation.md`. Framework follows the practitioner buy-sell/governance checklist (`library/llc_buy_sell_checklist.md`); each item notes the client-specific reason. Client decisions needed are marked **[DECIDE]** — see QUESTIONS.md.

## 1. Units, contributions, capital

- **Priya:** 50% of Class A units; capital account $120,000 for conveyance of the PriceLab tool (independent appraisal FMV $120,000, Coastline Valuation Advisors, 1/15/2026). Representations: sole owner, unencumbered, no prior exclusive licenses, full conveyance (vs. license — **[DECIDE]**, open question); discloses $31,000 tax basis and $89,000 deducted development costs.
- **Tomás:** 50% of Class A units; capital account $20,000 (cash). Additionally granted a **50% profits interest effective at grant** (structured for Rev. Proc. 93-27 treatment: $0 on an immediate liquidation at FMV) and a **capital interest that vests** per §2.
- §721 character agreed: contributions are property-for-interest; members agree to file consistently (Priya: nonrecognition; Tomás: profits interest per Rev. Proc. 93-27 / Rev. Proc. 2001-43, treated as a partner from grant).
- Tax classification: **partnership**; no S election while any member is a nonresident alien (§1361(b)(1)(C)); election only by unanimous consent after all members are eligible.

## 2. Vesting (their stated ask: Tomás 3 years, 1-year cliff)

- Tomás's **capital** rights vest 1/3 at month 12, then 1/12 monthly, fully vested at month 36. His **profits** interest is granted at closing (not subject to forfeiture except for-cause — **[DECIDE]** whether for-cause clawback applies to profits).
- Company repurchase right at **cost for unvested units** on departure (checklist item).
- Priya is fully vested (her equity is purchased with the tool, not time).
- Protective **§83(b)** election by Tomás within 30 days of grant; the agreement obligates him to file and deliver the copy (deadline is statutory, no extension — `library/irc_83b.md`).

## 3. Buy-sell triggers (checklist set, customized)

On: **death; disability; divorce (spousal interest); bankruptcy; voluntary departure; for-cause removal** — plus, client-specific: **loss of U.S. work authorization / visa termination for Tomás** (immigration consequences outside the library — flag for counsel **[DECIDE]**).

- On any trigger, the other member/company gets an **option to purchase** at:
  - Unvested capital units: **cost**.
  - Vested units: **fair market value by independent appraisal** (same posture as the Coastline appraisal), with a binding formula floor available **[DECIDE]** (formula vs. appraisal-only). No minority/marketability discounts between members **[DECIDE]** (checklist flags discounts as an option; 50/50 makes them a fairness question).
- Payment: **[DECIDE]** term payout (e.g., 20% down, 3–5 years, stated interest) vs. cash; funding via term life on each member recommended so death doesn't force a fire sale (standard practice, not a library item — **[U]**).
- **Divorce/community property — facts now confirmed (client round 2):** Tomás is **married, resides in Mexico, and has no prenuptial/marital property agreement**. The **spousal consent / community-property joinder is therefore mandatory at formation**, not a contingency: spouse acknowledges the units, vesting and buy-sell terms and waives rights to in-kind division; transferee spouse (if any) becomes a **non-voting economic-only** member subject to mandatory call. The Mexican-law community-property consequences themselves are outside the library — Mexican counsel should review the clause **[U]**. Priya is **confirmed single** (San Diego): same clause inserted as protection against a future marital regime **[DECIDE]**.

## 4. Deadlock (50/50 is the whole game)

Per the checklist escalation for 50/50 owners:
1. Good-faith executive negotiation (15 days) → **mediation** (30 days) →
2. **Buy-sell "shotgun":** either member may name a price per unit; the other must either sell all units at that price or buy all at that price (classic 50/50 breaker; symmetric because interests are equal).
3. Standing tie-breaker for money matters: unanimous consent for (i) new debt/credit lines, (ii) hiring/firing above $[DECIDE] (Watson-type reasonable-comp posture: owner compensation must be defensible — `library/reasonable_compensation.md`), (iii) admitting members, (iv) dissolution/sale of the company or the PriceLab asset, (v) entity tax elections.

## 5. Transfer restrictions (checklist)

- No transfers without unanimous consent; carve-outs: to a member's estate/trust on death (economic only) **[DECIDE]**.
- **Tag-along** (checklist item): if Priya sells her control block, Tomás may join.
- **Drag-along** (checklist item): either direction in a 50/50 is mutual; include symmetrical drag with unanimous-price protection.
- Non-compete / non-solicit during membership + 12–24 months **[DECIDE]**; Tomás's "client relationships" are his contribution — define company vs. pre-existing clients expressly **[DECIDE]**.
- IP: PriceLab conveyed to the company; any future member-created tool is assigned to the company as a condition of unit vesting.

## 6. Tax cooperation covenants (client-specific)

- Maintain and exchange a **monthly** travel/day-count ledger (substantial presence drives everyone's filings — `library/irc_7701b_substantial_presence.md`). Confirmed pattern (client round 2): Tomás ~1/3 of each year in the U.S. (~122 days) — within about a day of the steady-state residency line (1.5 × days ≥ 183 ⟺ 122 days/yr; see `recommendation.md` §2). The log is a core record, not housekeeping.
- Cooperation on valuations at each §83 vesting date and on any election (Form 2553 only if all members eligible, §1361(b)(1)(C)).
- Distributions: default 50/50 of available cash; mandatory tax distributions pro-rata to each member's estimated liability (covers SE tax and, in Tomás's NRA years, any U.S. filing/withholding posture — not in the library, **[U]**, confirm with counsel).
