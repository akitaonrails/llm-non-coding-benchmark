# RouteWise MSA — one page for the COO

**Deal:** RouteWise's routing/fleet-optimization platform (SaaS), up to 350 vehicles. Initial term **36 months**; **$18,500/month billed annually in advance ($222,000/year)** plus a one-time **$25,000** implementation fee. Committed spend before any increases: 36 × $18,500 = **$666,000**, plus $25,000 = **$691,000**. 99.5% uptime target with service credits. Provider's paper (v7.3), signed as "Cobalt Logistics Holdings LLC" only.

**Bottom line:** do not sign this week as-is. Five playbook hard rules are broken. All are fixable with the attached redlines; once fixed, the deal is signable quickly.

## Five things that must change before signature

1. **The "uncapped" IP indemnity is not actually uncapped.** §10.1 says RouteWise's IP-infringement indemnity is uncapped, but the liability cap in §9.1 ("notwithstanding anything to the contrary") swallows it — §9.3's exceptions do not mention the indemnity. If a third party sues us over RouteWise's software, RouteWise's exposure stops at ~12 months of fees: 12 × $18,500 = **$222,000** (about $239,760 after an 8% price bump) — and RouteWise's obligation to pay damages, costs and attorneys' fees then competes for that same $222,000; Cobalt funds everything above it. Fix: one added carve-out (RL-1).

2. **No data protection contract for EU driver data — that is illegal for us, not just a nit.** Exhibit C ("Data Processing Addendum") says "[To be attached]." We will run EU driver data through this system. Under GDPR Art. 28(3), the company that sends the data (Cobalt) is in breach **from day one of processing** unless a signed processor contract with specified terms exists. The DPA must be attached and signed with the MSA. This is the real schedule risk for "signing this week."

3. **RouteWise can raise our price mid-contract.** The Order Form allows one increase during the 36-month term (and uncapped renewal increases) of the **greater** of 8% or CPI, on 60 days' notice. Our playbook: no increases in the initial term; renewals capped at the **lower** of 5% or CPI on 90 days' notice. Numbers: 8% = **+$1,480/month**; if used at month 18 it costs **+$26,640** over the remaining term; at renewal it is **+$17,760/year** versus today's fees ($239,760 vs $222,000), where a compliant 5% cap would add only $11,100/year — a $6,660/year swing.

4. **24-month auto-renewal with a 90-day exit window.** Miss the notice deadline (month 33 of a 36-month term) and we are locked into **24 more months**: 24 × $19,980 ≈ **$479,520** at increased rates. Playbook: 12-month renewals, 30-day notice — max ~12 × $19,425 = **$233,100** per cycle. Fix the clause and put the notice date on the calendar.

5. **Our affiliates are not licensed.** The license covers Holdings only; affiliates must be on an Order Form "at additional fees." Our playbook requires Cobalt Freight Europe B.V. and Cobalt Brokerage LLC to be covered free — and the Rotterdam/Hamburg operations (source of the EU driver data) appear to sit in those entities. *Assumption: the EU entities will use the platform.* Fix: name them in §2.2 (RL-5); also confirm the 350-vehicle cap covers everyone's combined fleet.

**One more worth asking (playbook "preferred", not hard):** SLA credits (max 25% of a month's fee = **$4,625/month**) are the *sole* remedy for downtime unless we add a termination right for chronic failure (3 bad months in any 6). A routing outage costs us far more than $4,625.

## What is already fine (no fight needed)

Delaware law and jury waiver; mutual liability cap with gross-negligence/willful-misconduct/fraud carve-outs (except the indemnity fix in item 1); 30-day data export window; 15-day suspension notice; 99.5% SLA with credits. Per the GC's steer, these are playbook-compliant and are not on the issues list.

**Ask:** authorize the six redlines (deliverable/redlines.md); Legal drafts the DPA exhibit this week. With those changes, signing this week is achievable.
