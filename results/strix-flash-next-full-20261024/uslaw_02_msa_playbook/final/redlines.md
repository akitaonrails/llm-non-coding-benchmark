## RL-1 — §9.1 and §9.3: carve the §10 indemnity out of the liability cap (playbook A1, C2)

**Why:** §9.1's "NOTWITHSTANDING ANYTHING TO THE CONTRARY" cap controls over §10.1's "uncapped" statement, because a cap made to apply notwithstanding anything else is overridden only if the cap clause itself carves the obligation out (Restatement (Second) of Contracts §203; Delaware "notwithstanding anything to the contrary" authority — `library/contract_interpretation_notwithstanding.md`). Fix both clauses so §10 sits outside the cap. Keep the rest of §9: playbook B1 affirmatively accepts a mutual 12–24-month cap with gross-negligence/willful-misconduct/fraud carve-outs.

**Replace §9.1 with:**

> 9.1 EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SECTION 9.3, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

**Replace §9.3 with:**

> 9.3 The exclusions and limitations in this Section 9 shall not apply to: (a) a party's liability for its gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud; (b) a party's obligations under Section 10 (Indemnification), Provider's obligations under Section 10.1 being uncapped; or (c) Customer's obligation to pay fees due under this Agreement.

§10.1 itself needs no change once carve-out (b) is in place.

## RL-2 — §5.3 / Exhibit C: DPA executed at signing (playbook A2)

**Why:** Exhibit C is "[To be attached.]". Cobalt will process EU driver data (GC email). Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Art. 28(3) (`library/gdpr_art28.md`), processing by a processor must be governed by a binding contract stipulating items (a)–(h), and "[a]bsent such a contract, the controller is in breach of Art. 28 from the first day of processing." Cobalt is the controller; RouteWise the processor.

**Replace §5.3 with:**

> 5.3 The parties execute and attach as Exhibit C the Data Processing Addendum, which is incorporated into this Agreement and binds Provider with regard to all processing of personal data subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR performed by Provider on Customer's behalf (including personal data of Customer's drivers and customers). Provider shall not process any personal data of EU or UK residents until Exhibit C has been executed. Exhibit C stipulates, at minimum, that Provider: (a) processes personal data only on Customer's documented instructions; (b) ensures that personnel authorized to process the data are bound by confidentiality; (c) takes the security measures required by Article 28(3)(c); (d) respects the sub-processor conditions of Article 28(3)(d); (e)–(f) assists Customer with data-subject requests, security incidents, and prior consultations as required by Article 28(3)(e)–(f); (g) deletes or returns Customer Data at the end of the Services as required by Article 28(3)(g); and (h) makes available all information reasonably necessary and permits audits as required by Article 28(3)(h).

**Replace the body of Exhibit C ("[To be attached.]")** with the full executed DPA text. Open action: obtain Cobalt's standard DPA template and attach it — the clause-level redline above forces attachment and execution at signing and locks in the Art. 28(3)(a)–(h) content minimum. (The Art. 28 breach exposure is supported by the library; specific fine amounts are outside the library and are not asserted.)

## RL-3 — Exhibit A (Order Form) ¶4: no mid-term increases; renewal cap = lesser of 5% or CPI, 90 days' notice (playbook A3, C1)

**Why:** Current ¶4 allows one unilateral increase during the Initial Subscription Term (60 days' notice) and renewal increases up to "the greater of 8% or the change in the CPI-U." Playbook A3: no initial-term increases; renewal cap is the **lower** of 5% or CPI, on 90 days' notice. Because §14.1 makes the Order Form control over the MSA for its subject matter, the Order Form itself must be amended.

**Replace Order Form ¶4 with:**

> 4. Pricing adjustments. The fees for the Initial Subscription Term are fixed and shall not be increased. Fees for any renewal term shall not increase by more than the lesser of (a) five percent (5%) and (b) the increase in CPI-U (All Items, U.S. City Average) over the twelve (12) months preceding the start of the renewal term. Provider shall give Customer written notice of any renewal-term fee increase at least ninety (90) days before the start of that renewal term.
