## 1. NEW — Pinecone Dental Group (C-025): $36,000 billed and collected, recognized $0

- Order form C-025 (2025-02-01): one blended line, "Implementation & license —
  one-time," $36,000; payment due on signature; service start 2025-02-01;
  scope "platform deployment and user onboarding for up to 40 seats";
  **no contract term stated**; governed by Master Subscription Agreement
  v3.2, which is **not in the documents provided**.
- Invoice INV-2250 (billed 02/01) describes it as "Implementation & license —
  one-time." Per our standards, the invoice description is not the revenue
  criterion — the library is.
- Treatment: **deferred in full to 2200** (JE-2502-002), recognized $0.00 in
  February. Why:
  - Board revenue policy #2 (`library/revenue_policy_memo.md`) recognizes
    implementation services **only if a separately priced, distinct service is
    actually delivered**. The order form has no separate implementation price
    (one blended line), and February documents contain **no delivery evidence**
    — no completion certificate, sign-off, or seat-provisioning record.
  - The described scope — platform deployment and user onboarding — is
    **set-up activity**, which under ASC 606-10-25-17 is not a performance
    obligation if it does not transfer a service to the customer
    (`library/asc606_excerpts.md`).
  - Amounts billed before performance are a **contract liability**
    (ASC 606-10-45-2). The 02/15 wire collected the advance; collection does
    not create revenue.
  - No term is stated, so no ratable release can be computed without
    inventing facts (policy #1 needs a subscription term; a $0 recognized
    release is the only figure that comes from the documents).
- **Client reply received this session** (J. Alvarado, on the delivery and
  pricing questions): *"Honestly nothing bespoke — they self-onboarded like
  everyone else. Procurement just wanted it called a license."* Effect:
  - Delivery/pricing questions are **answered**: no bespoke, distinct
    implementation service was ever delivered. Policy #2 is explicit that
    **self-serve onboarding is not an implementation service**, so the
    implementation path is now closed by documented fact, not merely absent
    evidence.
  - The "license" wording is cosmetic ("procurement just wanted it called a
    license"). Under ASC 606 the substance governs — a change in description
    does not change the accounting (606-10-25-10 et seq.). The single
    performance obligation is **continuous platform access**, a series
    satisfied **over time** (606-10-25-14(b), 25-27).
  - **Still open:** the contract term (F1) and MSA v3.2 (F4) — the client
    provided no additional information. An over-time performance obligation
    with an unknown term has no computable release; recognizing any amount
    (e.g. guessing a 12- or 36-month life) would invent facts. The $36,000
    therefore **remains fully deferred and Pinecone contributes $0.00 to
    February revenue — the reply confirms rather than changes the treatment.**
  - Carried as standing unknown **U10** (below); not re-asked in later months
    per the client's protocol.
