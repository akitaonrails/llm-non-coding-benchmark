# 2026 individual rates used in this engagement

*Citation:* IRC §1(h), §1(j); §1411  
*Status in this library:* in force

For this engagement use: ordinary income marginal rate **37%**; long-term capital gain **20%**; the 3.8% net investment income tax does **not** apply to gain from an active trade or business in which the taxpayer materially participates. Texas has no individual income tax.
