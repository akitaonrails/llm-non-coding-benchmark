**Re: Granite Hollow Properties, LLC — rejection of the October 1, 2026 offer; objection to the purported capital call, Dilution Event and recomputation to 6.1%; demand for books and records; notice information update**

Dear Mr. Castellan:

This firm represents Imani Reyes-Okafor, a Member of Granite Hollow Properties, LLC (the "Company"). Sections quoted are from the Company's Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "Agreement").

**1. The October 1, 2026 offer is rejected.** The Company's offer to purchase Ms. Reyes-Okafor's interest for $189,100 is rejected, and no acceptance — express, conditional, partial or by conduct — has been or will be made. The offer stated it was open for 30 days; this writing is delivered within that period so that the record is unambiguous.

**2. No valid Dilution Event occurred, and no § 9.2 option exists.** Section 6.3 authorizes an additional capital call only "by written notice delivered to each Member **by certified mail and email** at least 30 days before the due date." The August 20, 2026 call for $1,500,000 was delivered by **neither** required method: no letter or certified mail was ever sent; the email address in the Company's records for Ms. Reyes-Okafor dates from 2019 and is no longer in service; the only communication was a text message with a PDF attachment. A call that does not comply with the Agreement's required delivery mechanics did not occur as a matter of the Agreement, and the September 25, 2026 "Dilution Event" therefore did not occur. **Ms. Reyes-Okafor remains an 18% Member.** Because § 9.2's purchase election arises only "following a Dilution Event," no such election is available to the Company.

**3. The recomputation is unexplained on its face.** The Manager's September 25, 2026 notice recomputed Ms. Reyes-Okafor's interest to 6.1% "based on a fair value of $7.2M determined by the Manager." That figure cannot be derived from $7,200,000 under any pro rata computation, and the Manager has produced no computation supporting either the $7,200,000 valuation or the 6.1% result. Section 6.3 conditions the recomputation on fair value "determined by the Manager **in good faith**"; a determination that cannot be reproduced from its own stated inputs is not such a determination. Ms. Reyes-Okafor reserves all rights as to this and as to the Manager's compliance with the covenant of good faith and fair dealing, which § 7.3 of the Agreement expressly preserves.

**4. No § 5.4 disclosure was ever made.** The Company has paid Castellan Asset Services LLC — an entity wholly owned by you — asset-management fees of $180,000, $195,000, $210,000, $225,000 and $240,000 on September 1 of 2022 through 2026 ($1,050,000 in total), plus a monthly fee of 6% of gross rents. Section 5.4 requires that any affiliate transaction involving payments over $50,000 per year be "disclosed in writing to all Members within 30 days," and requires that affiliate terms be "no less favorable to the Company than would be obtained in an arm's-length transaction with an unaffiliated third party." No such written disclosure has been received; Ms. Reyes-Okafor's year-end summaries described only "management expenses." All rights are reserved.

**5. Demand for books and records.** Within **fourteen (14) days**, produce the following, in full and unredacted:

1. The complete Agreement and every amendment, and all Member and Manager consents or waivers since 2021;
2. The Manager's computation of the $7,200,000 "fair value" and of the 6.1% Percentage Interest, including any workpapers, appraisal, or broker materials relied on;
3. A receipts-and-uses ledger for the $1,500,000 received in September 2026, and the books and records showing **how Ms. Reyes-Okafor's $270,000 share was recorded** — as her contribution, a loan to her, an advance, or otherwise — including any note, interest term, or offset agreement;
4. The year-end balance sheets for 2022–2025 (including the statement underlying the "$3.1M members' equity" figure used in the October 1 offer);
5. All agreements, invoices and payment records with Castellan Asset Services LLC, and every § 5.4 written disclosure allegedly made to Members;
6. Gross rents for 2022–2026 and proof of payment of the 6% property-management fee for each period;
7. The Company's records of each Member's notice addresses (mail, email, telephone) and its history of updates, and copies of every notice purportedly sent to Ms. Reyes-Okafor since 2021, with delivery confirmations.
