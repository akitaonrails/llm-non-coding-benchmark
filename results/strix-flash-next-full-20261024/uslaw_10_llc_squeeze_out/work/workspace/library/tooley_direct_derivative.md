# Direct vs derivative claims — Tooley; demand futility

*Citation:* *Tooley v. Donaldson, Lufkin & Jenrette*, 845 A.2d 1031 (Del. 2004); *United Food & Commercial Workers v. Zuckerberg*, 262 A.3d 1034 (Del. 2021); 6 Del. C. §18-1001 to 18-1003  
*Status in this library:* in force

A claim is derivative if the **company** suffered the harm and would receive the benefit of recovery (e.g., excessive fees paid to an affiliate); it is direct if the member suffered harm independent of the company (e.g., wrongful dilution of the member's own interest, or breach of a contractual right personal to the member). Derivative claims in an LLC require demand on the manager or pleaded futility (§18-1001; *Zuckerberg* test) — futility is readily pleaded where the sole manager is the interested party.
