## Resumed 2026-10-24 19:40 UTC (three-lane relaunch)

The first parallel launch did not survive: the Sol lane hit the ChatGPT usage
limit at 16:54 UTC (34 of 38 cases recorded as explicit infrastructure
failures, 4 completed), and the Strix and RTX 5090 harnesses were killed mid
first case with no retained result. Nothing was graded or inferred from those
partial directories.

This relaunch runs one lane per infrastructure, in parallel, and is supervised so
a dead process cannot silently end a lane:

- `scripts/run_lane.py` restarts the harness until every case holds a retained
  result, quarantines interrupted or transport-failed case directories under
  `<run>/_aborted/` with a reason in `<run>/retries.jsonl`, retries
  infrastructure failures up to three total attempts, and kills a harness that
  stops producing output for 3.5 h. Model outcomes (`completed_ungraded`,
  `candidate_output_limit`) are final and never retried.
- `scripts/run_local_baseline.py --resume` is the same harness as before; the
  flag only lets it continue an existing run directory and skip completed cases.
- `scripts/b300_sequence.py` waits for the Flash-Next lane, exports its pod
  evidence, drains the GPU, starts GLM 5.3 Flash from the pinned weights,
  re-runs admission, then supervises the GLM lane and stops the server.

Every lane is single-worker and the harness stops at each client-question round
and each scripted pushback/nudge decision until the controller answers
(`<stem>.pending.json` → `<stem>.decision.json`, one hour at most before the
round fails). `scripts/controller.py` is that console: it lists what is waiting,
prints the question text with the scripted facts and pushback conditions, and
writes the decision. The adjudicating agent records `controller` and `reason` on
every decision; no decision is generated automatically, so a slow controller is
visible in each case's files rather than hidden inside the harness.

and its replacement is a fresh, complete attempt. The Sol lane cannot resume
